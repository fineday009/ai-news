## 6. tailscale/tailcat

**是什么**：类似 netcat，但走 Tailscale 的数据平面，且不依赖 Tailscale 的控制平面。

**What it is**: "like netcat, but over Tailscale's data plane, without Tailscale's control plane."

**为什么值得关注**：netcat 是几十年来网络调试的瑞士军刀，tailcat 把它搬进 Tailscale 的加密数据平面，还刻意剥离了对中心控制平面的依赖——意味着更少的信任面和更灵活的点对点连接。Tailscale 官方出品，是本周非 AI 基础设施里最有"工程师趣味"的一个。

**Why it matters**: netcat has been the Swiss Army knife of network debugging for decades; tailcat moves it onto Tailscale's encrypted data plane while deliberately dropping the dependency on the central control plane — meaning less trust surface and more flexible peer-to-peer connectivity. An official Tailscale project, and the most "engineer's delight" piece of non-AI infrastructure this week.

**What it is**: "TimesFM (Time Series Foundation Model) is a pretrained time-series foundation model developed by Google Research for time-series forecasting."

**为什么值得关注**：语言和图像早已有"基础模型"，而时间序列长期还停留在一个数据集训一个模型。TimesFM 把"预训练 + 零样本预测"带进时序领域，一个模型就能对未见过的序列做预测。对做需求预测、金融、运维监控的团队，它可能大幅简化建模流程。

For teams doing demand forecasting, finance, or ops monitoring, it could sharply simplify the modeling pipeline.
