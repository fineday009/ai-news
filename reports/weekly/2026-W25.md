## 2. chopratejas/headroom

⭐ 27,710（本周 +10,653）· Python · [链接](https://github.com/chopratejas/headroom)

**是什么**：在工具输出、日志、文件和 RAG 片段抵达大模型之前，先对它们做压缩，可节省大量上下文。

**What it is**: Compress tool outputs, logs, files, and RAG chunks before they reach the LLM, saving a large share of context.

**为什么值得关注**：长上下文虽便宜了，但「塞太多无关内容」依然拖慢、拖贵、拖准。headroom 把上下文工程做成一个独立的前置层，对跑长链路 Agent、做 RAG 的团队是即插即用的省钱利器，与 last30days、Agent-Reach 这类「拉数据」的工具天然互补。

**Why it matters**: Long context got cheap, but stuffing in irrelevant material still makes runs slower, pricier, and less accurate. headroom turns context engineering into a standalone preprocessing layer—a plug-and-play cost saver for teams running long agent chains or RAG, and a natural complement to data-pulling tools like last30days and Agent-Reach.
