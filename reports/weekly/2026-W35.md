**Why it matters**: Fitting a trillion-scale MoE model into 8GB of RAM and running it in dependency-free C99 is an extreme engineering feat (MoE + MXFP4 quantization + SIMD). It proves "huge model ≠ huge hardware required," with high value for both edge inference and teaching.
