## 8. Zagitta/gpu-unlock

⭐ 7.2k · C · 创建于 2026-10-18 · [链接](https://github.com/Zagitta/gpu-unlock)

**是什么**：把 NVIDIA RTX 30 系显卡的 FP16/BF16 指令发射速率解锁到对应专业卡水平的 Linux 内核模块。

**What it is**: Unlock FP/BF16 instruction issue rate on Nvidia 3000's cards to their professional equivalents—a Linux kernel module.

**为什么值得关注**：在官方驱动加载前完成解锁，驱动更新后无需重新打补丁。作者实测 3090 Ti 的 BF16 稠密 GEMM 从 87 涨到 157 TFLOPS，用户报告多款 30 系卡有 +25% 到 +80% 的提升；几天后社区又出了面向 40 系的衍生项目。会改动硬件底层状态，使用前请看清兼容性和风险。

**Why it matters**: It runs the unlock before the official driver loads, so driver updates don't require re-patching. The author measured BF16 dense GEMM on a 3090 Ti going from 87 to 157 TFLOPS, users report +25% to +80% across several 30-series cards, and within days the community spun off a 40-series variant. It changes low-level hardware state, so check compatibility and risks before using it.
