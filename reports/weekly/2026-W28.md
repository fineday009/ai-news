## 6. google-research/tabfm

⭐ 1.7k · 新建于 06-16 · Python · [链接](https://github.com/google-research/tabfm)

**是什么**：谷歌研究院出品、兼容 scikit-learn 的表格基础模型，可对混合列类型的表格数据做零样本分类与回归。

**What it is**: A scikit-learn-compatible tabular foundation model from Google Research, doing zero-shot classification and regression on mixed-column-type tabular data.

**为什么值得关注**：表格是企业里最普遍的数据形态，而它无需在你的数据集上训练参数，靠上下文学习（in-context learning）直接预测，同时支持 JAX 与 PyTorch。「表格版基础模型」正在成为一个热门方向，谷歌的实现兼容 sklearn，落地门槛很低。

**Why it matters**: Tables are the most common data shape in the enterprise, and this model needs no parameter training on your dataset — it predicts via in-context learning, with both JAX and PyTorch backends. "Foundation models for tables" is a rising direction, and Google's sklearn-compatible implementation makes adoption nearly frictionless.
