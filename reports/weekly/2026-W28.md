## 5. baidu/Unlimited-OCR

**是什么**：面向「一次性长文档解析」的 OCR 模型——号称迎来识别超长文档的时代。

**What it is**: Unlimited OCR — one-shot, long-horizon document parsing, aimed at reading very long documents in a single pass.

**为什么值得关注**：文档 OCR 长期受限于分页、拼接与上下文丢失，百度这套主打「长程一次解析」正面解决该问题。与 olmocr 形成有趣对照：一个偏数据管线、一个偏端到端识别。

**Why it matters**: Document OCR has long been hobbled by pagination, stitching, and lost context; Baidu's "long-horizon one-shot" approach attacks that head-on. An interesting contrast to olmocr — one leans data-pipeline, the other end-to-end recognition.
