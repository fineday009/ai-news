**是什么**：隐私优先的 AI 会议助手，基于 Rust，用 Parakeet/Whisper 实现 4 倍速实时转录、说话人分离，并用 Ollama 本地总结，100% 本地处理。

**Why it matters**: Meeting-notes tools usually ship audio to the cloud, a privacy hazard. meetily runs transcription, diarization, and summarization entirely locally, and uses Rust for low-latency real-time. For law, healthcare, and finance where meeting content can't leave the building, it's a rare fully-local option.
