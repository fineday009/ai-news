**是什么**：用纯 C、零依赖，在一台 25GB 内存的消费级机器上跑 GLM-5.2（744B MoE）——专家权重从磁盘按需流式加载。引擎极小，模型极大。

**为什么值得关注**：它把"超大 MoE 模型只能在数据中心跑"的常识撕开一道口子。通过磁盘流式加载专家，普通台式机也能跑起 744B 级别的模型。这是本周"本地优先"叙事里技术含量最高、最具冲击力的一枪。

**Why it matters**: It cracks the assumption that giant MoE models only run in data centers. By streaming experts from disk, an ordinary desktop can run a 744B-class model. The most technically striking shot in this week's local-first narrative.

## 8. HUANGCHIHHUNGLeo/claude-real-video

**是什么**：让 Claude（或任意 LLM）真正"看"视频——从 URL 或本地文件提取场景感知、去重后的关键帧 + 转录文本，全程本地运行，MIT 协议。

**What it is**: Let Claude (or any LLM) actually watch a video — scene-aware, deduplicated frames + transcript, from a URL or local file. Runs locally, MIT.

**为什么值得关注**：多数"多模态"其实是把视频硬塞给模型，成本高、噪声大。claude-real-video 用场景检测 + 关键帧去重先做智能预处理，让文本 LLM 也能高效理解长视频。这种"用工程弥补模型短板"的思路既省钱又实用。

**Why it matters**: Most "multimodal" pipelines just dump video at the model — costly and noisy. claude-real-video does smart preprocessing with scene detection and deduplicated keyframes so even a text LLM can efficiently understand long videos. This "engineer around the model's weakness" approach is both cheap and practical.
