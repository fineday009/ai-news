**Why it matters**: Rather than embedding an LLM, it leverages the agent you already use — claiming a 3-minute index of the Linux kernel, sub-millisecond queries, and 99% fewer tokens — with Hybrid LSP type resolution, Cypher-like graph queries, and 3D visualization. **Why it matters**: It positions itself as an abstraction layer, not a point-in-time scraper — when a platform changes access rules (say Bilibili blocking yt-dlp), it auto-routes to a fallback backend, folding the long-term chore of "keeping internet access working" into one package.
