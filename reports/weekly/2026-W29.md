## 3. JustVugg/colibri

⭐ 16.1k（本周 +16.1k）· C · [链接](https://github.com/JustVugg/colibri)

**是什么**：在一台 25GB 内存的消费级机器上运行 GLM-5.2（744B MoE）——纯 C、零依赖，专家权重从磁盘流式加载。小引擎，跑巨大模型。

**What it is**: Run GLM-5.2 (744B MoE) on a 25GB-RAM consumer machine — pure C, zero deps, experts streamed from disk. Tiny engine, immense model.

**为什么值得关注**：MoE 的「稀疏激活」特性被这个项目用到极致——不是把 744B 全塞进内存，而是按需从磁盘流式加载专家。纯 C、零依赖意味着极强的可移植性，是「让前沿规模模型跑在普通电脑上」这条路线里工程最硬核的一个。

**Why it matters**: It pushes MoE's sparse-activation property to the limit — rather than loading all 744B into RAM, it streams experts from disk on demand. Pure C and zero dependencies mean extreme portability, making it one of the most hardcore engineering efforts in the "run frontier-scale models on an ordinary computer" line.
