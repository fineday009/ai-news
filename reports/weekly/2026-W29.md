## 1. JustVugg/colibri

⭐ 15.7k（本周期新建 +15.7k）· C · [链接](https://github.com/JustVugg/colibri)

**是什么**：在一台 25GB 内存的消费级机器上运行 GLM-5.2（744B MoE）——纯 C 实现、零依赖，专家参数从磁盘流式加载。引擎极小，模型极大。🐦

**What it is**: Run GLM-5.2 (744B MoE) on a 25GB-RAM consumer machine — pure C, zero deps, experts streamed from disk. Tiny engine, immense model. 🐦

**为什么值得关注**：本周最硬的工程。把近万亿参数的 MoE 通过磁盘流式专家加载塞进普通内存，直接改写了「本地能跑多大模型」的上限。纯 C、零依赖意味着可移植性和可审计性极佳，是开源本地推理的一次跳变。

**Why it matters**: The hardest piece of engineering this week. Streaming experts from disk to fit a near-trillion-parameter MoE into ordinary RAM rewrites the ceiling on "how big a model you can run locally." Pure C with zero deps means excellent portability and auditability — a jump for open-source local inference.
