## 5. CopilotKit/OpenBot

⭐ 4.5k · TypeScript · 8/17 创建 · [链接](https://github.com/CopilotKit/OpenBot)

**是什么**：开源的 AI「同事」——每个 Agent 都拥有自己的一台电脑（浏览器、文件、工具），每个动作在执行前先决策、执行后再记录，可接入任意 AG-UI 兼容的 Agent。

**What it is**: Open-source AI coworkers that each get a computer of their own (a browser, files, and tools), with every action decided before it happens and recorded after; bring any AG-UI agent.

**为什么值得关注**：出自 CopilotKit，切的是「Agent 治理」这个企业化痛点——不是让 Agent 黑箱乱跑，而是给每个动作加上「先审后行、事后留痕」的护栏。对想把 Agent 真正搬进生产环境的团队，这种可观测、可审计的设计比裸奔的自动化更有说服力。

**Why it matters**: From CopilotKit, it targets the enterprise pain of "agent governance"—rather than letting agents run as a black box, it adds "decide-before-act, record-after" guardrails to every action. For teams putting agents into production, this observable, auditable design is more convincing than naked automation.
