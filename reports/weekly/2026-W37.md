**What it is**: TimesFM (Time Series Foundation Model) is a pretrained time-series foundation model.

**为什么值得关注**：基础模型的浪潮从文本、图像蔓延到了时间序列。TimesFM 由 Google Research 出品，用「零样本预测」的思路做时序——不必为每个数据集单独训练，就能对需求、流量、金融等序列直接给出预测。对做预测的团队，这是把「大模型范式」迁到时序领域的权威参考。

**Why it matters**: The foundation-model wave has spread from text and images to time series. For forecasting teams, it's the authoritative reference for bringing the "big model paradigm" to time series.
