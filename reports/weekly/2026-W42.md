## 1. NandhaKishorM/laya

⭐ 32.4k · Python · [链接](https://github.com/NandhaKishorM/laya)

**是什么**：一个非自回归的"System 1"决策引擎，一次前向传播即可完成分类、打分、是非判断，支持 100+ 语言，并有路由器按请求挑选合适的 checkpoint。

**What it is**: A non-autoregressive System 1 decision engine — typed choice, score and yes/no decisions over any text in a single forward pass, in 100+ languages, with a router that picks the right checkpoint per request.

**为什么值得关注**：当大家都在用大模型"生成"来做本该一步到位的判断时，Laya 回归"判别式、单次前向"的高效老路，延迟和成本都大幅下降；它代表了"用对的小模型做对的小决策"这股正在成形的潮流（衍生出 kev、laya-mlx 等生态）。

**Why it matters**: While everyone bends generative LLMs to do decisions that should take one step, Laya returns to an efficient discriminative, single-forward-pass path that slashes latency and cost — emblematic of a forming trend ("use the right small model for the right small decision," spawning kev, laya-mlx and more).
