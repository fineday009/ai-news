## 2. NandhaKishorM/laya

⭐ 32.6k · 2026-09-18 新建 · Python · [链接](https://github.com/NandhaKishorM/laya)

**是什么**：一个非自回归的"System 1 决策引擎"——在单次前向中对任意文本做类型化的选择、打分与是非判断，支持 100+ 语言，并带一个按请求挑选合适检查点的路由器。

**What it is**: A non-autoregressive "System 1" decision engine — typed choice, score and yes/no decisions over any text in a single forward pass, in 100+ languages, with a router that picks the right checkpoint per request.

**为什么值得关注**：它代表了"决策模型"这一新范式：不生成文本，只在一次前向里给出结构化判断，因而又快又便宜，特别适合路由、分类、过滤、Agent 中的大量小决策。相比用大模型做每个判断，它的延迟与成本优势是数量级的。

**Why it matters**: It exemplifies the emerging "decision model" paradigm — no text generation, just a structured verdict in one forward pass, which is fast and cheap, ideal for routing, classification, filtering and the many small decisions inside agents. Versus calling a big model for each judgment, its latency and cost advantages are an order of magnitude.
