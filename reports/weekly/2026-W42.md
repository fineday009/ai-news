## 2. NandhaKishorM/laya

⭐ 33.1k（近一周高增长新项，建于 2026-09-18）· Python · [链接](https://github.com/NandhaKishorM/laya)

**是什么**：非自回归的「系统 1」决策引擎，在一次前向传播里对任意文本做分类、打分和是/否判断，支持 100+ 语言，并带一个按请求挑选合适检查点的路由器。

**What it is**: A non-autoregressive "System 1" decision engine that does typed choice, scoring, and yes/no decisions over any text in a single forward pass, across 100+ languages, with a router that picks the right checkpoint per request.

**为什么值得关注**：当大家都在堆生成式大模型时，laya 反向押注「不用生成、一次前向就出决策」。对分类、路由、过滤这类高频低延迟任务，它可能比调用一个大 LLM 便宜几个数量级，是一个被低估的方向。

**Why it matters**: While everyone piles onto generative LLMs, laya bets the other way — no generation, one forward pass to a decision. For high-frequency, low-latency tasks like classification, routing, and filtering, it can be orders of magnitude cheaper than calling a large LLM, an underrated direction.
