## 2. NandhaKishorM/laya

⭐ 32.8k · 2026-09-18 新建 · Python · [链接](https://github.com/NandhaKishorM/laya)

**是什么**：一个非自回归的"系统 1"决策引擎——对任意文本在单次前向传播内完成分类、打分、是否判断等"类型化决策"，支持 100 多种语言，并带一个按请求自动挑选合适 checkpoint 的路由器。

**What it is**: A non-autoregressive "System 1" decision engine that performs typed choice, scoring and yes/no decisions over any text in a single forward pass, across 100+ languages, with a router that picks the right checkpoint per request.

**为什么值得关注**：当多数应用无脑调用生成式大模型时，laya 反其道而行，用极快、极省的判别式路径处理"分类/判断"这类不需要生成的任务。对要在高吞吐、低延迟场景做意图识别、内容审核、路由决策的团队，这是一条被低估的高性价比技术路线。

**Why it matters**: While most apps reflexively call generative LLMs, laya goes the other way — a blazing-fast, cheap discriminative path for "classify/judge" tasks that don't need generation. For teams doing intent detection, moderation or routing decisions at high throughput and low latency, it's an underrated, cost-effective approach.
