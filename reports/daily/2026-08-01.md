## 1. andrewyng/openworker

⭐ 11.4k · Python/TypeScript · [链接](https://github.com/andrewyng/openworker)

**是什么**：一款开源的桌面 AI「同事」，目标是交付**成品**而不是聊天——一份排好版的文档、带数据的 Slack 回复、更新好的日历、整理过的收件箱。本地运行、自带审批闸门，接入 Slack、GitHub、Jira、Notion、Gmail 等 25+ 工具，支持 OpenAI/Anthropic/Google/Ollama 多家模型。（注：仓库归属用户名为 andrewyng，但并非 Andrew Ng 本人或其机构的官方项目。）

**What it is**: An open-source desktop AI "coworker" that delivers **finished work** rather than chat — a polished document, a Slack reply with numbers, an updated calendar, a triaged inbox. Runs locally with approval gates, integrates 25+ tools (Slack, GitHub, Jira, Notion, Gmail), and supports OpenAI/Anthropic/Google/Ollama. (Note: the repo is under the username "andrewyng" but is not an official project of Andrew Ng or his organizations.)

**为什么值得关注**：把「Agent 帮我干活」从演示推进到日常桌面工具，强调可交付成果 + 本地数据 + 审批安全，短短十来天冲上 11k star，代表了「个人 AI 助理产品化」的最新一波。

**Why it matters**: It pushes "let an agent do my work" from demo to daily desktop tool, emphasizing deliverables + local data + approval safety. Climbing to 11k stars in ~two weeks, it captures the latest wave of productized personal AI assistants.

## 3. yc-software/qm

⭐ 2.2k · TypeScript · [链接](https://github.com/yc-software/qm)

**是什么**：面向创业公司的「多人 Agent harness」。为团队每个人提供隔离的工作空间（各自的记忆、文件、权限、沙箱），同时支持共享协作；Slack 与 Web 双界面、身份同步，Agent 以「它所服务的人」的凭证与权限行事。可自托管，支持 Pi、OpenCode、Codex、Claude Code 多套 harness。

**What it is**: A multiplayer agent harness for startups. It gives each person an isolated workspace (scoped memory, files, permissions, sandbox) while enabling shared collaboration; dual Slack + web UI with synced identity, and the agent acts with the credentials and permissions of the person it works for. Self-hostable, supporting Pi, OpenCode, Codex and Claude Code.

**为什么值得关注**：个人 Agent 一旦推广到整个组织就会出现权限、记忆、安全的混乱，qm 用「按人隔离 + 管理员统管」的架构直击这个企业级痛点，是 Agent 从单人玩具走向组织基建的代表。

**Why it matters**: Personal agents get messy at org scale (permissions, memory, security). qm tackles that enterprise pain with a per-person isolation + admin-governed architecture — a good example of agents growing from single-user toys into org infrastructure.

### 播客 / Podcast

**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck ——《The Biggest AI Deployment Nobody Talks About | Samsara CEO Sanjit Biswas》**

嘉宾是 $200 亿市值公司 Samsara 的联合创始人兼 CEO Sanjit Biswas，主题是「物理 AI（Physical AI）」。要点：

Guest: Sanjit Biswas, co-founder and CEO of the $20B company Samsara, on "physical AI." Key points:

- **物理 AI 就是把 AI 用到没有被数字化的真实世界**（道路、工地、电网、地下管网）；难点在于这里没有可爬取的数据——「你没法靠爬 Reddit 知道工地上发生了什么」。Samsara 每天覆盖 99% 的美国道路、一年 25 万亿数据点。
  Physical AI means applying AI to the non-digitized real world (roads, construction sites, the grid, underground utilities); the hard part is there's no crawlable data — "you can't crawl Reddit and find out what happened on a construction site." Samsara covers 99% of US roads daily and 25 trillion data points a year.

- **安全成效实打实**：过去一年跨数百万车辆，帮助预防了约 38 万起道路事故。
  Real safety impact: across millions of vehicles over the past year, it helped prevent roughly 380,000 road accidents.

- **模型策略是「全都要」**：同时使用多家前沿实验室的模型，拥抱开放权重模型（蒸馏后塞进设备端），并从零训练几千万参数级的、专门解决现场问题的小模型。
  Model strategy is "all of the above": use multiple frontier-lab models simultaneously, embrace open-weight models (distilled to fit on-device), and train tiny (~tens of millions of parameters) task-specific models from scratch.

- **在六月的 Beyond 2026 大会上发布 Agent Studio**，对应「连接运营 → 理解运营 → 采取行动」的递进路线。
  At the Beyond 2026 conference in June it launched Agent Studio, matching a progression of "connect operations → understand them → take action."

- **落地靠 ROI 而非「因为是 AI」**：客户只在亲眼看到能解决减少事故、找回遗落工具等具体问题时才买单；赢得一线员工信任的关键是透明与隐私（摄像头可见而非隐藏，出发点是「帮你平安回家」）。
  Adoption is driven by ROI, not "because it's AI": customers buy only after seeing it solve concrete problems (fewer accidents, recovering left-behind tools); winning frontline trust hinges on transparency and privacy (cameras are visible, not hidden, framed as "helping you go home safe"). <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3FHsGiONOGw>
