## 1. Pika-Labs/Pika-H3-Flex

⭐ 1,933 · Python · 创建于 2026-10-29 · [链接](https://github.com/Pika-Labs/Pika-H3-Flex)

**是什么**：Pika H3 Flex 的视频与音频生成推理代码：基于 MiniMax-H3 后训练的开放权重模型，六步采样生成带同步立体声的视频，最多支持 12 个参考输入。

**What it is**: Pika H3 Flex: video and audio generation inference. A post-trained H3 model that generates video with synchronized audio in six sampling steps and supports up to 12 reference inputs, with shot direction through references and prompts.

**为什么值得关注**：商业视频生成公司开放权重并不多见。H3 Flex 包含两个适配器：FL2VA 负责文生视频与首/尾帧控制，Ref2VA 接收最多 9 张图、3 段视频、3 段音频作为参考；本地可生成 480p/768p、5–15 秒、24 fps、32 kHz 立体声的 MP4，覆盖 11 种画幅。门槛也写得很清楚：单请求单卡，测试基于 H100 80GB，两个任务的基础文件约 210 GB；1080p 到 4K、分轨音频则走 Pika 托管 API。许可证为 MiniMax H3 社区许可。适合需要可控多镜头叙事、动态图形和产品短片的团队做本地实验。

**Why it matters**: Commercial video-generation companies rarely open their weights. H3 Flex ships two adapters—FL2VA for text-to-video and first/last-frame conditioning, and Ref2VA for up to 9 images, 3 videos and 3 audio clips as references—and locally renders 480p/768p, 5–15 s, 24 fps MP4s with 32 kHz stereo across 11 aspect ratios. The bar is stated plainly: one request per GPU, tested on an H100 80GB, with roughly 210 GB of base files for both tasks; 1080p to 4K and audio stems go through Pika's hosted API. It's licensed under the MiniMax H3 Community License. A good fit for teams experimenting locally with controllable multi-shot storytelling, motion graphics and product films.

## 3. lz4/lz4

⭐ 12,406（今日 +23）· C · [链接](https://github.com/lz4/lz4)

**是什么**：极速无损压缩算法 LZ4 的参考实现，11 月 4 日发布 v1.11.0。

**What it is**: Extremely Fast Compression algorithm—the reference LZ4 implementation, which shipped v1.11.0 on November 4.

**为什么值得关注**：v1.11.0 是一次大版本更新，合入自 v1.10.0 以来的 850 多个提交。头号特性是多线程压缩进入 liblz4 本身：只需设置 `LZ4F_preferences_t.nbWorkers` 一个字段即可多线程压缩，速度近乎线性扩展（官方测试中 level 1 从单线程 522 MB/s 到 16 线程 7,550 MB/s），且输出与线程数无关、可复现。小数据与字典场景也大幅提速：4 KB 分块下复用状态压缩快 46%，带字典的 lz4frame 解压快 88%（gcc 11）。数据库页、缓存条目、网络消息、日志这类"小块压缩"负载都能直接受益，且保持二进制兼容，官方建议升级。

**Why it matters**: v1.11.0 is a major update integrating 850+ commits since v1.10.0. The headline is multithreaded compression inside liblz4 itself: setting one field, `LZ4F_preferences_t.nbWorkers`, enables it, and speed scales almost linearly (level 1 goes from 522 MB/s single-threaded to 7,550 MB/s with 16 workers in the official benchmark), with output independent of thread count and reproducible. Small data and dictionaries got much faster too: with 4 KB blocks, compression with a reused state is 46% faster and dictionary decompression via lz4frame is 88% faster (gcc 11). Small-block workloads such as database pages, cache entries, network messages and logs benefit directly, binary compatibility is retained, and upgrading is recommended.

## 7. unclebob/ub-mode

⭐ 7,120 · 创建于 2026-10-25 · [链接](https://github.com/unclebob/ub-mode)

**是什么**：《代码整洁之道》作者 Uncle Bob 出品的 Agent skill，在 Agent 写代码或改代码时强制执行严谨的软件工程原则。

**What it is**: An agent skill that enforces disciplined software engineering principles when writing or changing code, loosely based on portions of @poteto's rules and paired with deterministic quality-gate tools.

**为什么值得关注**：多数工程类 skill 只是写给模型的建议，ub-mode 则把建议和确定性的质量门禁绑在一起：配套的 crapper 要求每个函数 CRAP 分数低于 10，dryer 检测相似度 ≥0.8 的结构性重复，mutator 做变异测试、揪出没被测到的代码。流程分"先跑通（TDD、领域建模、幂等）→ 再做对（根因修复、测行为而非实现、重构）→ 日常实践（记日志、保护上下文窗口、小步提交）"三段。想让 Agent 写出可维护代码的团队可以直接拿来当门禁。

**Why it matters**: Most engineering skills are advice for the model; ub-mode binds the advice to deterministic quality gates. Companion tools enforce it: crapper keeps every function's CRAP score under 10, dryer flags structural duplication at ≥0.8 similarity, and mutator runs mutation testing to expose untested code. The flow is three stages—make it work (TDD, domain modeling, idempotency), then make it right (root-cause fixes, test behavior not implementation, refactoring), then practice (keep a log, guard the context window, small ordered commits). Teams who want agents to write maintainable code can adopt it as a gate as-is.

- **150 人的口述史**：书基于对约 150 人的采访，其中核心约 50 人；多数以"背景信息"方式进行，最难的是前 25 人，之后开始滚雪球——有人甚至因为"讨厌的人已经跟你讲了"而主动来讲自己的版本。秘诀是真诚和功课：采访前先读完对方的论文。
- **最爱的史料**：Dario Amodei 2017 年在 OpenAI 任研究副总裁时写的内部备忘录"big blob of compute hypothesis"——主张别再训练窄模型，而是把大规模算力对准"预测下一个词"这类通用问题；Roose 把前 10 页发在了自己的 Substack 上。另一本是深刻影响 Dario 的《原子弹秘史》（The Making of the Atomic Bomb）。
- **监管怎么走**：他认为当前政府不会出台联邦 AI 监管，第一步应是行业标准——像药企上市前测药一样，模型发布前必须过安全测试；他近几个月也开始相信，前沿实验室之间可能形成放缓节奏的协调机制。
- **"Claude 议会"审稿**：书一个字都没让 AI 写，但每章初稿会交给他在 Claude Code 里搭的多 Agent 小组——AI 怀疑论者、AI 历史学家、讲究叙事节奏的匠人等角色分别点评，再由"总编辑" Agent 汇总；它抓到过人类编辑都没发现的事实错误。"几年前我们要给 AI 做事实核查，现在是 AI 给我做事实核查。"研究资料则全靠 NotebookLM 检索和串联。
- **新节目**：他和 Casey Newton 与 NPR 合作推出 Machine Gods，每周两期，把 AI 当作"世界上最重要的新闻"来报道。

Former New York Times tech reporter Kevin Roose, now host of the new podcast Machine Gods, talks about his book chronicling a decade inside the AI labs. Bottom line: frontier AI carries real risks, but nothing is predestined, and we can navigate it.

- **An oral history of 150 people**: the book draws on about 150 interviews, roughly 50 of them core figures, mostly on background. The first 25 were the hardest; then it snowballed—some even came forward because someone they disliked had already told their side. The key was sincerity and homework: reading people's papers before talking to them.
- **Favorite sources**: Dario Amodei's 2017 internal memo at OpenAI, the "big blob of compute hypothesis," written when he was VP of research—arguing to stop training narrow models and instead point large masses of compute at general problems like predicting the next word; Roose published its first 10 pages on his Substack. Another is The Making of the Atomic Bomb, which deeply shaped Dario.
- **The path to regulation**: he doesn't expect federal AI regulation from the current administration; the first step should be an industry standard—just as drugmakers test before selling, AI companies should safety-test models before release. Over recent months he's also come to believe a coordination mechanism for slowing the frontier is possible.
- **A "council of Claudes" as reviewers**: AI wrote none of the book, but each draft chapter went to a multi-agent setup in Claude Code—an AI skeptic, an AI historian, a narrative-craft persona and others—with a master editor agent synthesizing; it caught factual errors his human editors missed. "A couple years ago we had to fact-check the AIs, and now the AIs fact-check me." NotebookLM handled searching and connecting his research.
- **The new show**: Machine Gods, with Casey Newton and in partnership with NPR, publishes two episodes a week and treats AI as the most important story in the world.
