## 7. n0-computer/iroh

⭐ 11.1k（本月 +2,398）· Rust · [链接](https://github.com/n0-computer/iroh)

**是什么**：一个 Rust 网络库——「IP 会变，改拨密钥吧」，为应用加上 QUIC + NAT 穿透，让服务用公钥而非 IP 相互直连。

**What it is**: A Rust networking library — "IP addresses break, dial keys instead" — that adds QUIC + NAT traversal so services dial each other by key rather than by IP.

**为什么值得关注**：P2P/直连一直难在 NAT 穿透与连接稳定性。iroh 把这套底层能力封装成开箱即用的库，用密钥寻址替代脆弱的 IP，是构建去中心化、本地优先应用时少数真正好用的网络基座之一。

**Why it matters**: P2P and direct connectivity have always struggled with NAT traversal and connection stability. Iroh packages this plumbing into an out-of-the-box library, replacing brittle IP addressing with key-based dialing — one of the few genuinely usable networking foundations for building decentralized, local-first apps.

### 播客 / Podcast

**No Priors — 与 Valar Atomics 创始人 Isaiah Taylor 聊核能如何解锁能源丰饶**（[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Xvbq_zvOQ4)）

**No Priors — How Nuclear Will Unlock Energy Abundance with Valar Atomics Founder Isaiah Taylor** ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Xvbq_zvOQ4))

一句话要点：核能从没有过自己的「福特时刻或特斯拉时刻」，而在 AI 算力狂拉电力需求、监管终于松动的当下，用「制造而非建造」的方式把能源做便宜 10 倍，可能才刚刚开始。

The takeaway: nuclear has never had its "Ford moment or Tesla moment," and with AI compute driving power demand and regulation finally loosening, making energy 10× cheaper by manufacturing (not constructing) reactors may only just be starting.

- Valar 刚在犹他把一台先进 Triso 反应堆点亮——是美国 50 多年来首台、也是首次由创业公司做到，并用它给一块 AI 芯片供电；这是在 Trump 的 EO 14301（要求三台先进反应堆在 7 月 4 日前临界）下、走 DOE 授权建成的。
  Valar just brought an advanced Triso reactor critical in Utah — the first in the US in 50+ years and the first ever by a startup — using it to power an AI chip; built under DOE authority per Trump's EO 14301 (three advanced reactors critical by July 4).

- Taylor 认为核能行业的病根不在物理，而在它变成了「建模仿真行业」，只产出精美的「纸上反应堆」；Valar 的差异化是学 SpaceX 走硬件迭代——真的把反应堆一台台点亮。
  Taylor argues the industry's disease isn't physics but that it became "a modeling-and-simulation industry" producing pretty "paper reactors"; Valar's edge is SpaceX-style hardware iteration — actually turning reactors on, one at a time.

- 美国在三里岛事故后停建反应堆（无人死亡、无人受伤、公众无辐射剂量，是一场公关与优化的失败），且大型土木施工能力已退化；未来核能应是「被制造的」小型模块化反应堆（SMR），而非「被建造的」巨型工程。
  The US stopped building after Three Mile Island (no deaths, no injuries, no public radiation dose — a PR and management failure), and its large-scale civil-construction skill atrophied; future nuclear should be "manufactured" small modular reactors, not "constructed" mega-projects.

- 监管突破口：DOE 前身是从原子能委员会拆出的 ERDA，本就有一条与 NRC「商业部署」并行、却沉睡了四十年的「测试」路径；Valar 用它破解了「要数据才能过审、要过审才有数据」的死循环。
  The regulatory unlock: the DOE (formerly ERDA, spun out of the Atomic Energy Commission) has long had a "testing" pathway parallel to the NRC's "commercial deployment" one, dormant for forty years; Valar used it to break the "need data to get approved, need approval to get data" chicken-and-egg.

- 核心信念：能源是商品，需求由价格决定——「如果你能想办法把能源做便宜，你就会有需求」；把反应堆量产、让能源便宜 10 倍，AI 算力驱动的需求几乎无上限。
  Core belief: energy is a commodity where demand is set by price — "If you can figure out how to make energy cheaper, you will have demand"; mass-produce reactors, make energy 10× cheaper, and AI-compute-driven demand is effectively unlimited.
