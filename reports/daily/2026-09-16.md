### 播客要点 / Podcast

**《How a Professional Writer Writes With AI》— AI & I by Every**

嘉宾是 Every 的专职作者 Katie Parrott，由 Natalia Quintero 主持，借新插件「Compound Writing」聊「和 AI 一起写作」。核心要点：

The guest is Katie Parrott, staff writer at Every, interviewed by Natalia Quintero, using the new "Compound Writing" plugin as a lens on writing with AI. Key takeaways:

- **决定质量的是「输入」，不是提示词**：模型本身知道的都是被商品化的通用信息，真正让文章独一无二的是你独有的数据、研究与亲身经历——「模型是厨房，但食材得新鲜」。

- **What sets quality apart is your inputs, not the prompt**: the model only knows commoditized information; what makes writing unique is your proprietary data, research, and lived experience — "the model is the kitchen, but the ingredients have to be fresh."

- **把最佳实践「编码」下来**：Compound Writing（灵感来自 Compound Engineering）把风格指南、流程沉淀成可复用的结构，让你只需带来「脑子里独有的东西」。

- **Codify your best practices**: Compound Writing (inspired by Compound Engineering) turns style guides and process into reusable structure, so you only bring what's uniquely in your head.

- **AI 不只是生产力工具，更是「支撑性技术」**：Katie 患双相情感障碍，用 Agent 处理「电脑杂务」——让 Codex 帮她找到接收新病人的家庭医生（一件她拖了三年的事）、整理收件箱，极大降低了生活摩擦。原话：「我发现 AI 作为一种支撑性技术，和作为生产性技术一样强大。」

- **AI is a supportive technology, not just a productive one**: living with bipolar disorder, Katie uses agents for "computer errands" — she had Codex find a primary-care physician accepting new patients (something she'd put off for three years) and tame her inbox, sharply reducing daily friction. In her words: "AI is just as powerful as a supportive technology."

- **她担心价值分配不均**：AI 有巨大的乘数效应，但风险是价值只向「早期少数人」复利；更值得追求的未来，是「让每个人都能一起上车」。

- **She worries about unequal access**: AI has a huge multiplicative effect, but the risk is value compounding only to the early few — a better future is "one where everyone gets to come along for the ride."
