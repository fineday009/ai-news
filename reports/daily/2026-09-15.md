**Why it matters**: As content-platform APIs grow pricier and more closed, it unifies cross-platform fetching into one command with no API fees, filling a key gap in agents' access to live external information — and at 80k+ stars with steady daily growth it confirms that "getting agents online" is one of the most-wanted capabilities right now.

## 2. ruvnet/RuView

⭐ 93.9k（今日 +383）· Rust · [链接](https://github.com/ruvnet/RuView)

**是什么**：把 WiFi 无线电信号变成空间智能的开源感知平台——无需摄像头或穿戴设备，即可检测人体存在、测量呼吸与心率、估计姿态并监测活动，甚至能穿墙。

**What it is**: An open-source WiFi-sensing platform that turns radio signals into spatial intelligence — detecting presence, measuring breathing and heart rate, estimating pose and monitoring activity without cameras or wearables, even through walls.

**为什么值得关注**：用一枚 8–9 美元的 ESP32 就能做到过去需要专业设备的无接触感知；Rust 负责边缘处理、Python 训练 ML 模型、TypeScript/React 做可视化，并可接入 Home Assistant、Apple Home、Google Home 与 Alexa。物理级的射频感知叠加机器学习，隐私友好且成本极低，是本年度最具想象力的硬核项目之一。

**Why it matters**: With a single $8–9 ESP32 it delivers contactless sensing that once needed specialized hardware; Rust handles edge processing, Python trains the ML models, and TypeScript/React powers visualization, with hooks into Home Assistant, Apple Home, Google Home and Alexa. Physics-based RF sensing layered with machine learning, privacy-friendly and dirt cheap — one of the year's most imaginative hardcore projects.

**播客要点 / Podcast**

一句话要点：加速器负责「生产 token」，但真正让 AI 系统跑起来的是无处不在的 CPU——而 AI 用得最猛的地方，恰恰是芯片设计里最枯燥的验证环节。《No Priors》对话 Arm 兼软银国际 CEO Rene Haas，谈芯片、供应链与机器人的未来。

The takeaway: accelerators "make the tokens," but what actually makes AI systems run is the ubiquitous CPU — and AI's heaviest use is the most tedious part of chip design, verification. No Priors talks with Rene Haas, CEO of Arm and SoftBank Group International, on chips, supply chains and the future of robotics.

- 芯片设计要 24–36 个月，最耗时的不是设计本身，而是验证、校验与调试——而 AI 恰好极擅长这些。Arm 有 80–90% 的工程师每天在用 AI，若关掉它「就像在 1990 年代只准每天两点到四点上网，之后去走廊尽头的图书馆——那会天下大乱」，Haas 说「精灵已经出瓶，无法阻挡」。

  Chip design takes 24–36 months, and the biggest time sink isn't the design but verification, validation and debug — exactly what AI is great at. 80–90% of Arm's engineers use it daily, and switching it off would be "like the 1990s where you can only use the internet from two to four, then go to the library down the hall — it'd be anarchy"; the genie, Haas says, is out of the bottle.

- 关于「AI 泡沫」：论供需，「远远没到」过剩——需求因模型本身的工作方式而近乎无限。瓶颈在层层转移：先是封装，再是内存，如今是数据中心的建设（劳动力与各地对建站的限制），未来五年内都将处于受限环境。

  On the "AI bubble": in supply-vs-demand terms, we're "not even close" to oversupply — demand is nearly insatiable given how the models work. The bottleneck keeps moving — first packaging, then memory, and now data-center buildout (labor and local restrictions on new sites) — a constrained environment for at least the next five years.

- Arm 正从纯 IP 授权（毛利率高达 98.5%）迈向自研实体芯片——首款 Arm AGI CPU 源于 Meta 想要一颗「通用智能体 CPU」而市面无人能供；Haas 预判五年以上，部分较直接的设计有望「从想法直接到可送厂的 GDS-II 文件」，大幅压缩研发周期。

  Arm is moving from pure IP licensing (a 98.5% gross margin) to building its own physical silicon — its first Arm AGI CPU came from Meta wanting a "general-purpose agentic CPU" nobody could supply; Haas predicts that in five-plus years some straightforward designs could go "from idea to a tape-out-ready GDS-II file," sharply compressing the cycle.

- CPU 被低估了：ChatGPT 爆发后焦点全在加速器上，但「有史以来没有任何计算问题用不上微处理器，它是一切的核心」；token 工厂不断生产 token，「得有卡车把 token 运走送到用户手里」，而做编排与仲裁的正是 CPU，机器人的「大脑」如今也大多跑在 Arm 上。

  The CPU is underrated: after the ChatGPT explosion all focus went to accelerators, but "there's no computing problem ever invented that can't use the microprocessor — it's the heart of everything"; the token factory keeps generating tokens, and "something has to be the trucks that take the tokens away to users" — that orchestration and arbitration is what CPUs do, and most robots' "brains" already run on Arm.
