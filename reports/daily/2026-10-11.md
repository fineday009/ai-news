## 1. openai/math

**是什么**：OpenAI 公开的一批数学手稿与证明"文物"，共 719 份手稿、372 个家族，由一个内部模型产出，用于评估模型在开放研究问题上的能力，其中约 42% 的核心结论配有 Lean 形式化验证。

**What it is**: A catalogue of mathematical manuscripts and proof artifacts produced by an internal OpenAI model — 719 manuscripts across 372 families, used to evaluate the model on open research problems, with roughly 42% of top-line results backed by Lean formalizations.

**为什么值得关注**：这是"AI 做数学研究"从演示走向可审计产物的一步——用 Lean 形式化把一部分结论钉死为可机器验证，同时坦诚标注未形式化部分可能有错。对想复核前沿模型推理能力的人来说，这是难得的一手材料。

**Why it matters**: It moves "AI doing math research" from demos toward auditable output: Lean formalization pins a portion of the results down as machine-checkable, while the README is candid that unformalized results may contain errors. For anyone wanting to scrutinize frontier reasoning claims, it's rare primary material.

### 播客 / Podcast

**No Priors — 《Beam: The Great American Open Model》对话 ReflectionAI 联合创始人兼 CEO Misha Laskin**

**No Priors — "Beam: The Great American Open Model" with ReflectionAI Co-Founder and CEO Misha Laskin**

- 核心观点：开源权重不只是理念，更是安全刚需——"当你移除网络攻击能力，你也移除了网络防御能力"；Laskin 把 Linus 定律搬到 AI 安全上："只要有足够多的眼睛，绝大多数安全与安全性漏洞都会变得浅显。"

  The takeaway: open weights aren't just ideology, they're a security necessity — "When you remove cyber offensive capabilities, you also remove cyber defensive capabilities." Laskin applies Linus's law to AI safety: "with enough eyeballs, most security and safety vulnerabilities become shallow."

- ReflectionAI 一年内从约 30 人扩到约 300 人，组齐 pre-training / mid-training / RL 全栈团队，端到端训练并发布了首个开源模型 Beam。

  ReflectionAI scaled from ~30 to ~300 people in a year, assembled full pre-training / mid-training / RL teams, and trained end-to-end to ship Beam, its first open model.

- 强化学习"不再触顶"：他们的 RL 系统曲线一直往上走，模型构建已从一门科学变成一道工程与经济题——"就是一个你往里投多少钱、换多少提升的问题"。

  RL no longer plateaus: their RL curves "just keep going up," and model building has shifted from a science to an engineering-and-economics question — "how much money do I put in to get some improvement out of it."

- Beam 专为编码与 agentic 任务训练，在同等能力级别里 token 效率高出 3–4 倍，对比更大的模型甚至可达 10 倍，直接转化为更快的完成时间和更低的成本。

  Beam is trained for coding and agentic tasks and is 3–4× more token-efficient than models of the same capability class (up to 10× versus larger models), translating directly into faster completion and lower cost.

- 开源的真正杠杆在于定制：企业可以拿开源模型按自己的工作负载微调，得到成本-性能帕累托最优；高价值垂直包括金融 KYC/合规、网络防御和法律。

  The real leverage of open models is customization: enterprises can fine-tune them for their own workloads to hit a cost/performance Pareto optimum, with high-value verticals in finance KYC/compliance, cyber defense, and legal.

https://www.youtube.com/@NoPriorsPodcast
