**是什么**：一个基于 RustVMM 与 KVM 的高性能沙箱服务，号称能在 60 毫秒内、以低于 5MB 的内存开销创建一个硬件隔离、可完整服务化的沙箱，用来安全执行不可信代码。

**Why it matters**: As agents increasingly generate and run code, "how to run untrusted code safely" becomes a hard requirement. It's more isolated than kernel-sharing Docker and faster than traditional VMs, and its E2B SDK compatibility lets existing setups migrate smoothly. A pragmatic step on the "agent execution substrate" infra line — from Tencent Cloud, written in Rust.

### 播客要点 / Podcast Notes

**Training Data — Why Hardware-Software Co-Design Is AI's Real 100x: Dylan Patel of SemiAnalysis**

**一句话要点**：AI 效率最大的跃升，不来自更快的芯片或更强的模型单打独斗，而来自把模型、软件、硬件三层一起协同设计——这正是「8 倍」变成「100 倍」的地方。

**The Takeaway**: The biggest leap in AI efficiency comes not from a faster chip or a better model alone, but from co-designing the model, software, and hardware layers together — that's where "8x" becomes "100x."

- **三层协同才是真正的 100x**：单看每一层的进步都是 2x 级别，但把它们从「本该相乘得 8x」变成真正的 100x，靠的是跨三层协同优化。Dylan 的原话：“instead of being multiplicative to 8x, it's actually 100x, because you've optimized across all three layers.”

- **The 100x is co-design, not any one layer**: Each layer alone yields ~2x, but turning "what should multiply to 8x" into an actual 100x comes from co-optimizing across all three. In his words: "instead of being multiplicative to 8x, it's actually 100x, because you've optimized across all three layers."

- **成本在飞速下降**：同等质量的模型成本每年下降约 60x，「智能/瓦」每年约 40x——背后是 PyTorch、vLLM、SGLang 这些库每周两次的更新节奏在不断压低成本。

- **Costs are plummeting**: Model cost for equivalent quality drops ~60x per year, and intelligence-per-watt ~40x per year — driven by the twice-a-week update cadence of libraries like PyTorch, vLLM, and SGLang relentlessly pushing cost down.

- **硬件并不通用**：DeepSeek 的专家结构是为 Hopper（再到 Blackwell、华为芯片）量身优化的；TPU 训练很强、也能跑好某些模型，但「跑 DeepSeek 很糟」。顶尖实验室会把模型架构与自家芯片深度协同。

- **Hardware isn't fungible**: DeepSeek's expert shapes were optimized for Hopper (then Blackwell and Huawei's chip); TPUs are great at training and some models but "suck at running DeepSeek." The best labs co-optimize model architecture to their silicon.

- **InferenceX 的公开 Pareto 曲线**：SemiAnalysis 每天在约 15 种芯片上跑最新模型的推理基准，公开吞吐/延迟/成本/功耗的 Pareto 最优曲线与容器，任何人都能下载并逼近峰值推理表现。

- **InferenceX's public Pareto curve**: SemiAnalysis benchmarks the latest models daily across ~15 chip types, publishing the Pareto-optimal throughput/latency/cost/power curves and open containers so anyone can download them and hit near-peak inference.

- **推理将吃掉巨量电力**：预计到 2030 年仅 OpenAI 与 Anthropic 合计就超过 100 吉瓦，2040 年达太瓦级；长期（2040）大部分增量算力可能上天入轨，但到 2030 年占比仍不到 1%。

- **Inference will consume enormous power**: By 2030 OpenAI and Anthropic alone are projected to exceed 100 GW combined, reaching terawatts by 2040; long term (2040) most incremental compute may go to space, but that's under 1% by 2030.
