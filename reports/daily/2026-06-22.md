## 1. bytedance/deer-flow

⭐ 72.7k（今日 +442）· Python · [链接](https://github.com/bytedance/deer-flow)

**是什么**：一个开源的长程「SuperAgent」执行框架，能做研究、写代码和创作；借助沙箱、记忆、工具、技能、子智能体和消息网关，处理从几分钟到几小时不等的多层级任务。

**What it is**: An open-source long-horizon SuperAgent harness that researches, codes, and creates. With the help of sandboxes, memories, tools, skill, subagents and message gateway, it handles different levels of tasks that could take minutes to hours.

**为什么值得关注**：来自字节跳动、已逼近 7.3 万星，是少数把「长时间运行」当成一等公民来设计的开源 Agent 框架。沙箱 + 记忆 + 子智能体 + 消息网关的组合，瞄准的是当下最难的工程问题——让 Agent 在几小时的任务里不跑偏。适合想自建复杂自动化流水线的团队。

**Why it matters**: Backed by ByteDance and already near 73k stars, it's one of the few open-source agent frameworks that treats long-running execution as a first-class concern. The sandbox + memory + subagents + message-gateway stack targets today's hardest engineering problem—keeping an agent on track across multi-hour tasks. A fit for teams building complex automation pipelines in-house.

**Why it matters**: While every vendor ships its own coding agent, omnigent goes the other way—a unifying layer that orchestrates them all, emphasizing portability, governance and sandboxing. It echoes Anthropic's recent Managed Agents "meta-harness" thesis: the abstraction outlasts any single harness.

### 播客要点 / Podcast

**Unsupervised Learning · 《AI Vibe Check: Lab Wars, Why APIs Might Vanish & Future Predictions》**（主持人 Jacob Efron，嘉宾 Ari，Datology 创始人、前 DeepMind/Meta 研究员，以及 Radical Ventures 的 Rob）。[节目链接](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W_iO8XxgD_I)

- 编码 agent 能在更长时间跨度上工作后，工程师正从「IC」转为「agent 的管理者」；但生产力提升常被高估，因为瓶颈只是从写代码转移到了 review 和「理解鸿沟」。
- 嘉宾对近前沿开放权重模型转为悲观：Meta 在收缩，中国实验室（Qwen、DeepSeek 等）倾向把最强模型留在 API 后、只开源较弱版本；他们认为开源模型公司没有可持续的商业模式。
- 一个「辛辣预测」：随着算力紧张，某前沿实验室（很可能是 Anthropic，也可能是 OpenAI）会在某段时间暂停或大幅限制 API 访问——「到 2027 年底」很有信心，甚至可能就在 2026 下半年。
- 「应用层全完蛋」是过度解读：没有一两家公司能赢下所有垂直市场，最后一公里的应用仍有大量空间。
- Anthropic 的下一个大赌注是生命科学/生物学（传闻在自建湿实验室）；Rob 预测「Anthropic 将成为全球最重要的生命科学公司之一」。

**Unsupervised Learning · "AI Vibe Check: Lab Wars, Why APIs Might Vanish & Future Predictions"** (host Jacob Efron, with Ari—Datology founder, ex-DeepMind/Meta—and Rob of Radical Ventures). [Episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W_iO8XxgD_I)

- As coding agents work over longer horizons, engineers are shifting from ICs to "managers of agents"; but productivity gains are often overstated, because the bottleneck just moves from writing code to review and the "understanding gap."
- The guests turned bearish on near-frontier open-weight AI: Meta is pulling back, and Chinese labs (Qwen, DeepSeek, etc.) increasingly keep their strongest models behind APIs while open-sourcing weaker versions; they see no sustainable business model for open-model companies.
- A spicy prediction: as the compute crunch bites, a frontier lab (likely Anthropic, possibly OpenAI) will suspend or heavily limit API access for some period—"by the end of 2027" with confidence, perhaps even in the back half of 2026.
- "Apps are cooked" is an overreaction: no one or two companies can win every vertical, and last-mile applications still have plenty of room.
- Anthropic's next big bet is life sciences/biology (rumored in-house wet labs); Rob predicts "Anthropic will become one of the most important life sciences companies in the world."
