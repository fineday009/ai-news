## 1. Panniantong/Agent-Reach

⭐ 30,466（今日 +1,100）· Python · [链接](https://github.com/Panniantong/Agent-Reach)

**是什么**：给你的 AI 智能体装上「眼睛」，让它能读取并搜索整个互联网——Twitter、Reddit、YouTube、GitHub、Bilibili、小红书等都能抓。

**What it is**: Give your AI agent eyes to see the entire internet — read and search Twitter, Reddit, YouTube, GitHub, Bilibili, XiaoHongshu and more.

**为什么值得关注**：Agent 落地最大的瓶颈之一就是「拿不到实时、跨平台的信息」。它把多个封闭平台的读取/搜索统一成一套接口，省去各自接 API、对抗反爬的麻烦，对做研究型、监控型 Agent 的开发者尤其实用。

**Why it matters**: One of the biggest blockers for real-world agents is access to fresh, cross-platform information. It unifies reading/searching across many walled-garden platforms behind one interface, sparing developers from wiring up each API and fighting anti-scraping — especially handy for research and monitoring agents.

## 2. trycua/cua

⭐ 18,197（今日 +70）· HTML · [链接](https://github.com/trycua/cua)

**是什么**：面向「计算机使用型智能体（Computer-Use Agents）」的开源基础设施，提供沙箱、SDK 和基准测试，用于训练与评测能操作真实电脑的 AI。

**What it is**: Open-source infrastructure for Computer-Use Agents — sandboxes, SDKs, and benchmarks to train and evaluate AI agents that operate a real computer.

**为什么值得关注**：当各家都在推「能点鼠标、敲键盘」的 Agent 时，缺的是安全隔离的运行环境和可比较的评测标准。cua 把这两块补齐，让你能在沙箱里放心地跑、量化地比，是 Computer-Use 方向少见的「打地基」型项目。

**Why it matters**: As everyone races to ship agents that click and type, what's missing is a safely isolated runtime and comparable benchmarks. cua fills both gaps, letting you run agents in a sandbox and measure them apples-to-apples — a rare foundational play in the computer-use space.

### 播客要点 / Podcast

**《Training Data》— LIVE: Jensen Huang on Building the Dynamo of the Intelligence Age**

英伟达 CEO 黄仁勋把当下定义为人类史上最大的基础设施建设，核心观点如下：

NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang frames this moment as the largest infrastructure build-out in human history. Key points:

- **从「检索」到「生成」**：过去六十年计算机本质是检索系统（所以叫数据中心），如今每一次回答、每一个像素都在实时生成。未来世界需要海量「智能发电机」，这正是英伟达造的东西。

- **From retrieval to generation**: For sixty years computers were retrieval systems (hence "data centers"); now every answer and pixel is generated in real time. The future needs a vast number of "intelligence dynamos" — which is what NVIDIA builds.

- **投资的「五层蛋糕」**：从底到顶依次是能源、芯片/计算/网络、基础设施（土地/电力/数据中心）、模型层、应用层；今年约有 1 万亿美元涌入这个生态，而它可能是个每年 20 万亿美元体量的市场。

- **The five-layer cake for investing**: energy → chips/compute/networking → infrastructure (land/power/data centers) → models → applications. ~$1T flows into this ecosystem this year, which he pegs as a potential $20T/year market.

- **关于工作的金句**：「你也许会、也许不会被 AI 抢走工作，但你一定会输给一个会用 AI 的人。」他以放射科为例——计算机视觉早已超越人类，但放射科医生的需求不降反升，因为机器自动化了任务，人去做更高价值的诊断（区分「任务」与「目的」）。

- **On jobs**: "You may or may not lose a job to an AI. But you will absolutely lose a job to someone who uses AI." Using radiology as proof — computer vision long surpassed humans, yet demand for radiologists rose, because automating the *task* freed people for the higher-value *purpose* of diagnosis.

- **驳斥末日论**：他直言「终结者」「奇点」式的叙事是无稽之谈；AI 正在消弭技术鸿沟——过去只有约 2% 的人会写 C++，而现在「人人都能用人类语言给计算机编程」。

- **Debunking doomerism**: He calls "Terminator/singularity" narratives nonsense, arguing AI is closing the technology divide — only ~2% could write C++, but now "everyone can program a computer" in human language.
