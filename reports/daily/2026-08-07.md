## 1. MiniMax-AI/MiniMax-H3

⭐ 654（新仓库，7 月 30 日创建）· Python · [链接](https://github.com/MiniMax-AI/MiniMax-H3)

**是什么**：MiniMax 开源的通用全模态视频生成模型，核心是一个 330 亿参数的稠密 Transformer，可接收文本、图像、视频、音频输入，生成最长 15 秒、2K 分辨率、且带原生立体声的视频，支持文生视频与参考图生成两种模式。

**What it is**: MiniMax's open-source omni-modal video generation model. At its core is a 33-billion-parameter dense Transformer that ingests text, image, video, and audio, and produces video up to 15 seconds at 2K resolution with native stereo audio, supporting both text-to-video and reference-based generation.

**为什么值得关注**：把「画面 + 声音」放进同一个模型一次生成，是开放视频模型少见的能力，此前多要靠外挂 TTS/音效拼接。对内容创作、短视频与游戏原型来说，这是一块可自托管、可微调的底座。

**Why it matters**: Generating picture and sound together from one model is rare among open video models—most bolt on TTS or sound effects afterward. For content creation, short video, and game prototyping, this is a self-hostable, fine-tunable foundation.

## 2. tirth8205/code-review-graph

⭐ 29,078（今日 +237）· Python · [链接](https://github.com/tirth8205/code-review-graph)

**是什么**：本地优先的代码智能图谱，面向 MCP 与 CLI 使用。它把代码库解析成可查询的图结构，让 AI 代理在做代码审查、跨文件推理时只加载相关上下文，从而压缩 token 消耗。

**What it is**: A local-first code intelligence graph for MCP and CLI. It parses a codebase into a queryable graph so AI agents pull only the relevant context during code review and cross-file reasoning, cutting token consumption.

**为什么值得关注**：随着代理编码普及，「怎样喂上下文」比「模型多强」更影响成本与准确率。用图谱做确定性检索、无需向量库，是当下很务实的一条路线，且本地运行、隐私友好。

**Why it matters**: As agentic coding spreads, *how* you feed context matters more to cost and accuracy than raw model power. Deterministic graph-based retrieval with no vector store is a pragmatic route, and it runs locally—privacy-friendly.

  There's a "slider" in consumer tech: as the technology matures, founders shift from deeply technical toward product-genius. Google was 95% technical, whereas the CEOs of Pinterest, Snap, and Instagram "weren't technical at all—they were product geniuses."

- 目前 AI 的消费级大赢家（ChatGPT、Character AI）本质仍偏后端、偏「模型即产品」，就像早期的 Google——一个文本框。这说明我们还很早。

- 值得注意的历史照应：OpenAI 是「一个研究实验室，意外做出了史上最大的消费产品」，其 DNA 与当年的 Google 高度相似——押注可能没有实际用途的长期研究，本不是风投常走的路。
  A notable historical echo: OpenAI is "a research lab that accidentally built the biggest consumer product of all time," its DNA strikingly similar to Google's—betting on long-horizon research with no obvious use, which isn't usually the venture playbook.
