## 5. gastownhall/beads

⭐ 29.1k（今日 +56）· Go · [链接](https://github.com/gastownhall/beads)

**是什么**：面向 AI Agent 的分布式图结构任务追踪器，基于 Dolt。

**What it is**: Distributed graph issue tracker for AI agents, powered by Dolt.

**为什么值得关注**：它给编码 Agent 一份持久、结构化的记忆：用带依赖关系的任务图代替零散的 Markdown 计划，Agent 通过 `bd ready` 找到可认领的工作、`bd update --claim` 认领、`bd close` 关闭并释放被阻塞的任务，长周期任务不再因上下文丢失而断档。底层是可版本化、支持单元格级合并的 Dolt 数据库，哈希式 ID 避免多 Agent、多分支并行时的冲突，`bd setup` 可一键接入 Claude Code、Codex、Cursor、Factory Droid 等。适合同时跑多个 Agent、需要共享任务状态的团队。

**Why it matters**: It gives coding agents persistent, structured memory: a dependency-aware task graph replaces scattered markdown plans, so agents find claimable work with `bd ready`, take it with `bd update --claim` and close it with `bd close`, which releases whatever it was blocking, and long-horizon tasks no longer fall apart when context is lost. Underneath is Dolt, a version-controlled database with cell-level merges, and hash-based IDs prevent collisions when several agents and branches work in parallel; `bd setup` wires it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Factory Droid and more. A fit for teams running several agents that need shared task state.

## 9. leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis

**是什么**：由 Lean 社区成员整理的黎曼猜想（Alpöge，2026-11-20）Lean 形式化证明。

**What it is**: Lean formalization of the Riemann Hypothesis (Alpöge, 2026-11-20) being cleaned up by members of the Lean community.

**为什么值得关注**：据 README，这是一个社区主导的项目，整理并维护 2026 年黎曼猜想以及 Dirichlet L 函数广义黎曼猜想（GRH）证明的 Lean 形式化，代码约 30 万行。`src/Main.lean` 证明 Mathlib 中的 `RiemannHypothesis` 等五条定理；`Challenge.lean` 只导入 Mathlib、用 `sorry` 给出同样五条陈述，比对工具会检查 Main.lean 证明的恰好是这些陈述、除 `propext`、`Quot.sound`、`Classical.choice` 外不用其他公理，并由 Lean 内核和 nanoda 两个检查器接受。目标是让没有大量算力的研究者也能构建、阅读这份证明，并把代码质量向 Mathlib 标准看齐；11 月 25 日主分支已切换到更精简的版本，最初公开的版本保存在 `original` 分支。

**Why it matters**: Per the README, this community-led effort cleans up and maintains a Lean formalization of the 2026 proofs of the Riemann Hypothesis and of GRH for Dirichlet L-functions—about 300,000 lines of code. `src/Main.lean` proves five theorems including Mathlib's `RiemannHypothesis`; `Challenge.lean` states the same five with `sorry` in a file that imports only Mathlib, and a comparator checks that Main.lean proves exactly those statements, uses no axioms beyond `propext`, `Quot.sound` and `Classical.choice`, and is accepted by both Lean's kernel and nanoda. The aim is a proof researchers can build and read without massive compute, with code quality pushed toward Mathlib standards; on November 25 the main branch moved to a much more condensed version, and the first public release is kept on the `original` branch.

**是什么**：用 Rust 重写的 Postgres，如今已比 Postgres 和 ClickHouse 更快。

**为什么值得关注**：7 月 12 日上日报时 2.1k 星，当时的卖点是"跑通完整回归测试"；如今 6,081 星，v0.4-beta 已发布，定位变成"为 Postgres 数据做快速分析的替代品"。它与 Postgres 18 线协议和 SQL 方言兼容，现有驱动、ORM 和 `psql` 无需改动；新加入向量化、推送式、JIT 编译的查询引擎 Gravity，线程化并发模型，防止单个查询拖垮整个库的查询调度器，以及统一管理缓冲池、列式缓存和查询内存的自动内存管理器。可以直接在浏览器里试用编译成 WebAssembly 的完整服务端（AGPL-3.0）。性能说法来自项目自述。

You can try a full server compiled to WebAssembly in the browser (AGPL-3.0). The performance claims are the project's own.

### 播客 / Podcast：[Unsupervised Learning · Ep 98: China Expert on Jensen’s Return](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gkd5eOQGASU)

**一句话结论**：在 ChinaTalk 主持人 Jordan Schneider 看来，黄仁勋回归之后真正要盯的，是刚出台的出口管制新规会不会被撤销或不执行。主持人是 Redpoint 投资人 Jacob Efron。

- **问题不在马来西亚**：Schneider 认为，经马来西亚等地的合法销售不是问题，问题在于对被禁芯片走私进中国、甚至流向军方相关用户长期睁一只眼闭一只眼。他拿银行业类比：美国财政部开出巨额罚单之后，银行才真正认真做合规。
- **新规的两项内容**：在台积电代工先进芯片需要商务部批准；马来西亚等国为中国客户服务的 neocloud 在 90 天后不得再这样做。他估计中国约五分之二的算力来自马来西亚数据中心和远程访问，所以主张国会立法，免得政策被总统个人交易推翻。
- **算力差距才是关键**：只要不卖 EUV 等制造设备、并堵住远程访问和走私，西方能维持 10 到 15 倍的算力优势，软硬件协同优化相比之下只是舍入误差。华为缺的不是需求和资金，而是 HBM 和制造设备。Efron 则认为，有黄仁勋掌舵的英伟达对整个生态更健康。
- **政治逆风**：他引用民调说只有 2% 的美国人希望 AI 发展得更快，预计下一轮选举会出现直接反对这个行业的候选人。他的判断是，光靠砸钱解决不了这个问题。
- **机器人还不行**：他在 CoRL 会议上看到人形机器人现场演示基本"不能干活"；硬件和稀土磁体都高度依赖中国。

**The Takeaway**: For ChinaTalk's Jordan Schneider, what matters after Jensen Huang's return is whether the new export-control rule gets rescinded or goes unenforced. The host is Redpoint investor Jacob Efron.

- **Malaysia isn't the problem**: Schneider says legal sales through places like Malaysia aren't the issue; the issue is years of turning a blind eye to banned chips being smuggled into China, including to military-linked users. His analogy is banking: banks only got serious about compliance after the Treasury imposed huge fines.
- **What the rule does**: fabbing an advanced chip at TSMC now needs Commerce Department approval, and neoclouds in Malaysia and elsewhere must stop serving Chinese customers after 90 days. He estimates about two-fifths of China's compute came from Malaysian data centers and remote access, and wants Congress to legislate so the policy can't be dealt away.
- **The compute gap is what counts**: as long as the US withholds EUV and other chipmaking tools and shuts off remote access and smuggling, the West keeps a 10–15x compute advantage, next to which hardware-software co-design amounts to a rounding error. Huawei lacks HBM and manufacturing tools, not demand or money. Efron counters that Nvidia with Jensen at the helm is healthier for the ecosystem.
- **Political headwinds**: citing a poll in which only 2% of Americans want AI to progress faster, he expects candidates in the next cycle to campaign directly against the industry, and that "money can't solve the problem."
- **Robots aren't there yet**: the humanoids he saw at the CoRL conference could barely do anything in person, and both the hardware and the rare-earth magnets depend heavily on China.
