## 8. clawkwork/clawk

**是什么**：给编码 Agent 一台一次性的 Linux 虚拟机，而不是你的笔记本。

**What it is**: Give coding agents a disposable Linux VM, not your laptop.

**为什么值得关注**：随着 Agent 越来越敢「跳过权限、直接动手」，隔离爆炸半径成了刚需。用一台随开随扔的 microVM 承接 Agent 的所有操作，比逐条审批更省心也更安全，是把「让 Agent 放手干」变得可接受的关键基础设施——纯 Go、聚焦沙箱，是今日榜单少见的非 AI 内核项目。

**Why it matters**: As agents grow bolder about "skip permissions and just do it," isolating the blast radius becomes essential. Fronting all agent actions with a throwaway microVM is calmer and safer than approving each step — key infrastructure for making "let the agent rip" acceptable. Pure Go and focused on sandboxing, it's one of today's few non-AI-core picks.

### 🎧 播客 / Podcast

**MAD Podcast · OpenAI 算力负责人：我们建得再快也不够（嘉宾 Sachin Katti，OpenAI 工业算力负责人；主持 Matt Turck）**

**MAD Podcast · OpenAI's Compute Chief: We Can't Build Fast Enough (guest Sachin Katti, Head of Industrial Compute at OpenAI; host Matt Turck)**

> 核心观点：AI 的瓶颈已经从「模型」搬到「物理世界」——电力、供应链、以及把算力建得比现实允许的更快。
>
> The Takeaway: AI's bottleneck has moved from the model to the physical world — power, supply chains, and building compute faster than physical reality wants to move.

- **需求永不满足**：「任何我们能上线的算力，都会被立刻消耗掉。」最大的担忧不是过剩而是不够——每次以为算力够了、想放缓，事后都后悔不该放缓。
  Demand is insatiable: "anything we can bring online, we consume immediately." The biggest worry isn't oversupply but shortage — every time they thought they had enough and slowed down, they regretted it.

- **数据中心是「把电子变成 token 的巨型工厂」**：与云时代数据中心的根本区别就是规模——本质是在建大型超级计算机，而模型越强、任务越复杂，就需要越大的机器。
  A data center is a "giant factory turning electrons into tokens." The fundamental difference from cloud-era data centers is scale — they're really building large supercomputers, and the more capable the models, the bigger the machines needed.

- **电力是真正的约束**：当下靠燃气轮机（美国最密集、可运输、供应最广的能源形态），但受供应链掣肘；核能重新回到讨论桌上。
  Power is the real constraint: today it's gas turbines (the densest, transportable, most available energy form in the US), but bottlenecked by the supply chain — and nuclear is back on the table.

- **「递归」并不遥远**：OpenAI 相信 AI 将设计出训练与运行下一代 AI 所需的系统，已经进入自研芯片（Jalapeno），AI 也开始参与设计自己的芯片。
  "Recursion" isn't far off: OpenAI believes AI will design the systems it needs to train and run the next generation of AI — it's already moved into custom silicon (Jalapeno), and AI is beginning to help design its own chips.
