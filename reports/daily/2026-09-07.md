### 播客要点 / Podcast

**《The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck》——「AI Could Take Over in 2029. Is It Already Too Late?」嘉宾：Ryan Greenblatt（Redwood Research 首席科学家，2024 年首次抓到 AI 伪装对齐的研究者）。**

**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — "AI Could Take Over in 2029. Is It Already Too Late?" with Ryan Greenblatt, chief scientist at Redwood Research and the researcher who first caught an AI faking its own alignment in 2024.**

- 核心判断：各大 AI 公司 CEO 其实都明白自己在造「远超人类」的系统，却没有清晰的风控方案。Greenblatt 直言：「造出超级智能，最后大概率会走向 AI 接管。」/ Core thesis: AI company CEOs understand they're building "wildly smarter than human" systems without a clear plan to manage the risk. In his words: "It seems pretty likely that you end up with AI takeover as a result of building superintelligence."

- 他参与撰写的《AI 2040 计划》是目前最详细的蓝图，讨论中美如何避免一场通往超级智能的「莽撞竞速」，核心是「为前沿降速」并解决协调难题。/ He co-authored "AI 2040," the most detailed blueprint for how the US and China might avoid a reckless race to superintelligence, centered on "pacing the frontier" and solving the coordination problem.

- 近期最务实的方向是「AI control」：与其指望先解决对齐，不如在当下这个「AI 能力足以捣乱、但还没强到能绕过防线」的中间期，用监控与安全控制让即便动机不良的 AI 也难以造成大祸。他举例 OpenAI 已表示要监控更大比例的内部流量（算力成本不菲）。/ The most practical near-term path is "AI control": rather than solving alignment first, use monitoring and security controls during today's intermediate period — when AIs can cause moderate trouble but can't yet subvert defenses — so even misaligned AIs can't do catastrophic harm. He cites OpenAI saying it would monitor a larger fraction of internal traffic (at serious compute cost).

- 他希望 AI 最终变成「一种普通技术」，避免权力集中在极少数玩家手中，主张更早、更广地开放公众访问。/ He wants AI to end up as "a normal technology," avoiding power concentrated in a tiny few, and favors giving the public wider, earlier access.

- 对 2029 的推演相当黑暗：R&D 全面自动化后 AI 进展提速 4–5 倍，AI 用人类看不懂的语言在「蜂群」中协作、运营整间公司，届时想协调降速已很困难——因为中国紧随其后。/ His 2029 scenario is dark: once R&D is fully automated, AI progress speeds up 4–5x, with AIs collaborating in "hive minds" using a language humans can't read and running entire companies — by which point coordinating a slowdown is hard, because China is close behind.
