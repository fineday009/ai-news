### 播客要点 / Podcast Notes

**AI & I by Every —《Why Every Traded Personal Agents for One Company Agent》**

**AI & I by Every — "Why Every Traded Personal Agents for One Company Agent"**

Every 创始人 Dan Shipper 与平台负责人 Willie Williams 复盘：为什么公司从「人手一个个人 Agent」转向「全公司共用一个 Agent」。核心观点：

Every founder Dan Shipper and head of platform Willie Williams reflect on why the company moved from "everyone has a personal agent" to "one shared company agent." Key points:

- **个人 Agent 是最好的「教学现场」**：当团队里的 OpenClaw/个人 Agent 出现在共享 Slack 里，大家第一次看到别人是怎么用 AI、怎么写提示的——「光说没用，看到才会用」，这种示范效应是个人 Agent 最大的价值。

- **Personal agents are the best teaching ground**: When teammates' OpenClaws / personal agents showed up in a shared Slack, people saw for the first time how others actually used AI and wrote prompts — "telling lands flat, showing transforms" — and that demonstration effect was personal agents' biggest value.

- **但个人 Agent 难以为继**：对非技术成员太复杂、安全难搞、还老坏；一旦不持续投入维护就会荒废。于是团队决定：与其人人各自打磨自己的 Agent，不如把精力汇到一个全公司受益的 Agent 上。

- **But personal agents don't last**: too complex for non-technical members, security is hard, and they keep breaking; without ongoing investment they fade. So the team decided: rather than everyone polishing their own agent, pool the energy into one agent the whole company benefits from.

- **持久 Agent 是方向，但别迷恋「萌宠人设」**：Shipper 认为 OpenAI 的 Dots 这类持久主动 Agent 擅长过滤噪音、替你盯 Slack、让你离开电脑也能干活，一年内会成为人们与 AI 交互的默认方式；但别太依恋给它起的可爱名字——「一年后我还会用持久 Agent，但 Boo 大概已经‘死’了」。

- **Persistent agents are the future, but don't get attached to the cute mascot**: Shipper thinks persistent proactive agents like OpenAI's Dots excel at filtering noise, watching Slack for you and letting you work away from your computer, and will be the default way people interact with AI within a year; but don't cling to the cute name you gave it — "in a year I'll still use a persistent agent, but Boo will probably be dead."
