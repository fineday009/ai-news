**Why it matters**: Its fully continuous, token-free autoregressive design claims SOTA on benchmarks like multilingual speaker similarity (83.9 avg), and it ships base, self-corrective, and distilled checkpoints. For developers wanting a commercially usable, fine-tunable high-quality Mandarin/Cantonese TTS, it's a rare open option.

## 4. tamnd/kage

⭐ 695（新项目 · 创建于 2026-06-14）· Go · [链接](https://github.com/tamnd/kage)

**是什么**：把任意网站克隆成可离线浏览的文件夹，用无头 Chrome 渲染后剥掉所有 JavaScript，只保留 HTML/CSS/图片/字体。

**What it is**: Clones any website into an offline-browsable folder—rendering in headless Chrome, then stripping out every script while keeping HTML, CSS, images, and fonts.

**为什么值得关注**：传统「另存为」常常存出打不开、还偷偷联网的残页。kage 捕获页面最终渲染态再去脚本，产出干净、隔离、可校验的存档，支持 ZIM 压缩包、自包含可执行文件、断点续传与确定性打包。对做网页归档、离线知识库的人很实用。昨天刚建仓就破 600 星。

**Why it matters**: A plain "Save As" often yields broken pages that still phone home. Kage captures the final rendered state and removes all scripts, producing clean, isolated, checksummable archives—with ZIM packages, self-contained executables, resumable crawls, and deterministic packing. It's handy for web archiving and offline knowledge bases, and crossed 600 stars a day after launch.

## 5. SkyBlue997/enableMacosAI

⭐ 1,201（新项目 · 创建于 2026-06-10）· Shell · [链接](https://github.com/SkyBlue997/enableMacosAI)

**是什么**：在国行 Mac（macOS 27 / Apple Silicon）上一键开启完整 Apple 智能，包含端侧与 Private Cloud Compute 云端能力。

**What it is**: One command to enable full Apple Intelligence—both on-device and Private Cloud Compute—on China-region Macs running macOS 27 on Apple Silicon.

**为什么值得关注**：国行设备固件区域码被设为 CH/A，系统据此关闭 AI 功能。该项目用一个极简内核扩展（kext）在 IORegistry 层把区域码改成 LL/A，让所有进程都把设备识别为美版，一处改动全局生效。对被区域限制挡住的用户是直接、彻底的解法（需关闭 SIP）。

**Why it matters**: China-region devices carry a CH/A firmware region code that gates Apple Intelligence off. The project uses a minimal kernel extension to rewrite that code to LL/A at the IORegistry layer, so every process sees a US-region device from one change. For users blocked by regional gating, it's a direct, system-wide fix (SIP must be disabled).

### 播客要点 / Podcast

**No Priors —《Biohub: The Future of Biology is Open-Source》**（嘉宾：Mark Zuckerberg、Priscilla Chan，及首席科学家 Alex Rives）

No Priors — "Biohub: The Future of Biology is Open-Source" (guests: Mark Zuckerberg, Priscilla Chan, and Head of Science Alex Rives)

- **造工具，而非治病**：Biohub 的定位不是亲自治愈疾病，而是开源工具去加速整个科学界的进步。Zuckerberg：「我们不会是治愈疾病的人，目标是建工具让科学界集体把病治了。」当年「本世纪末治愈所有疾病」被诺奖得主当笑话，如今他觉得「这个目标反而太保守了」。

  **Tools, not cures**: Biohub's role isn't to cure diseases itself but to open-source tools that accelerate the whole field. As Zuckerberg puts it, "the theory isn't that we're gonna cure the diseases—we're not; it's that we wanna help accelerate the pace of progress for the whole scientific field." A goal once laughed at by Nobel laureates—cure all disease by century's end—he now calls "too conservative."

- **蛋白质语言模型的涌现能力**：团队折叠并预测了超 11 亿个蛋白质结构，而模型并非为抗体或某个靶点专门设计——只是训练它「理解蛋白质」，生物结构与功能便作为涌现能力出现，像 NLP 一样从「token 预测」中长出生物学。

  **Emergent abilities in protein language models**: The team folded and predicted structures for over 1.1 billion proteins—without designing the model for antibodies or any single target. They "just designed a model that could understand proteins," and biological structure and function emerged from token prediction, much like in NLP.

- **为什么是非营利而非创业公司**：数据，而非算力，是更大的瓶颈——蛋白质数据没有现成工厂可买，必须发明新的实验方法（细胞工程、新型设备）去产生。Biohub 用开源把模型尽快交到更多科学家手里，并承担 10–15 年的长周期。

  **Why a nonprofit, not a startup**: Data, not compute, is the bigger bottleneck—protein data can't be bought from a factory and requires inventing new wet-lab methods (cellular engineering, novel devices) to generate. Biohub open-sources its models to get them into more scientists' hands faster, over a 10–15 year horizon.

- **可编程生物与个体化医疗**：当设计分子、跑通各阶段的门槛大幅降低，「为每位患者定制药物」成为可能；叠加患者自组织的罕见病登记与试验，整套药物开发范式与「未来医学长什么样」都会被改写。

  **Programmable biology and individualized medicine**: As the barrier to designing a molecule and clearing each stage drops, "a medicine for every individual patient" becomes plausible; combined with patient-organized rare-disease registries and trials, the whole drug-development paradigm—and what the future of medicine looks like—gets rewritten.
