## 2. LMCache/LMCache

⭐ 8,911（今日 +238）· Python · [链接](https://github.com/LMCache/LMCache)

**是什么**：为大模型推理打造的最快 KV Cache 层，给你的 LLM 加速。

**What it is**: LMCache — supercharge your LLM with the fastest KV cache layer.

**为什么值得关注**：长上下文和高并发场景下，重复计算 KV 缓存是推理成本和延迟的大头。LMCache 通过跨请求复用、分层存储把这部分省下来，能直接接入 vLLM 等推理栈，是实打实的推理基础设施而非演示玩具。

**Why it matters**: Under long contexts and high concurrency, recomputing the KV cache dominates inference cost and latency. LMCache reuses caches across requests with tiered storage and plugs into stacks like vLLM — real inference infrastructure rather than a demo.
