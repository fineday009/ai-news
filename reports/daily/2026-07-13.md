## 2. Dicklesworthstone/destructive_command_guard

⭐ 3.0k（今日 +444）· Rust · [链接](https://github.com/Dicklesworthstone/destructive_command_guard)

**是什么**：一个高性能的「危险命令拦截钩子」（dcg），在 AI 编码 Agent 真正执行破坏性命令前把它拦下。

**What it is**: A high-performance hook (dcg) that blocks destructive commands before an AI coding agent actually runs them.

**为什么值得关注**：当 Agent 被放开手去跑 shell，`rm -rf`、误删分支这类事故的代价被放大。dcg 用一层 Rust 钩子横跨 Claude Code、Codex CLI、Gemini CLI、Copilot、Cursor、Hermes、Grok 等主流工具做拦截，正好卡在「自动化提效」与「别把我硬盘清空」的矛盾点上，今日 +444 星说明这份焦虑相当普遍。

**Why it matters**: Once agents are free to run shells, accidents like `rm -rf` or a wiped branch get much costlier. dcg drops a single Rust hook across Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, Copilot, Cursor, Hermes and Grok to catch them, sitting exactly on the tension between "automate everything" and "please don't erase my disk." Its +444 stars today shows how widely shared that anxiety is.

### 播客要点 / Podcast

**No Priors — 与 Booking.com CEO Glenn Fogel 聊「AI 视角下的旅行」**（[节目链接](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8nj_0wZkbtA)）

- **没有护城河**：Fogel 开门见山——「不存在什么护城河，也不存在什么能让你免于被创新冲击的安身之处」；今天的优势明天就可能消失，长期赢的唯一办法是不停开发新服务、新做法。

- **Penny 的真实曲线**：Priceline 的 agentic 工具 Penny 采用量「过去几个月每月翻一倍」，带来更高转化、更短的下单路径、更低取消率；但相对去年 1860 亿美元的旅行总额，它的绝对规模「还非常非常小」。

- **Token 经济学是新变量**：该用哪个模型、用在哪、来回调用几次、单次成交成本多少、长期 LTV 如何——这些「还不知道答案」的问题，正成为把 AI 真正铺开前必须算清的账。

- **客服先见成效**：用 AI 处理客服后，单次接触成本已降约 10%、满意度上升；但他反复强调要保留「转人工」的选项，因为「最终一切以对客户最好为准」。

- **AI 的价值在出岔子时最亮**：旅行像多米诺骨牌，一个环节倒下就连锁崩塌；Fogel 的目标是让系统能提前预测问题、在其发生前就建议调整。今年公司在 AI 与技术使能上的投入约 7 亿美元。

**No Priors — "Travel Through the Lens of AI" with Booking.com CEO Glenn Fogel** ([episode link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8nj_0wZkbtA))

- **No moat**: Fogel opens bluntly—"there is no such thing as a moat, no such thing as somewhere you're gonna be protected against innovation"; today's advantage can vanish tomorrow, and the only way to win long-term is to keep developing new services and new ways of doing things.

- **Penny's real curve**: Priceline's agentic tool Penny has seen adoption "double every month for the past few months," lifting conversion, shortening the path to booking and lowering cancellations—yet against last year's $186B in travel it's still "really, really small" in absolute terms.

- **Token economics is the new variable**: which model, used where, how many round-trips, cost per completed trip, long-term LTV—these still-unanswered questions are the accounting that has to be settled before AI can be fully rolled out.

- **Customer service pays off first**: with AI handling support, cost per contact is already down ~10% and satisfaction is up; but he repeatedly stresses preserving a "talk to a human" option, because "in the end it's always what's best for the customer."

- **AI shines most when things go wrong**: travel is dominoes—one thing falls and it all starts falling; Fogel's goal is a system that predicts problems well enough to suggest changes before they happen. The company is investing roughly $700M in AI and tech enablement this year.
