**Why it matters**: It lets AI coding assistants "remember" the whole codebase instead of stuffing files into context every turn—exactly the gap in today's agent toolchains. Written in C with zero dependencies and sub-ms queries, its performance focus hits the token-and-latency pain of large repos.

## 5. alexzhang13/rlm

⭐ 4,946（今日 +43）· Python · [链接](https://github.com/alexzhang13/rlm)

**是什么**：递归语言模型（RLM）的通用即插即用推理库，支持多种沙箱。RLM 把标准的补全调用换成一个能访问代码环境的调用，让模型自己以编程方式拆解、递归调用自身来处理近乎无限长的上下文。

**What it is**: A general plug-and-play inference library for Recursive Language Models (RLMs), supporting various sandboxes. RLMs replace the standard completion call with one that gives the model a code environment, so it can programmatically decompose and recursively call itself over near-infinite-length input.

**为什么值得关注**：超长上下文一直靠堆窗口或外挂检索硬扛，RLM 换了个思路——让模型自己决定怎么把输入切块、递归处理，是上下文工程里少见的新范式，研究与工程都值得跟踪。

**Why it matters**: Long context is usually brute-forced with bigger windows or bolt-on retrieval; RLM flips it—the model itself decides how to chunk and recurse over its input. A rare new paradigm in context engineering, worth tracking for both research and engineering.

### 🎧 播客 / Podcast

**Training Data：《Simulating Humans at Scale: Simile's Joon Sung Park》**

一句话要点：与其造一个「超人」的理性模型，不如造一个尽可能「像人」的模型，去大规模模拟真实人群的行为。

The takeaway: instead of a superhuman "rational" model, build one that is as human as possible—to simulate the behavior of real populations at scale.

- Simile 创始人 Joon Sung Park（斯坦福「Smallville」生成式智能体小镇的作者）正在打造一个「人类行为基础模型」，把 25 个智能体的小镇实验推向真实社会模拟。/ Joon Sung Park, author of Stanford's "Smallville" generative-agents town, is building a foundation model of human behavior, scaling the 25-agent experiment toward real societal simulation.
- 核心是弥合「说–做差距」：大模型训练于人们「在网上说的话」（态度数据），Simile 则采集行为数据（RCT 随机对照试验、人生故事访谈）来预测人们「实际会做什么」。/ The core is closing the "say-do gap": LLMs train on what people say online (attitudinal data), while Simile collects behavioral data (RCTs, life-story interviews) to predict what people actually do.
- 一句原话点题：「今天的模型像智能的 CPU……Simile 的模型更接近智能的 GPU」——不追求超人，而追求能代表不同子人群真实价值观与偏好的众多子单元。/ A memorable line: "today's models are the CPU of the intelligence unit… Simile's model is closer to the GPU"—not superhuman, but many subunits representing the real values of different subpopulations.
- 应用场景：概念测试、定价、甚至模拟财报电话会议；客户最大的痛点往往不是「问什么问题」，而是「如何触达有代表性的人群」。/ Use cases span concept testing, pricing, even simulating earnings calls; customers' biggest pain is often not "what to ask" but "how to reach a representative population."

[节目 / episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lfhFmwcESRw)
