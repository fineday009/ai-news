### 播客要点 / Podcast

**Training Data —《Building the Automated AGI Lab: Core Automation's Jerry Tworek and Rohan Anil》**

一句话takeaway：Transformer 已经赢在"经济性"，但它现在正是通往更强模型的瓶颈——下一步要造能在使用中、在真实数据上"测试时学习"的新架构。

The takeaway: the transformer already won on economics, but it's now the bottleneck to smarter models—the next step is a new architecture that "learns at test time" on real user data.

- Core Automation 由前 OpenAI VP、strawberry/推理团队负责人 Jerry Tworek 与前 Gemini 预训练负责人、Google Brain 与 Anthropic 老将 Rohan Anil 创立，主张"要替换 Transformer，先深刻欣赏它带我们走了多远"。

  Core Automation was founded by Jerry Tworek (ex-OpenAI VP, strawberry/reasoning lead) and Rohan Anil (ex-Gemini pretraining lead, Google Brain and Anthropic veteran), arguing that "to replace the transformer, first deeply appreciate how far it carried us."

- Jerry 曾是坚定的 RL 极大主义者，2024 年认为 2025 年就能实现 AGI；但基准分数一路上涨、真实世界的杂乱任务却没被解决，因为训练/评测分布与真实分布是"同一枚硬币的两面"，都不够真实。

  Jerry was a committed RL maximalist who in 2024 expected AGI by 2025; benchmark scores kept rising but messy real-world tasks weren't solved, because training and eval distributions are "two sides of the same coin" and neither matches reality.

- 他给出的结论是需要"测试时学习"的模型：在上下文学习数据高效但不可扩展（Codex 用 ~20 分钟就得压缩上下文），微调又有灾难性遗忘——因此要 meta-learn 出一种能在长时程上表达"学习本身"的架构。

  His conclusion: we need models that learn at test time—in-context learning is data-efficient but not scalable (Codex needs context compaction after ~20 minutes) and fine-tuning suffers catastrophic forgetting—so we must meta-learn an architecture that expresses "learning itself" over long horizons.

- 为什么是创业公司而非大厂：最成功的实验室正陷入史上最激烈的竞争，全都在赶工发布"同一个编码 Agent"，没有余力去赌 Transformer 的替代路线；而这正是 Core Automation 想填的生态位。

  Why a startup and not a big lab: the most successful labs are locked in the fiercest competition ever, all rushing to ship "the same coding agent," with no appetite to bet on transformer alternatives—exactly the niche Core Automation aims to fill.

- 金句：Jerry 说 Transformer 的"神奇"在于它在经济上成立——"训练它的成本低于它带来的收入，这是机器学习的魔法，并非天经地义"；而这一点对 LSTM 未必成立，正是它没能被大规模投喂算力的原因。

  Memorable quote: Jerry says the transformer's magic is that it's economically viable—"the cost of training them is lower than the revenue that they generate, which is the magic of machine learning; it's not guaranteed by itself"—something that wouldn't hold for LSTMs, which is why no one poured compute into scaling them.
