### 🎧 播客 / Podcast

**AI & I by Every**：《The Founder of a $1.5B AI Company on What Comes After the First Wave of AI Apps》——Every CEO Dan Shipper 对话 Granola 联合创始人兼 CEO Chris Pedregal（公司估值 15 亿美元）。

*AI & I by Every*: "The Founder of a $1.5B AI Company on What Comes After the First Wave of AI Apps" — Every's Dan Shipper talks with Chris Pedregal, cofounder and CEO of Granola (valued at $1.5B).

要点 / Key takeaways：

- **创业是"贴身刀战"，赢的时候也一样难。** Chris 坦言最让他意外的是：创业不只在失败时艰难，成功时同样是刀战——"你永远在能力边缘之外"。

  Startups are "knife fights" — brutal even when you're winning. What surprised Chris most: it's hard not only when things fail but when they work, because "you're always just beyond the edge of your abilities."

- **会议记录只是入口，不是终点。** "Meeting notes are not the end all, be all value everyone's running after. There's something much bigger." 他认为我们正处在一场计算革命的极早期。

  Meeting notes were just the door, not the destination. In his words: "Meeting notes are not the end all, be all value that everyone's running after. There's something much bigger." He believes we're in the very earliest steps of a computing revolution.

- **工作正分化为两个界面。** Dan 提出：一端是 Slack 里的异步、多人、公开的委派式协作（Agent 像同事）；另一端是 Codex/Claude 桌面端——你和 Agent 坐在同一个应用里、共同操作。

  Work is bifurcating into two surfaces. Dan's framing: async, multiplayer, public delegation in Slack (agent-as-coworker) on one side; a Codex/Claude desktop where you and the agent sit in the same app and manipulate the same thing on the other.

- **"Codex 原生应用"新范式。** 应用只负责保存状态与渲染 UI，用户"把自己的 Agent 带过来"（通过 MCP/CLI 直接修改 UI 状态），而不是每个应用各自造一个 Agent。

  A new "Codex-native app" paradigm: apps are responsible only for saving state and rendering UI, and you "bring your own agent" to them (via MCP/CLI that can modify the UI state), instead of every app building its own agent.

- **开发者的模式会外溢到所有知识工作。** 一切始于开发者的 agent-in-the-loop 模式最终会进入全部知识工作——Dan 举例：他让 Codex 打开 Verizon 网站、登录并与客服对话，替他办完了搬家换网。

  Patterns that start with developers spill into all knowledge work. Dan's example: he had Codex open the Verizon site, log in, and chat with support to switch his internet during a move — no manual steps on his end.
