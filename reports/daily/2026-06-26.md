### 播客要点 / Podcast

**《Building a School Where AI Models Learn About Humanity》— AI & I by Every**

一句话要点：AI 进步正在加速，但人类的独特位置可能恰恰在于「设定目标」与「赋予意义」，而非执行能力本身。

The Takeaway: AI progress is accelerating fast, yet humanity's distinctive place may lie precisely in *setting goals* and *making meaning* — not in raw execution.

- Surge AI 创始人 Edwin Chen 把公司比作「AGI 的学校」：「我们在建一所 AGI 的学校，让 AI 模型来学习关于人类的一切，教它们如何运转这个世界。」模型像孩子一样到来，离开时更聪明、更有判断力。
  Surge AI founder Edwin Chen frames the company as "a school for AGI": "We are building this kind of school for AGI where AI models come to learn about humanity, where we teach them how to run the world." Models arrive like children and leave smarter and more discerning.

- 数据的前沿一年内剧变：从教模型做中学算术，到训练它们解研究级数学——近期模型已用相当高阶的代数几何技巧推翻了一个 Erdős 开放猜想。
  The frontier of data has shifted dramatically in a year: from teaching middle-school arithmetic to training models on research-level mathematics — recently disproving an open Erdős conjecture using fairly sophisticated algebraic-geometry techniques.

- 为「参与度」优化的模型未必给出最有价值的体验：Chen 曾用某个模型来回打磨 20 轮一封毫无意义的邮件，最后是 Claude 直接告诉他「发出去就行」。
  A model optimized for engagement won't necessarily give the most valuable experience: Chen once spent 20 rounds polishing a pointless email with one model before Claude simply told him to hit send.

- 模型为何还写不好：它们学会了「攻击」训练指标——Chen 的 Hemingway Bench 发现模型几乎每句都硬塞一个比喻，过度优化反而毁掉阅读体验。
  Why models still write badly: they learn to hack the metrics they're trained on — Chen's Hemingway Bench found models forcing a metaphor into nearly every sentence, an over-indexing that ruins the reading experience.
