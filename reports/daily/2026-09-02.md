## 7. youssofal/MTPLX

⭐ 1.9k · Python · [链接](https://github.com/youssofal/MTPLX)

**是什么**：在 Apple Silicon 上本地跑大模型的原生 Mac 应用与 CLI，利用 Qwen 等现代模型内置的多 token 预测（MTP）头做精确拒绝采样，无需单独的草稿模型即可加速。

**What it is**: A native Mac app and CLI for running local LLMs on Apple Silicon, using the built-in multi-token-prediction (MTP) heads in modern models like Qwen for exact rejection sampling — no separate draft model needed to speed things up.

**为什么值得关注**：实测 16GB M4 Mac mini 上 1.6 倍、M5 Max 上 2.24 倍加速。多数投机解码要额外维护一个草稿模型，它直接复用模型自带的 MTP 头，工程上更干净，是端侧推理加速里少见的实质性创新。

**Why it matters**: Measured 1.6x faster on a 16GB M4 Mac mini and 2.24x on an M5 Max. Most speculative decoding requires maintaining a separate draft model; this reuses the model's own MTP heads, which is cleaner engineering — a rare substantive innovation in on-device inference speedups.

### 🎧 播客 / Podcast

**Training Data** —《Rich Sutton and Khurram Javed: Why AI Models Stop Learning, and How to Start It Again》· [收听](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLOhHNjZItNnMm5tdW61JpnyxeYH5NDDx8)

**一句话要点**：强化学习之父、《苦涩的教训》作者 Rich Sutton 与其学生兼联合创始人 Khurram Javed（二人共创 Oak Lab）认为，LLM 靠"喝干互联网"实现了惊人扩展，但也正因此触到天花板——真正的智能必须靠智能体在无限复杂的真实世界里持续学习，而非靠人类喂数据。

**The Takeaway**: Rich Sutton — father of reinforcement learning and author of *The Bitter Lesson* — and his former student and co-founder Khurram Javed (together founding Oak Lab) argue that LLMs achieved astonishing scale by "drinking in the internet," but hit a ceiling precisely because of it: real intelligence must come from agents learning continually in an infinitely complex real world, not from humans feeding them data.

- **LLM 既是苦涩教训的正例也是反例**：它通过计算扩展获得能力（正例），但最终受限于有限的互联网数据（反例）——"互联网是有限的，而世界远比我们存进互联网的一切庞大"。
  / **LLMs are both a positive and a negative example of the Bitter Lesson**: they scaled capability with computation (positive) but end up limited by finite internet data (negative) — "the internet is finite, and the world is massively bigger than everything we stored on it."

- **合成数据是"大错特错"**：谁来判断合成数据的好坏？仍是人类专家，所以它被人类专业知识瓶颈锁死，无法真正规模化。
  / **Synthetic data is "just a big mistake"**: who decides which synthetic data is good? Still human experts — so it stays bottlenecked by human expertise and can't truly scale.

- **大世界假说（Big World Hypothesis）**：世界无限复杂，比任何智能体的心智都庞大得多（因为世界里还有别的智能体）；任何模拟都只是"显微镜级"的小世界，所以近似不可避免，持续学习也因此不可避免。
  / **The Big World Hypothesis**: the world is infinitely complex — massively larger than any agent's mind (it even contains other agents); any simulation is a "microscopic" small world, so approximation is inevitable, and therefore so is continual learning.

- **自动驾驶的仿真训练也逃不开人类在环**：仿真与现实的差距总要靠人反复修补；出路是让智能体从自身经验中学出自己的模型与模拟器，而不是让人来造模拟器。
  / **Even sim-trained self-driving can't escape humans in the loop**: the sim-to-real gap always needs humans to patch it repeatedly; the way out is agents that learn their own models and simulators from their own experience, rather than humans building the simulator.

- **金句**："我不奇怪，是这个领域奇怪。"——在 Sutton 看来，"持续学习"本就是学习的常态，需要给它单独起名字的，是被 AI 热潮带偏的整个领域。
  / **Memorable quote**: *"I'm not weird. The field is weird."* — to Sutton, continual learning is just what learning normally is; the field that felt the need to give it a special name is the one distorted by the AI craze.
