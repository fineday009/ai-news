## 2. modiqo/waggle

⭐ 815（自创建约 10 天 +815）· Rust · [链接](https://github.com/modiqo/waggle)

**是什么**：面向 Agent 交接的「可归属、可解析」制品引用——用一个约 30 字节的 token 代替粘贴整段上下文，MCP 原生，定位为 Agent-harness 世界的引用层。

**What it is**: Attributed, resolvable artifact references for agent handoffs — a ~30-byte token instead of pasted context. MCP-native; the reference layer for the agent-harness world.

**为什么值得关注**：多 Agent 协作最脏的活是上下文传递，粘来粘去既贵又易错。把「引用」标准化成短 token，是让 Agent 之间像函数调用一样交接的基础设施尝试，方向比大多数 wrapper 更底层。

**Why it matters**: The dirtiest part of multi-agent work is passing context around — copy-pasting is expensive and error-prone. Standardizing a "reference" into a short token is an infrastructure bet on making agent-to-agent handoffs feel like function calls, and it sits lower in the stack than most wrappers.

### 🎧 播客 / Podcast

**Unsupervised Learning · Ep 91：顶级 AI 分析师拆解当下的 AI 炒作周期（嘉宾 Benedict Evans，主持 Redpoint 的 Jacob Effron）**

**Unsupervised Learning · Ep 91: Top AI Analyst Unpacks Today's AI Hype Cycle (guest Benedict Evans, host Jacob Effron of Redpoint)**

> 核心观点：我们正处在「彻底不确定」的阶段——与其套用「这就像电力/核武器」的宏大隐喻，不如先动手造东西、看什么真的有用。
>
> The Takeaway: we're in a phase of radical uncertainty — instead of reaching for grand "it's like electricity / nuclear weapons" metaphors, build things and find out what actually works.

- **专业服务的「消费者剩余」悖论**：AI 让律师、会计一天能找 20 个判例而不是 2 个，但对手方也一样，账单工时、交付页数、胜诉概率都没变——多做了大量工作，结果却相同。价值不会自动兑现。
  The consumer-surplus paradox in professional services: AI lets a lawyer or accountant surface 20 precedents instead of 2 — but so does the other side. Billed hours, pages filed and odds of winning stay the same. A lot more work, same result; value doesn't auto-realize.

- **企业采用极慢，别用 demo 速度线性外推**：Goldman 的 CIO 调查显示，二十年后企业工作流上云比例也才 ~30%。「这需要时间，又难又复杂，人们还有别的优先级。」
  Enterprise adoption is slow — don't extrapolate from demo speed. Goldman's CIO survey shows only ~30% of enterprise workflows in the cloud after twenty years. "It takes time and it's hard and complicated and people have other priorities."

- **「重新想象」本身就是个大项目**：真正的价值来自重构工作流，而这正是过去叫「数字化转型」、如今叫「企业转型」的那件难事——没有哪家公司凭空拥有全部技能坐等改造。
  "Reimagining" is itself a big, hard project. Real value comes from redesigning workflows — the difficult thing once called "digital transformation" and now "enterprise transformation." No company just has all those skills sitting around ready.

- **默认一部分当下的押注会失败**：就像 1995、2005 年很多技术最终没成，应该预设现在做的一些东西不会 work——OpenAI 上周放弃了网页浏览器，MCP 也可能被别的东西取代。
  Assume some of today's bets will fail. Just as many technologies from 1995 and 2005 didn't pan out, presume some current work won't either — OpenAI just gave up on the web browser last week, and MCP may well be replaced by something else.
