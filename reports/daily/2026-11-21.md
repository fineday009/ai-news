## 1. haziqahmedqasmi/PeakCut

⭐ 1,555 · Python · 创建于 2026-11-14 · [链接](https://github.com/haziqahmedqasmi/PeakCut)

**是什么**：面向长视频和直播的 AI 切片工具，自托管，自带 AI key。

**What it is**: AI clipping for long videos and live streams. Self-hosted, bring your own AI key.

**为什么值得关注**：贴一个 YouTube、Twitch 或 Kick 链接（或上传文件），它会转写音频、让 AI 挑出值得剪的片段，裁成 9:16 并加逐词字幕和开场钩子，还能排队或自动发布到已连接的 YouTube、Instagram、TikTok 账号。差异点是直播切片：把直播录进滚动缓冲区，结合响度、弹幕活跃度和转写内容边播边剪。模型可选 Gemini、OpenAI、Claude、Ollama 本地模型等，不填 key 也能按响度挑片段，适合帮创作者做切片的个人和小团队。

**Why it matters**: Paste a YouTube, Twitch or Kick link (or upload a file) and it transcribes the audio, asks an AI which moments are worth clipping, cuts them to 9:16 with word-by-word captions and a hook, and can queue or auto-post to connected YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. The differentiator is live clipping: it records a stream into a rolling buffer and clips as it happens using loudness, chat activity and the transcript. It works with Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, a local model through Ollama and more—and with no key at all it picks clips by loudness. A fit for individuals and small teams clipping for creators.

## 2. iamajmeena/neonclipping

⭐ 1,276 · Python · 创建于 2026-11-19 · [链接](https://github.com/iamajmeena/neonclipping)

**是什么**：贴一个链接，约 2 分钟得到带字幕的 9:16 短片。免费、本地运行的开源切片引擎（音频优先、YouTube 字幕、本地 LLM）。

**What it is**: Paste a link, get captioned 9:16 clips in ~2 min. Free, local, open-source clipping engine (audio-first, YouTube captions, local LLM).

**为什么值得关注**：创建仅两天就有 1,276 星。它的快来自"绝不看完整视频"：只下载音频，直接用 YouTube 自带的逐词字幕当转写（其他来源才回退到本地 Whisper），按响度突变和抬高的嗓音找候选片段，再让本地 LLM（Ollama）打分并写标题和钩子，最后只下载被选中的片段渲染成 1080x1920。全程在本机完成，不需要账号、订阅或上传；与同日上榜的 PeakCut 相比，它更偏"零云端、零费用"。

**Why it matters**: 1,276 stars two days after creation. Its speed comes from never looking at the whole video: it downloads audio only, takes the transcript from YouTube's own word-timed captions (falling back to local Whisper for other sources), finds candidates from sudden loudness jumps and raised voices, has a local LLM (Ollama) score each moment and write a title and hook, then downloads only the chosen sections and renders 1080x1920 clips. Everything runs on your machine with no account, subscription or upload; next to PeakCut on today's list, it leans toward zero cloud and zero cost.

## 7. joshpuckett/allo

⭐ 1,256 · Python · 创建于 2026-11-15 · [链接](https://github.com/joshpuckett/allo)

**是什么**：Allo 是一款亲切友好的字体，免费且开源。

**What it is**: Allo is your friendly neighborhood typeface, free and open source.

**为什么值得关注**：这是一款为屏幕和界面优化的可变字体，字重和圆润度都可调，共三个可变轴，光学尺寸会在正文与标题之间自动调整。细节很足：等宽数字、分数、每个字重都有真正的斜体，还有不少替换字形。发布包含 18 款静态 OTF、可变字体和 WOFF2 网页字体，也可以通过 npm 的 `allo-font` 安装，适合想给产品换一套有性格又不失清晰的界面字体的设计师和前端。

**Why it matters**: A variable typeface optimized for screens and interfaces, adjustable in weight and roundness across three variable axes, with an optical size that shifts automatically between text and display. It's rich in detail: tabular figures, fractions, a true oblique in every weight and plenty of alternates. Releases include 18 static OTFs, the variable font and WOFF2 webfonts, and it installs from npm as `allo-font`—a fit for designers and frontend developers who want an interface font with personality that stays clear.

## 8. jhaizhou-ops/S2Arm

⭐ 3,736 · C# · 创建于 2026-11-08 · [链接](https://github.com/jhaizhou-ops/S2Arm)

**是什么**：把你自己的 Switch 游戏原生带到 iPhone / iPad / Mac：游戏代码被重写为 ARM64，图形直接走 Metal，一个应用管理整个游戏库。需自备游戏和密钥。

**What it is**: Run your own Switch games natively on iPhone, iPad and Mac: game code rewritten to ARM64, graphics direct to Metal, one app for the whole library. Bring your own games and keys.

**为什么值得关注**：它走的不是模拟器路线，而是把游戏代码改写成原生 ARM64 直接跑在设备处理器上。0.6.0 起手机上只需装一个 S2Arm 应用，游戏通过 Mac 或 Windows 启动器逐个发送，更新应用不必重装游戏、存档保留，还加入了随时保存的快照功能。项目采用 Apache-2.0 协议，支持 iOS / iPadOS 18 及以上和 Apple silicon Mac，游戏文件需要用户自备。

**Why it matters**: Rather than emulating, it rewrites game code to native ARM64 that runs directly on the device's processor. From 0.6.0 there's a single S2Arm app on your phone; games are sent one by one from a Mac or Windows launcher, updating the app never means reinstalling games and saves stay, and snapshots let you save at any moment. It's Apache-2.0 licensed, supports iOS / iPadOS 18 and later and Apple silicon Macs, and you bring your own games.

### 播客 · No Priors：[Superintelligence Needs the Physical World with CEO and Co-Founder of Arda Bob McGrew](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_wLzJINt9iQ)

**一句话**：把研究员自动化不会带来失控式的自我加速，因为瓶颈从来不是研究员，而是实验和物理世界——超级智能要走出数据中心，就得先改造制造业。

**The takeaway**: Automating researchers won't set off runaway self-improvement, because researchers were never the bottleneck—experiments and the physical world are. For superintelligence to leave the data center, manufacturing has to change first.

- **嘉宾**：Bob McGrew，Arda 的 CEO 兼联合创始人，曾是 Palantir 早期核心高管和 OpenAI 首席研究官。他说离开 OpenAI 时唯一确定的是不想再办一家前沿实验室。
- **Guest**: Bob McGrew, CEO and co-founder of Arda, previously an early key exec at Palantir and chief research officer at OpenAI. Leaving OpenAI, the one thing he knew was that he didn't want to start another frontier lab.
- **"硬起飞"论证是错的**：他说 2024 年在 OpenAI 时，真正的限制不是能招到多少研究员，而是手头有多少 GPU。自动化研究是一次很大的一次性收益，类似从没有推理的模型到有推理和工具的模型，但它只是又一条 S 曲线，"不是 RSI"。当假设变得便宜，稀缺的就是物理的部分；他认为连深度学习本质上也是一门实验科学。
- **The hard-takeoff argument is wrong**: At OpenAI in 2024, he says, the limiting factor wasn't how many researchers they could hire but how many GPUs were on hand. Automated research is a big one-time gain, comparable to going from models without reasoning to models with reasoning and tools, but it's one more S-curve—"it's not RSI." When hypotheses become cheap, the physical part becomes scarce; even deep learning, he argues, is really an experimental science.
- **美国制造的症结是自动化不够灵活**：他说 iPhone 每年约 75% 的零件会变，只有约 25% 的工序是高度自动化的，其余靠手工组装，因为今天的自动化是只解决一个具体问题的定制机器。Arda 的目标是让你把产品设计上传到云端，在七天内设计并最终重新配置一条自动化产线。他认为这不需要人形机器人："两条机械臂加一个摄像头就够了"，很多时候一条就够。
- **Inflexible automation is what holds back US manufacturing**: He says about 75% of an iPhone's parts change each year, so only about 25% of the line is highly automated and the rest is assembled by hand, because today's automation is a bespoke machine that solves one physical problem. Arda's goal is to let you upload a product design to its cloud, then design and ultimately reconfigure an automated line in seven days. No humanoids required: "two arms and a camera is enough," and often one arm will do.
- **先做"制造工程师的 Cursor"**：过去一年 Arda 与 Anduril 合作，搭建让制造工程师设计产线的软件与 AI 平台。第一位用户为一个执行器做虚拟装配，原本要几天，用它 90 分钟完成；现在已部署到对方所有产品部门，从无人机、导弹到 Fury 协同作战飞机。Arda 不自己训练模型——前沿模型更适合当编排者，调用运动规划、CAD 检查等专用工具。
- **Start as "Cursor for manufacturing engineers"**: Arda spent the past year partnered with Anduril on a software and AI platform for manufacturing engineers to design lines. Its first user did the virtual build for an actuator in ninety minutes instead of a few days, and it's now deployed across all of their product divisions, from drones and missiles to the Fury collaborative combat aircraft. Arda doesn't train its own models: frontier models work best as an orchestrator over specialized tools such as motion planning and CAD inspection.
- **FDE 是产品策略，不是外包顾问**：他说没有准备好使用某个功能的客户，他就不会开发这个功能；除了出资的"远见者"，你还需要一个愿意陪你熬过痛苦的重度用户。如果前线工程师一直在填补产品与用户之间的缺口，就没有杠杆——必须把他们看到的东西做回产品里。
- **FDE is a product strategy, not consulting**: He wouldn't develop a feature unless a customer was ready to use it, and beyond the visionary sponsor you need a power user willing to fight through the pain with you. If forward-deployed engineers keep filling the gap between product and user, you get no leverage—what they see has to be baked back into the product.
