## 1. KKKKhazix/agent-autoloop

⭐ 5,144 · TypeScript · 创建于 2026-11-03 · [链接](https://github.com/KKKKhazix/agent-autoloop)

**是什么**：让你的命令行 Agent 在后台自己接活、自己跟进到底；平时不烦你，遇到拿不准的事才发卡片找你拍板。

**What it is**: Lets your command-line agent pick up work in the background and follow it through on its own—staying quiet until something needs your call, then sending you a card. (Translated from Chinese.)

**为什么值得关注**：11 月 5 日上日报时 2,674 星，如今已达 5,144，一周多几乎翻倍。它把 Claude Code、Codex 这类"人守着终端用"的 Agent 变成后台常驻的同事：内核约 1800 行、零运行时依赖，事件源、Agent、通知通道（飞书、钉钉、企微、Slack、Discord）都是插件。设计上很讲究——等你拍板时进程直接退出、不耗 token；状态全部来自只追加日志；Issue 等外部文字只当资料不拼进提示词，防注入；Agent 互相派活超过 8 层自动熔断。

**Why it matters**: It had 2,674 stars when it made the daily on November 5 and has 5,144 now—nearly doubling in just over a week. It turns "babysat-in-a-terminal" agents like Claude Code and Codex into background teammates: a ~1,800-line kernel with zero runtime dependencies, with event sources, agents and notification channels (Feishu, DingTalk, WeCom, Slack, Discord) as plugins. The design is careful—processes exit while waiting for you, so no tokens burn; all state comes from an append-only log; external text like issue bodies is passed as data, never spliced into prompts, to resist injection; and agent-to-agent hand-offs trip a breaker after 8 hops.

## 2. alibaba/open-code-review

⭐ 50.7k（今日 +215）· Go · [链接](https://github.com/alibaba/open-code-review)

**是什么**：在阿里规模下久经考验的混合架构代码审查工具：确定性流水线 + LLM Agent，精确到行的评论，内置多语言规则集（空指针、线程安全、XSS、SQL 注入），兼容 OpenAI 与 Anthropic 接口。

**What it is**: Secure, fast, efficient, battle-tested at Alibaba's scale. Hybrid architecture code review tool: deterministic pipelines + LLM Agent, precise line-level comments, built-in multi-language ruleset (NPE, thread-safety, XSS, SQL injection), OpenAI & Anthropic compatible.

**为什么值得关注**：它不是纯靠模型"看一眼就评"，而是先跑规则化流水线、再交给 Agent 做语义级判断，并直接产出行级评论，工程化程度高、可落地到 CI。10 月 10 日上日报时 4.53 万星，如今已过 5 万，今日又回到 Trending。在今天 Agent 写代码越来越多的背景下，"可复核的 AI 审查"是对想自建审查体系的团队最实用的参考之一。

**Why it matters**: It doesn't just let a model eyeball a diff—it runs a rule-based pipeline first, then hands off to an agent for semantic judgment, emitting line-level comments; it's engineering-heavy and CI-ready. It had 45.3k stars when it made the daily on October 10, has now passed 50k, and is back on Trending today. As agents write more and more code, reviewable AI review is one of the most practical references for teams building their own review stack.

## 3. voyager-so/vork

⭐ 1,521 · Rust · 创建于 2026-11-05 · [链接](https://github.com/voyager-so/vork)

**是什么**：为 AI Agent 打造的无头 3D 与视觉特效引擎（研究预览版）：Agent 用代码写出整个场景，包括自己的着色器、材质、相机和物理，由 vork 在 GPU 上渲染。

**What it is**: Headless 3D and VFX engine for agents (research preview). Agents write the whole scene as code—including their own shaders, materials, cameras and physics—and vork renders it on the GPU.

**为什么值得关注**：核心 API 只有约 2,500 token，Agent 读完 `vork help` 就能上手，还能用自己的 GPU 程序扩展。用 Rust 和 Slang 编写，跑在 Vulkan 与 Metal 上（Linux、Windows、macOS），也可以交给 Voyager 的云 GPU 渲染。官方示例里 100 万只鸟各走各的轨迹，1080p 192 帧在单张 NVIDIA L4 上 97 秒渲完，并称默认场景比 Blender EEVEE 快 1.1–2.3 倍（README 附有测量说明与限定条件）。它把"Agent 会写代码"延伸到了"Agent 会做 3D 镜头"。

**Why it matters**: The core API fits in about 2,500 tokens, so an agent can get going after reading `vork help`, and it can extend the engine with its own GPU programs. Written in Rust and Slang on Vulkan and Metal (Linux, Windows, macOS), with an option to render on Voyager's cloud GPUs. Its showcase renders a million birds, each on its own path—192 frames at 1080p in 97 seconds on a single NVIDIA L4—and it claims 1.1–2.3× EEVEE's speed on default scenes (the README details how it measured and the qualifications). It stretches "agents can write code" into "agents can make 3D shots."

- **Experimental accuracy as the bar**: Every internal leaderboard carries a dotted line marking experimental accuracy; the goal is AlphaFold-style predictive models good enough to do most design iteration on a computer and go to the real world mainly to validate. In his words: "I wanna get out of the real world."
- **动物实验只是弱代理**：他引用的研究称，大鼠实验对人体临床毒性的预测力只有约 30%；他希望把体外指标、类器官/器官芯片与 AI 模型结合，直接建模人体临床毒性。数据方面，他们发现被动积累的历史数据偏差大，自己生成数据反而看到了很好的数据规模定律。
- **Animal models are weak proxies**: By the studies he cites, rats are only about 30% predictive of human clinical toxicity; he wants to combine in-vitro endpoints, organoids and organs-on-a-chip with AI models to model human toxicity directly. On data, passively accumulated historical datasets are heavily biased, so they generate their own—and see strong data scaling laws.
- **更安全的分子才能做"鸡尾酒"疗法**：联合用药难，往往是因为单个分子有毒性或药物相互作用；如果能把分子设计得足够精准安全，就能组合出同时调控多个靶点的复杂疗法。Isomorphic 不对外出售模型，而是自研管线（肿瘤、免疫与炎症）并与药企合作，正在组建临床开发团队，但未给出首个临床试验的时间。
- **Safer molecules unlock combination therapy**: Combination treatments are hard because individual molecules carry toxicity or drug–drug interactions; precisely engineered, safer molecules could be combined to modulate a whole set of targets at once. Isomorphic doesn't sell or license its models—it runs its own pipeline (oncology, immunology and inflammation) plus pharma partnerships, and is building a clinical development team, without giving a date for its first trial.
