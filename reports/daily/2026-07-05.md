## 6. HUANGCHIHHUNGLeo/claude-real-video

⭐ 755（近期新建，6/30）· Python · [链接](https://github.com/HUANGCHIHHUNGLeo/claude-real-video)

**是什么**：让 Claude（或任意 LLM）真正「看」视频——基于场景变化检测抽取去重关键帧，配上转写文本，本地运行，MIT 协议。

**What it is**: Lets Claude (or any LLM) actually watch video — scene-aware, deduplicated keyframes plus a transcript, from a URL or local file, running locally under MIT.

**为什么值得关注**：常见「视频转 LLM」方案要么固定间隔抽帧（漏掉快切）、要么过采样静止画面。它按场景变化抽帧，既省 token 又不漏关键镜头，处理后内容默认留在本地。一个小而实用、直击痛点的工具，还上了 Hacker News 头条。

**Why it matters**: Common video-to-LLM approaches either sample at fixed intervals (missing fast cuts) or over-sample static footage. This captures frames on scene change — saving tokens without missing key shots — and keeps output local by default. A small, sharp tool that hits a real pain point, and it made the Hacker News front page.

## 9. AlexandrosGounis/pdfx

⭐ 706（近期新建，6/24）· TypeScript · [链接](https://github.com/AlexandrosGounis/pdfx)

**是什么**：对传统 PDF 标准的扩展，通过元数据把多个文件塞进「一个仍然是 PDF」的文件里。口号是「一个文件，多个文档，仍是 PDF」。

**What it is**: An extension of the traditional PDF standard that packs multiple documents into a single file via metadata — "One file. Many documents. Still a PDF."

**为什么值得关注**：合并后的文件在任何 PDF 阅读器里都能正常打开，但用 PDFx 应用可拆回独立文档。这是对一个日常格式的巧妙增强，兼容性无损却解决了「一堆 PDF 难以打包分享」的老问题。跨平台且 MIT 开源。

**Why it matters**: The combined file opens normally in any PDF reader, yet the PDFx app can split it back into separate documents. A clever, backward-compatible enhancement to an everyday format that solves the old headache of bundling and sharing many PDFs. Cross-platform and MIT-licensed.

## 🎙️ Builders 动态 / What Builders Are Saying

**Box CEO Aaron Levie**：AI 的下一场战争是「上下文之战」。他认为 Agent 的效果取决于是否拥有正确的领域知识、上下文和工具，因此「应用层」远不止是套壳——能以受治理的方式组织关键知识、在多个模型间路由任务（前沿模型做规划/审查，低成本模型做量产）、并做深工作流变更管理的公司，才会拥有最深的护城河。

Box CEO Aaron Levie argues the next battle in AI is a battle for context: an agent's effectiveness comes down to having the right domain expertise, context, and tools, which is why the applied layer is far more than an "LLM wrapper." The companies that govern critical knowledge, route work across models (frontier for planning/review, cheaper models for volume), and own the workflow change management end-to-end will have the deepest moats.

[原推 / Source](https://x.com/levie/status/2073138135014502777)

**Builder Zara Zhang**：人们越来越不愿意为「工具」付费——因为他们觉得自己用编码 Agent 就能造一个。真正愿意买单的，是「雇佣自己不具备的专业能力」那种感觉。

Builder Zara Zhang observes that people are increasingly unwilling to pay for tools, since they figure they can just build one themselves with coding agents. What they'll still pay for is the feeling of hiring expertise they don't have.

[原推 / Source](https://x.com/zarazhangrui/status/2073295900395606401)

**Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch**：Agent 的自我改进正在成为现实——给 Agent 自省过去运行记录的能力，让它发现低效、错误和冗余的工具调用，进而生成新的 prompt 和 skill。这也是他强调「部署时内建 Agent 可观测性」的原因。

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch pitches agentic self-improvement: give an agent the ability to introspect its past runs, spot inefficiencies, errors, and redundant tool calls, then generate new prompts and skills — which is why he argues agent observability should be built in at deploy time.

FPV Ventures partner Nikunj Kothari — often a vocal critic of Gemini's product experience — concedes it's still the only place where a single API key does it all: Flash for cheap fast long-context structured tasks, Nano Banana for world-class images, search with grounding, real-time audio/video, and more, letting many of his BYOK side projects collapse down to "just bring a Gemini key."

### 播客 / Podcast

**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck —《Why NVIDIA Is Giving Away AI Models》嘉宾 Bryan Catanzaro（NVIDIA Nemotron 负责人）**

- **为什么免费送模型**：Catanzaro 直言这是纯粹的好生意——「只要 AI 被更多地开发和部署，就是我们业务的机会」。NVIDIA 不追求成为唯一的开源提供者，而是通过繁荣整个生态来卖出更多算力。

  On why NVIDIA gives models away: Catanzaro is blunt that it's simply good business — "whenever AI is further developed and further deployed, it's an opportunity for our business." NVIDIA isn't trying to be the only open provider; it grows the whole ecosystem to sell more compute.

- **效率即智能**：他抛出的核心观点是，如果我们已在算力极限上运行，那么「获取更多智能的方式就是变得更高效」，而不是一味加码算力。人类造工具、造「外部器官」来解决问题，如今在造的是「外部大脑」。

  Efficiency is intelligence: his core claim is that if we're already running at the compute limit, "the way to get more intelligence is to be more efficient," not to apply more force. Humans build tools and "external organs" to solve problems; now we're building an external brain.

- **开源与闭源的差距被高估了**：与其纠结开闭源谁领先，不如看整个领域「过去三个月」的进步之快——Nemotron 3 Ultra 发布即成美国第一的开源权重模型，GLM 5.2 紧随其后。

  The open-vs-closed gap is overrated: rather than fixate on who leads, look at how fast the whole field moved in just the past three months — Nemotron 3 Ultra shipped as the #1 US open-weights model, with GLM 5.2 right behind.

- **Nemotron 联盟**：今年 3 月成立，理念是「在模型造出来之前就和伙伴一起做」，把他们的基准、环境和反馈提前纳入，而非发布后才让大家试用；且是非排他的开放联盟。

  The Nemotron Coalition (formed in March) is built on collaborating with partners before the model is built — folding in their benchmarks, environments, and feedback up front rather than dropping a finished model and hoping — and it's explicitly non-exclusive.

- **模型家族**：Nano（300 亿总参/30 亿激活）、Super（1200 亿/120 亿）、Ultra（5500 亿/550 亿）覆盖小中大部署场景，Super 因兼顾成本与智能而最受欢迎；技术上用了 4-bit 训练、混合 Mamba-Transformer 架构、MoE、多 token 预测与多教师蒸馏。

  The model family — Nano (30B total/3B active), Super (120B/12B), Ultra (550B/55B) — spans small/medium/large deployments, with Super the most popular for balancing cost and intelligence; technically it uses 4-bit training, a hybrid Mamba-Transformer architecture, MoE, multi-token prediction, and multi-teacher distillation.
