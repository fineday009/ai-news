  这期与 Anthropic Platform 团队的对谈，核心是"做生态而非围墙花园"的平台哲学。要点：

  This conversation with the Anthropic Platform team centers on a "build an ecosystem, not a walled garden" philosophy. Key points:

  1. Platform 同时有内外两个"北极星"：对内为团队提供最大杠杆与速度去做"AGI-pilled"产品，对外则让任何 builder 都能用 Claude 造出想要的东西。/ Platform serves two "north stars": internally, maximum leverage and speed for teams shipping "AGI-pilled" products; externally, letting any builder create whatever they want with Claude.

  2. Skills 与 MCP 这类标准，最初是"因为 Claude 自己需要"才做，做好后直接开放给整个生态——这正是"生态而非围墙"的体现。/ Standards like Skills and MCP were built "because Claude itself needs them," then handed to the whole ecosystem — the essence of ecosystem-over-walled-garden.

  3. 团队用"分层蛋糕"来理解栈：知识 → 执行 → 协调；他们正从执行层向上走到"协调层"，用"strategies / meta-harness"编排不同用途的 token（有的负责建议、有的负责执行）。/ They frame the stack as a layered cake — knowledge → execution → coordination — and are moving up toward a "coordination layer," orchestrating tokens with different jobs (some advising, some executing) via "strategies / a meta-harness."

  4. 形态一直在变：两年前"一切皆聊天"，如今"一切皆 agent"，未来还会有新形态；与其押注单一形态，不如建一个够稳的平台让大家自己去探索。/ Form factors keep shifting: two years ago "everything's chat," now "everything's agents," with more to come — so rather than betting on one form factor, build a robust platform for everyone to explore on.
