## 1. google-research/timesfm

⭐ 30.7k（今日 +1,618）· Python · [链接](https://github.com/google-research/timesfm)

**是什么**：Google Research 出品的时间序列预测基础模型（TimesFM），开箱即用做通用时序预测。

**What it is**: TimesFM time-series foundation model for forecasting, from Google Research.

**为什么值得关注**：时序预测长期是「一个数据集训一个模型」的碎片化领域，TimesFM 把「基础模型 + 零样本预测」范式带了进来，一套预训练权重就能覆盖多种频率与领域。今天单日涨 1,600+ 星，说明工程界对「不用再为每条曲线单独建模」有真实需求。

**Why it matters**: Forecasting has long been fragmented — one bespoke model per dataset. TimesFM brings the "foundation model + zero-shot" paradigm to time series, so a single set of pretrained weights covers many frequencies and domains. Gaining 1,600+ stars in a day signals real demand for not having to hand-build a model for every curve.

### 🎧 播客 / Podcast

**《How a Professional Writer Writes With AI》— AI & I by Every（主持 Dan Shipper）**

嘉宾 Katie Parrott 是 Every 的专职作者，由其 AI 咨询主管 Natalia Quintero 访谈，围绕新插件「Compound Writing」聊「专业写作者到底怎么和 AI 协作」。要点：

Guest Katie Parrott is a staff writer at Every, interviewed by head of AI consulting Natalia Quintero, discussing the new "Compound Writing" plugin and how a professional writer actually works with AI. Key points:

- **上下文工程是产量的真正杠杆**：她能月产惊人内容量，靠的不是让 AI 凭空写，而是先把品牌信息、产品细节、受众画像等「地基」喂足——「你在前期投入越多，后面就跑得越快」。

- **Context engineering is the real lever for output**: her prolific volume comes not from asking AI to write from scratch but from feeding it a foundation — brand messaging, product specifics, audience personas. "The more you put in that work upfront, the easier the process gets farther down the line."

- **先搭数据与框架，再谈遣词造句**：受众画像、痛点、差异化这些「构成性要素」要先就位，之后才轮到「用这些词、避开那些词、控制阅读难度和语气」。

- **Data and frameworks first, diction later**: audience personas, pain points, and differentiators must be in place before you ever get to "use these words, avoid those, target this reading level and tone."

- **人类的价值在「最后一公里」**：AI 有知识截止日期，永远落后现实几个月；人类要补上的是模型拿不到的真实世界经验、独家研究与个人洞察——「输入才是让写作变得独一无二的东西」。

- **The human edge is the "last mile"**: AI has a knowledge cutoff and is always months out of date; the human closes that gap with real-world experience, proprietary research, and personal insight the model can't have. "Inputs are really what makes the writing unique."
