## 4. ogulcancelik/herdr

⭐ 21.5k（本月 +13,835）· Rust · [链接](https://github.com/ogulcancelik/herdr)

**是什么**：一个住在终端里的"智能体多路复用器"，用于同时启动、切换和管理多个编码 Agent。

**What it is**: An agent multiplexer that lives in your terminal, for launching, switching between, and managing multiple coding agents at once.

**为什么值得关注**：随着并行跑多个编码 Agent 成为常态，"编排/观察多个会话"本身成了痛点；Rust 实现轻快，把 tmux 式的复用体验带给 AI 工作流。

**Why it matters**: As running several coding agents in parallel becomes normal, orchestrating and watching multiple sessions is itself a pain point; the lightweight Rust implementation brings a tmux-style multiplexing experience to AI workflows.

### 播客要点 / Podcast

**《The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck》——OpenAI's Compute Chief: We Can't Build Fast Enough（嘉宾：Sachin Katti，OpenAI 工业级算力负责人，前 Intel CTO）**

- 需求远超算力供给：任何上线的算力都会被立刻用光，OpenAI 今年算力开支约 500 亿美元，全行业约 7000 亿美元，最大的担忧仍是"建得不够快"。

- 这被视为人类史上最大的基础设施建设，"比高速公路、比铁路都大"；决策速度被逼到极限，过去在 Intel 要花几个月的决定，现在几天就要拍板。

- 瓶颈在电力：当下主要靠燃气轮机（美国最密集、可运输、供应广的能源），但受供应链掣肘，核电重新回到讨论桌上。

- 液冷已成标配：既要冷却整个数据大厅，也要在芯片级散热，连芯片之间的互联也要冷却。

- 自研芯片与"递归"：OpenAI 正推进代号 Jalapeño 的定制硅片，而 AI 已经开始参与设计自己的芯片——"AI 设计训练和运行下一代 AI 所需系统的那个世界，已经不远了。"

**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — OpenAI's Compute Chief: We Can't Build Fast Enough (guest: Sachin Katti, OpenAI's head of industrial compute, ex-Intel CTO)**

- Demand far outstrips compute supply: anything brought online is consumed immediately; OpenAI's compute spend is ~$50B this year against a ~$700B industry, and the biggest worry is still not building fast enough.

- It's described as the largest infrastructure buildout in human history — "bigger than the highway, bigger than railroads" — with decisions that once took months at Intel now made in days.

- Power is the bottleneck: today it's gas turbines (the densest, most transportable, most available US energy), constrained by the supply chain, which is bringing nuclear back into the conversation.

- Liquid cooling is now standard: cooling the data halls, the chips individually, and even the interconnects between chips.

- Custom silicon and "recursion": OpenAI is pushing a custom chip codenamed Jalapeño, and AI is already helping design its own chips — "the world of recursion is not that far, where AI will design the systems it needs to train and run the next generation of AI."
