## 1. sapientinc/PRAXIST

⭐ 6.5k · Python · 8/27 创建 · [链接](https://github.com/sapientinc/PRAXIST)

**是什么**：面向「可度量、可运行」研究的自主研究系统，把研究当成持续、以证据为驱动的过程，让多个研究 Agent 并行探索相互竞争的假设，并跨「世代」自动综合结论、逼近最优解。

**What it is**: An autonomous research system for measurable, computer-executable research. It treats research as a continuous, evidence-driven process—running multiple agents in parallel to explore competing hypotheses, then synthesizing findings across successive generations to converge on the best solution.

**为什么值得关注**：出自做过 HRM（分层推理模型）的 Sapient，路子不是「一次跑一个实验」，而是把评测协议、证据留存、代际综合都编排进闭环，适合目标明确可跑分、但最优路径未知的课题。上线一周多即破 6k star。

**Why it matters**: From Sapient (of Hierarchical Reasoning Model fame), it goes beyond one-off experiments—orchestrating task-owned evaluation protocols, durable evidence, and generation-to-generation synthesis into a closed loop. Ideal when the objective is measurable and runnable but the optimal path is unknown. Over 6k stars in a bit more than a week.

## 2. XiaoDuoYa/codex-with-chatgpt

⭐ 2.7k · TypeScript · 8/28 创建 · [链接](https://github.com/XiaoDuoYa/codex-with-chatgpt)

**是什么**：让 ChatGPT 当「规划大脑」、Codex 当「执行手」的协作方案——用对话模型做任务拆解与推理，把落地执行交给 Codex 的 Agent 框架。

**What it is**: "ChatGPT thinks, Codex works." It uses ChatGPT as the planning brain for reasoning and task decomposition while keeping the Codex harness to actually carry the work out.

**为什么值得关注**：抓住了当下一个真实痛点——单个 Agent 既要想又要做常常顾此失彼；把「规划」和「执行」拆到两套模型，让各自扬长，是很多人正在手搓的模式，这里给了现成方案。

**Why it matters**: It targets a real pain point—one agent that both plans and executes tends to do neither well. Splitting "planning" and "execution" across two models to play to each one's strengths is a pattern many are hand-rolling; this packages it up.

**Why it matters**: DLSS 5's neural rendering is one of the year's biggest selling points in game graphics, yet hardware-locked to NVIDIA.
