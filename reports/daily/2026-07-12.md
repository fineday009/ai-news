## 1. JustVugg/colibri

⭐ 4.1k · 新建于 07-01 · C · [链接](https://github.com/JustVugg/colibri)

**是什么**：一个纯 C、零依赖的推理引擎，通过把 MoE 专家权重从磁盘流式加载，让 744B 参数的 GLM-5.2 跑在约 25GB 内存的消费级机器上。

**What it is**: A pure-C, zero-dependency inference engine that runs the 744B-parameter GLM-5.2 on a ~25GB-RAM consumer machine by streaming MoE expert weights from disk instead of loading everything into memory.

**为什么值得关注**：它抓住了 MoE 每个 token 只激活约 40B 参数的特性——稠密层常驻内存、路由专家按需从存储读取，并配合 int8 MTP 头的推测解码把前向利用率拉到每步 2.2–2.8 token。对没有显卡集群、又想在本地跑前沿模型的人，这是一条真实可用的路径（已提供交互聊天与 OpenAI 兼容 API）。

**Why it matters**: It exploits the fact that MoE models activate only ~40B params per token — dense layers stay resident while routed experts are cached from disk — and pairs this with an int8 MTP speculative-decoding head reaching 2.2–2.8 tokens per forward pass. For anyone without a GPU cluster who still wants frontier models locally, this is a genuinely working path (interactive chat plus an OpenAI-compatible API are included).

**播客要点 / Podcast**

**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck —《Stripe's AI Chief: How AI Agents Will Buy, Sell, and Pay》**（嘉宾：Stripe 数据与 AI 负责人 Emily Sense）

**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — "Stripe's AI Chief: How AI Agents Will Buy, Sell, and Pay"** (guest: Emily Sense, Head of Data & AI at Stripe)

一句话要点：AI 时代最被低估的风险不是钱被偷，而是「token 被偷」——当 agent 开始替你买卖，整套支付基础设施都得重建。

- **Token 盗窃是最被忽视的话题**：Emily 说骗子发现不必偷钱或凭证，直接偷 token 即可，规模之大让她震惊——「AI 公司超过六分之一的注册是这类滥用」，她称之为「token 版的吃霸王餐」。
- **Agentic 商务是一条光谱**：一端是 agent 全自主发现服务、自主下单（Stripe 的机器支付协议 MPP）；另一端是人在 AI 界面里搜索、AI 给出带「购买」按钮的答案。Stripe 已与 Google、微软/OpenAI、Meta 合作，把商品直接接进 AI Mode、Copilot/ChatGPT 与广告结账。
- **按席位（per-seat）定价正在瓦解**：当干活的是 agent 而不是「座位上的人」，按人头收费的模式失效，token 货币化成为新命题。
- **终局是 agent 自己经营业务**：真正要谈的不是「Emily 授权 agent 替她买东西」，而是「Emily 有一个被指派去经营一门生意的 agent，它要自己买、自己卖、自己赚利润」——这是她希望一年后能讨论的世界。

One-line takeaway: The most underrated risk of the AI era isn't stolen money — it's stolen tokens; as agents start buying and selling for us, the entire payments stack has to be rebuilt.

- **Token theft is the most under-discussed topic in AI**: Emily notes fraudsters realized they needn't steal money or credentials — just tokens — and the scale shocked her: "more than one in six signups at AI companies are this kind of abuse," a "dine-and-dash, but for tokens."
- **Agentic commerce is a spectrum**: at one end, agents autonomously discover services and transact with no human in the loop (Stripe's Machine Payments Protocol); at the other, people search inside an AI surface and the answer arrives with a buy button. Stripe now partners with Google, Microsoft/OpenAI and Meta to make products purchasable inside AI Mode, Copilot/ChatGPT and ads.
- **Per-seat pricing is breaking**: when the worker is an agent rather than a person in a seat, charging per head stops making sense — token monetization becomes the new question.
- **The endgame is agents running businesses**: the real story isn't "Emily permissions an agent to buy for her" but "Emily has an agent tasked with running a business — buying, selling and turning a profit" — the world she hopes to be discussing a year from now.
