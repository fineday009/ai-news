## 1. milind-soni/OpenMausBot

⭐ 7.6k（今日 +1,199）· TypeScript · [链接](https://github.com/milind-soni/OpenMausBot)

**是什么**：开源版"Grok Bot"——把 AI 做成一个聊天应用，侧边栏里每个 bot 都是一个真实 Agent（本地跑的 Claude 或 Codex），各有性格、模型、云电脑和连接的 App。

**What it is**: The open-source Grok Bot alternative: your own team of AI bots in a chat app, where each bot in the sidebar is a real agent (Claude or Codex running locally) with its own personality, model, cloud computer and connected apps.

**为什么值得关注**：2026 年里 Grok Bot、Meta Muse、OpenAI dots、Manus Cue 等"个人 Agent"产品七周内密集发布，但都把模型、算力和你的数据留在厂商一侧。OpenMausBot 做的是这个形态的开源、本地优先版：用你本机已装的 `claude`/`codex`/`grok` CLI 和已有订阅，转录、密钥、事件都存在 `~/.openmausbot`，并用审批卡控制 Agent 的每一步危险操作。它正好接住了"想要个人 Agent、但不想交出数据主权"的那批人。

**Why it matters**: Through 2026, a wave of personal-agent products—Grok Bot, Meta Muse, OpenAI dots, Manus Cue—shipped within seven weeks, each keeping the model, the compute and your data on its maker's side. OpenMausBot is the open, local-first version of that shape: it runs on the `claude`/`codex`/`grok` CLIs already on your machine and your existing subscriptions, keeps transcripts, keys and events in `~/.openmausbot`, and gates risky agent actions behind approval cards. It lands squarely with people who want a personal agent without giving up data ownership.

## 2. Donchitos/Claude-Code-Game-Studios

⭐ 29.4k（今日 +353）· Shell · [链接](https://github.com/Donchitos/Claude-Code-Game-Studios)

**What it is**: Turn a single Claude Code session into a full game development studio—49 specialized agents, 74 skills and 15 hooks, organized like a real studio with directors, department leads and specialists.

**为什么值得关注**：单会话写游戏很强，但没有结构：没人拦着你写死魔法数字、跳过设计文档或堆意大利面代码，也没有 QA 和设计评审。这个项目给 AI 会话套上一套工作室流程——创意总监守愿景、各部门主管管领域、专家干活，还有钩子在提交/推送时自动校验。它代表了"从单 Agent 到多 Agent 分工"在一个垂直领域里被做到极致的样子。

**Why it matters**: A solo session can build a game but has no structure—nothing stops hardcoded magic numbers, skipped design docs or spaghetti, and there's no QA or design review. This bolts a studio process onto an AI session: a creative director guards the vision, department leads own their domains, specialists do the work, and hooks auto-validate on commit and push. It's what "single agent to a coordinated multi-agent team" looks like taken to the extreme in one vertical.

**What it is**: A skill that has your coding agent produce commercial-grade launch films end to end—it reads your product, offers three directions, writes the whole film, composes its own score in ABC notation, then opens a browser editor where you point at a frame, say what to change, and it edits just that spot.

**为什么值得关注**：多数"AI 做视频"要么直接驱动视频模型（不稳、难控），要么只能一句话出一段。huashu-motion 换了套抽象：用 Agent 擅长的代码来描述画面和音乐，于是整支片子可编程、可逐帧审查、可"指着改"。作者展示的样片（含给 DeepSeek、Claude Duet 做的发布片）大多半小时出片、成本极低。来自中文作者、差异化明显，是"Agent 当创意团队"里很有想象力的一支。

**Why it matters**: Most "AI video" either drives a video model directly (unstable, hard to control) or only yields a single clip from one prompt. huashu-motion picks a different abstraction: describe the visuals and music in the code agents are good at, making the whole film programmable, reviewable frame by frame, and editable by pointing at it. The author's samples (including launch films for DeepSeek and Claude Duet) mostly render in half an hour at very low cost. From a Chinese builder and clearly differentiated, it's one of the more imaginative takes on agents as a creative crew.

As "can you trust an autonomous agent to run" becomes the key question, making actions explicit and controllable is a precondition for agents actually entering real workflows.

### 播客要点 / Podcast

**《AI & I》（Every 出品）本期对话四次艾美奖得主、影视人兼技术人 Fred Graver**，主题是"能不能用 AI 做出好艺术，而不只是粗制滥造（slop）"。几个要点：

- **把 AI 当"虚拟编剧室"**：不是让它替你写，而是让一群"智能体"帮你把事情想透；甚至可以建一个八人焦点小组，专门留一个"你真不想请来的那个爱唱反调的人"。
- **用领域专家的 skill 当护栏**：他把创作拆成"构思—制作—触达观众"三段循环，每段都灌入从塞尔兹尼克到塔伦蒂诺的公开方法论和自己的"文化文档"，让 Agent 据此自检，避免滑向 slop。
- **一句话真经**："成功就是在观众知道他们想要什么之前，你就知道了。"（Success is knowing what the audience wants before the audience knows it.）AI 能帮你说出来，但说什么得是你自己的。

**AI & I (by Every) talks with Fred Graver**, a four-time Emmy-winning filmmaker and technologist, on whether you can use AI to make good art rather than slop. Key points:

- **Go inward before touching the tools**: Graver insists on spending a day or two dumping everything you know onto a page before opening AI—"you will never have that opportunity again once you open AI" to be purely yourself and confront the idea. Without your own compass, you just follow Claude wherever it goes.
- **Treat AI as a virtual writers' room**: not to write for you, but as a group of intelligences to think things through; you can even build an eight-person focus group that includes "the guy you really wish you hadn't invited."
- **Use domain experts' skills as guardrails**: he splits creation into a three-phase loop—ideation, production, reaching the audience—pouring published methodology from Selznick to Tarantino plus his own "culture documents" into each so the agent self-checks and avoids sliding into slop.
- **The takeaway**: "Success is knowing what the audience wants before the audience knows it." AI can help you say it, but what you say has to be your own. ([节目 / show](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLuMcoKK9mKgHtW_o9h5sGO2vXrffKHwJL))
