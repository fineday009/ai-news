## 1. ruvnet/RuView

⭐ 101.1k（今日 +557）· Rust · [链接](https://github.com/ruvnet/RuView)

**是什么**：把 WiFi 无线电信号变成空间智能的开源感知平台——无需摄像头或穿戴设备，即可检测人体存在、测量呼吸与心率、识别活动和跌倒、估计姿态，甚至能穿墙。

**What it is**: π RuView turns commodity WiFi signals into real-time spatial intelligence—detecting presence, measuring breathing and heart rate, recognizing activity and falls, and estimating pose without cameras or wearables, even through walls.

**为什么值得关注**：星数已突破 10 万（9 月 15 日上日报时为 9.39 万），今日仍 +557。它用低至 9 美元一个的 ESP32 节点读取 WiFi 信道状态信息（CSI），再用 Hugging Face 上公开的预训练模型推断人的状态；可通过 Home Assistant 接入，也能以 Matter 桥接方式接入 Apple Home、Google Home、Alexa、SmartThings。新版还加了 RuView MetaHarness，让 AI 操作员来完成部署、校准、训练和核验。README 也坦言，融合雷达、UWB 等的"统一射频世界模型"在真实数据验证前精度仍属合成数据结果。

**Why it matters**: It has now passed 100k stars (it had 93.9k when it made the Sept 15 daily) and still added +557 today. It reads WiFi Channel State Information (CSI) with ESP32 nodes costing as little as $9 each, then infers what people are doing with a pretrained model published on Hugging Face; it plugs into Home Assistant and pairs with Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa and SmartThings as a Matter bridge. The new RuView MetaHarness lets an AI operator onboard, calibrate, train and verify a deployment. The README is also candid that its unified RF world model (fusing radar, UWB and more) still reports synthetic accuracy until it's validated on real data.

## 2. interplains/Zero-Breach-Toolkit

⭐ 2.8k · Python · [链接](https://github.com/interplains/Zero-Breach-Toolkit)

**是什么**：免费开源的离线检测工具，在 Windows、macOS、Linux 电脑、Android 手机和 iPhone 备份中查找已知雇佣间谍软件（Pegasus、Predator、Graphite、Candiru 等）的痕迹。

**What it is**: A free, open-source check for traces of known mercenary spyware (Pegasus, Predator, Graphite, Candiru and others) on Windows, macOS and Linux computers, Android phones and iPhone backups.

**为什么值得关注**：10 月 25 日创建。它的设计很克制：只读、不改动原始证据和系统设置；扫描时不发任何网络请求、没有上传功能，只需事先更新一次公开的特征清单；每份报告都写明发现了什么、证据强度如何、哪些没能检查，并强调"没发现"不等于"安全"。附有面向零技术背景用户的分步教程，同时首先建议疑似被针对的人联系 Access Now 或国际特赦组织安全实验室。

**Why it matters**: Created Oct 25. Its design is restrained: read-only, never modifying source evidence or system settings; no network requests during a scan and no upload feature, only a one-time update of the published indicator lists beforehand; and every report states what was found, how strong the evidence is and what couldn't be checked, stressing that a clean result is not proof you're safe. It ships step-by-step guides for people with no technical background, and first points anyone who thinks they're being targeted to Access Now's helpline or Amnesty International's Security Lab.

## 3. JustAResearcher/gpu-unlock-ada

⭐ 4.4k · C · [链接](https://github.com/JustAResearcher/gpu-unlock-ada)

**是什么**：面向 RTX 4090 和实验性 RTX 4070 Ti SUPER 的 GPU 张量算力解锁源码，固件在本机生成。

**What it is**: GPU tensor unlock source for RTX 4090 and experimental RTX 4070 Ti SUPER; firmware generated locally.

**为什么值得关注**：10 月 23 日创建，是 10-26 日报介绍过的 Zagitta/gpu-unlock 的 Ada 架构分支。作者在 RTX 4090 上测得 FP16/BF16（FP32 累加）张量吞吐约翻倍（165.6 → 331.6 TF/s），独立复现也接近 2 倍；但真实 cuBLAS GEMM 只提升 12–34%，一个量化 MoE 推理负载则没有任何收益。README 把每个数字的测试条件和局限写得很清楚，并说明结果只来自一两张卡；它会改动底层硬件状态，请先看清风险。

**Why it matters**: Created Oct 23, it's the Ada-architecture fork of Zagitta/gpu-unlock, which the Oct 26 daily covered. On an RTX 4090 the author measured FP16/BF16 tensor throughput with FP32 accumulation roughly doubling (165.6 → 331.6 TF/s), and an independent run also came close to 2×; real cuBLAS GEMM, though, gained only 12–34%, and a quantized mixture-of-experts serving workload saw no gain. The README spells out the conditions and limits behind every number and notes results come from one or a few cards. It changes low-level hardware state, so read the risks first.

It's built in SolidJS and Rust, dogfooded for two years, and fully open source under AGPLv3 (not open core), so you can self-host it.

It's a showcase of splitting a multi-step decision process into skills—not investment advice, and simulated odds don't predict future results.

**What it is**: Todas as leis do Brasil—the Federal Constitution and federal, state, district and municipal norms, converted from official sources to Markdown with a structured header and a link to each text's origin.

**为什么值得关注**：初始库收录 323,849 部法规，而且"一部一提交"——每个 commit 的署名和日期对应颁布者和通过日期，git 历史本身就是立法时间线。目标是把巴西整个法律体系（含监管机构规章、条约和有约束力的判例）放进同一个纯文本仓库，对法律检索、研究和做 RAG 都很有用。README 明确提醒：文本为自动转换和 OCR 结果，不能替代官方公报，引用前须核对原文。

**Why it matters**: The initial base holds 323,849 norms, committed one per commit—each signed by whoever enacted it and dated to the day it passed—so the git history is itself the legislative timeline. The goal is Brazil's entire legal order (including regulator rules, treaties and binding precedent) in one plain-text repo, useful for legal search, research and RAG. The README is explicit that the texts are automated conversions and OCR, don't replace the official gazette, and should be checked against the source before citing.
