Unlike most closed-source AI companions, it's a "you own it" open alternative and a leading blueprint for anyone wanting to truly control their own AI character.

**Why it matters**: It's essentially "Folding@Home / BOINC for LLM inference": pooling idle GPUs into a distributed inference network, MIT-licensed and built on Ollama, with earnings tied to real inference usage rather than token speculation. ## 5. wouterdebie/davit

⭐ 911 · 创建于 2026-07-04 · Swift · [链接](https://github.com/wouterdebie/davit)

**是什么**：一个原生 macOS 图形界面，用于管理苹果为 Apple Silicon 提供的容器平台——"就像 OrbStack/Docker Desktop，但面向苹果自带的 Linux-容器-即轻量-虚拟机方案"。

**What it is**: A native macOS GUI for Apple's container platform on Apple silicon—"think OrbStack/Docker Desktop, but for the Linux-containers-as-lightweight-VMs stack Apple ships."

**为什么值得关注**：苹果为 Apple Silicon 提供了原生容器能力，但一直缺一个像样的图形界面。davit 用纯 Swift 编写，直接通过 XPC 与苹果的容器服务通信（而非套壳调用 CLI），支持镜像拉取/构建、卷与网络配置、compose 文件和交互式终端。对 Mac 开发者来说，它把「苹果原生容器」从命令行体验拉到了 Docker Desktop 级别的易用度，是本期最务实的非 AI 开发工具之一。

**Why it matters**: Apple ships native container support for Apple silicon but has long lacked a decent GUI. Written in pure Swift, davit talks directly to Apple's container services over XPC (rather than wrapping CLI calls), with image pulling/building, volume and network config, compose-file support and interactive terminals. For Mac developers it brings "Apple-native containers" up to Docker Desktop–level usability—one of the most practical non-AI dev tools this period.

### 🎧 播客 / Podcast

**No Priors — 《核能如何解锁能源丰裕：对话 Valar Atomics 创始人 Isaiah Taylor》**

一句话要点：AI 算力的爆炸式需求，正把「用制造业方式量产先进核反应堆、让能源便宜十倍」这件被搁置四十年的事重新变成可能。

The Takeaway: The explosive demand for AI compute is making it possible again to mass-manufacture advanced nuclear reactors and make energy 10× cheaper—a project the industry had left stalled for four decades.

- **核能从未有过「福特时刻」**：Isaiah Taylor（Valar Atomics 创始人兼 CEO，其曾祖父是曼哈顿计划的核物理学家）认为核裂变虽是 1940 年代的老技术，却从未被「为大规模量产」而设计。Valar 的反应堆「更多是被制造出来的，而非被建造出来的」，目标是把能源成本降到十分之一。

- **AI 是这轮核能复兴的需求引擎**：能源是商品，需求由价格决定——只要能把能源做便宜，需求自然涌现；而美国因 AI 计算激增带来的电力饥渴，正好提供了这个价格拐点。Valar 甚至已让「首颗由核反应堆供电的 AI 芯片」跑起来，并点亮了美国五十多年来第一座 TRISO 反应堆。

- **安全哲学：不是压低"概率"，而是消除"后果"**：传统核电把精力都放在「让事故永不发生」，而先进反应堆转向「即便一切都失效，也不会对公众造成辐射」。Valar 递交监管的安全论证前提是「厂内所有系统全部失效」，靠堆芯几何、材料与 TRISO 燃料从物理上保证安全。他还指出：按「单位能量致死人数」算，核能其实比太阳能还安全。

- **核工业的「虚高成本」**：Taylor 团队举例，一套被供应商报价 500 万美元、要造两年半的「反应堆保护系统」，他们五个工程师关在会议室六周、花约 40 万美元就自己造了出来。他直言「这是一个四十年没造过什么东西的虚假行业，仅有的一点产出还在收 100 倍溢价」——这正是 Valar 的成本护城河所在。

- **No Priors — "How Nuclear Will Unlock Energy Abundance with Valar Atomics Founder Isaiah Taylor"**: Isaiah Taylor (founder/CEO of Valar Atomics, whose great-grandfather was a Manhattan Project physicist) argues fission is old 1940s tech that was never designed for mass manufacturing—Valar's reactors are "more manufactured than constructed," aiming to make energy 10× cheaper. AI is the demand engine: energy is a commodity whose demand is set by price, and America's AI-driven power hunger provides the tipping point; Valar has even powered "the first AI chip run on a nuclear reactor" and lit up the first TRISO reactor in the US in over 50 years. On safety, advanced reactors reduce *consequence* rather than just *odds*—Valar's regulatory safety case assumes *everything* in the plant has failed and still doses no one, and by deaths-per-energy nuclear is already safer than solar. On cost, a "reactor protection system" a vendor quoted at $5M and 2.5 years, his five engineers built themselves in six weeks for ~$400k: "It's a fake industry that has not been building anything for forty years, and the little bit they have been building, they're charging up 100× margin on."
