### 播客要点 / Podcast

**《Training Data》：Google AI 基础设施负责人 Amin Vahdat 谈前沿 AI 的物理学与经济学**

**Training Data: Google's AI Infrastructure Chief Amin Vahdat on the Physics & Economics of Frontier AI**

一句话要点：AI 数据中心的胜负正从"堆算力"转向"软硬件协同设计"——用专用化换能效，再用芯片之间的"可互换性"对冲押错赛道的风险。

The takeaway: the AI-datacenter game is shifting from "pile up compute" to co-designing hardware and software — trading specialization for efficiency, while hedging the risk of a wrong bet with chips that stay fungible.

- 我们正处于人类史上最大的资本开支潮，仅 Google 今年 CapEx 预计就超过 2000 亿美元，绝大部分投入数据中心。/ We're in the biggest CapEx buildout in human history; Google alone expects $200B+ in CapEx this year, most of it data centers.

- AI 数据中心更"专用"：机房与要放进去的硬件是协同设计的，而传统数据中心是按 25–30 年通用寿命规划、硬件只活约 6 年。/ AI data centers are more purpose-built: the building is co-designed with the hardware going into it, versus a general 25–30 year planning horizon with ~6-year hardware life.

- 2026 年推理/服务真正起量，促成今年发布两款芯片——8i 主打推理、8T 主打训练；但两者都能干对方的活，以保留灵活性。/ With inference/serving taking off in 2026, Google shipped two chips this year — 8i for inference, 8T for training — yet both can do the other's job, preserving fungibility.

- 芯片—网络—软件的协同设计能把每层 10%–20%–2x 的优化叠乘成巨大的"每瓦智能"收益，代价是更强的锁定、更低的可迁移性。/ Co-design across chip–network–software multiplies 10%–20%–2x gains per layer into huge "intelligence per watt," at the cost of more lock-in and less portability.

- 金句 / Quote：「越是为某个特定负载做专用化，它就越不灵活，但也越快、越省电——这是一门艺术。」"The more you specialize to a particular workload, the less flexible it is, the faster, the more power efficient the hardware is going to be. So it is this art."
