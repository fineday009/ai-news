## 2. slvDev/esp32-ai

⭐ 851 · 2026-07-23 创建 · Python / C · [链接](https://github.com/slvDev/esp32-ai)

**是什么**：在一块约 8 美元的 ESP32-S3 单片机上跑一个 2890 万参数的语言模型——大部分权重放在 flash 而非内存，纯本地生成、约 9.5 tokens/秒。

**What it is**: Runs a 28.9M-parameter language model on an ~$8 ESP32-S3 microcontroller — most weights live in flash rather than RAM, generating text fully on-device at ~9.5 tokens/sec.

**为什么值得关注**：它用 Google 的 Per-Layer Embeddings 技巧把"大模型跑在极限硬件上"从段子做成了可复现的工程。无需联网、成本一杯咖啡，这类项目正在把 LLM 推向 IoT / 嵌入式的最边缘，也是"本地优先"叙事最极端的注脚。

**Why it matters**: Using Google's Per-Layer Embeddings trick, it turns "LLM on absurdly constrained hardware" from a meme into reproducible engineering. No network, cost of a coffee — projects like this push LLMs to the far edge of IoT/embedded, and are the most extreme footnote to the local-first narrative.

**Why it matters**: Its real edge is being "designed for AI": documents are stored as plain JSON that Claude Code, Cursor or even local Ollama can read and edit to auto-build decks. It re-designs the *document format itself* to be agent-friendly — a clean example of the local-first, agent-drivable school of tools.

**Why it matters**: One of the few non-AI, hardcore infrastructure picks today. ### 🎧 播客 / Podcast

**No Priors — 与 DoorDash 联合创始人 Andy Fang、Stanley Tang 聊自动配送 / "Building an Autonomous Delivery Experience with DoorDash Co-Founders Andy Fang and Stanley Tang"**

一句话要点：DoorDash 悄悄做了八年"机器人公司"，它的护城河不是模型，而是 100 亿次配送积累的物理世界数据。

The takeaway: DoorDash has quietly been a robotics company for eight years — its moat isn't the model, it's the physical-world data from 10 billion deliveries.

- **对话式点单正在改变消费**："Ask DoorDash" 上有 50% 的餐厅点单来自用户从没点过的店（这是 DoorDash 历史上最难撬动的指标之一）；生鲜品类的客单量则大了约 40%——用户会拍下冰箱照片让它"帮我补货"。
- **Agent 优先的未来**：Andy 指出"如今网上的 Agent 流量已超过人类流量"，团队甚至做了一个 DoorDash CLI，让 Agent 能直接下单（例如让货架摄像头在快空时自动补货）。
- **customer-back，而非 technology-first**：Stanley 反驳"自动驾驶会像 ChatGPT 一样自然通用"的想法——物理世界更脏更乱。他们自研的配送机器人 **DoorDash Dot**（300 磅、时速 20 英里、可上自行车道和马路）已在凤凰城运行近两年，定位正好卡在慢吞吞的人行道机器人与 4000 磅 robotaxi 之间。
- 一句金句："如果你只是运几个卷饼，真的需要一辆带座椅和空调的 4000 磅汽车吗？"

- **Conversational ordering is changing consumption**: On "Ask DoorDash," 50% of restaurant orders go to places the user has never ordered from (one of DoorDash's historically hardest metrics to move); grocery basket sizes run ~40% larger — people photograph their fridge and say "help me restock."
- **An agent-first future**: Andy notes "there's more agent traffic on the web than human traffic," and the team built a DoorDash CLI so agents can order directly (e.g. a pantry-shelf camera auto-restocking when it runs low).
- **Customer-back, not technology-first**: Stanley pushes back on the idea that autonomy "will just work like ChatGPT" — the physical world is messier. Their in-house robot **DoorDash Dot** (300 lbs, 20 mph, bike-lane- and road-capable) has run in Phoenix for nearly two years, sized deliberately between slow sidewalk robots and 4,000-lb robotaxis.
- Best line: "If you're only carrying a couple burritos around, do you really need a 4,000-pound car with chairs and AC?"
