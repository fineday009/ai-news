## 1. heygen-com/hyperframes

⭐ 47.9k（今日 +2,627）· TypeScript · [链接](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes)

**是什么**：写 HTML，渲染出视频。HeyGen 开源的、专为 Agent 设计的视频生成框架，用 Puppeteer + GSAP + FFmpeg 把网页级的排版与动画烘焙成成片，并带 MCP 接口。

**What it is**: Write HTML, render video. HeyGen's open-source, agent-first video framework bakes web-grade layout and animation into finished clips via Puppeteer + GSAP + FFmpeg, and ships an MCP interface.

**为什么值得关注**：让 LLM 用它最擅长的 HTML/CSS 直接「编排」视频，绕开了传统视频 API 的黑盒，模板可复用、可版本管理；HeyGen 自家产品发布视频就是这么做的。今日单日涨 2.6k star，是当下 Agent 做多媒体这条线最有说服力的落地之一。

**Why it matters**: It lets an LLM author video in the HTML/CSS it's best at, sidestepping the black box of traditional video APIs—templates are reusable and version-controllable, and HeyGen ships its own launch videos this way. Up 2.6k stars in a day, it's one of the most convincing examples of agents doing real multimedia work.

**Why it matters**: The first step of feeding an LLM is always turning messy formats into plain text; markitdown makes that grunt work a standard part, one of the most-cited preprocessors in RAG/agent data pipelines. It isn't new, but climbing Trending again today with +2k stars shows demand for "documents into LLMs" is still expanding.

## 3. mksglu/context-mode

⭐ 21.4k（今日 +651）· TypeScript · [链接](https://github.com/mksglu/context-mode)

**是什么**：面向 AI 编码 Agent 的上下文窗口优化层，通过沙箱化工具输出（宣称减少 98%）、持久化会话记忆、跨 17 个平台用 MCP + hooks 强制路由。

**What it is**: A context-window optimization layer for AI coding agents—sandboxing tool output (a claimed 98% reduction), persisting session memory, and enforcing routing across 17 platforms via MCP + hooks.

**为什么值得关注**：随着 Agent 工具调用越来越多，「工具回显把上下文撑爆」成了真痛点。它不改模型，而是在中间层做输出裁剪与记忆持久化，一套配置横跨 Claude Code、Codex、Cursor 等多家。今日 +651，属于「省 token」赛道里较系统化的一个。

**Why it matters**: As agents make more tool calls, "tool output blowing up the context" has become a real pain. Rather than touch the model, it trims output and persists memory at the middle layer, with one config spanning Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and more. Up 651 today, it's one of the more systematic entries in the token-saving race.

**Why it matters**: The "last mile" of agents browsing the web often stalls at getting blocked; camofox packages anti-detection into a standard browser interface that scraping/automation agents can use directly. Up 871 today, it answers the demand for "eyes that can actually reach the open web."

### 播客 / Podcast

**《一个 100 亿美元对冲基金的 AI 打法》— AI & I by Every**（嘉宾：Walleye 掌门人 Will；主持：Ben）

**"A $10B Hedge Fund's AI Playbook" — AI & I by Every** (guest: Will, head of Walleye; host: Ben)

- **自上而下、不容商量的 AI 文化**：Walleye（近 100 亿美元 AUM）把用 AI 变成硬性要求。金句：「把用 ChatGPT 当作弊，是学术界那套不适用的观念。在现实世界里，用 AI 就像喝下一剂让你瞬间聪明 20% 的魔法药水」——「不用这些工具，就像 1995 年因为互联网还不完美就拒绝上网一样愚蠢」。/ A top-down, non-optional AI culture: Walleye (~$10B AUM) makes AI use mandatory. The line: "Using ChatGPT is not cheating—that's a non-applicable idea from academia. In the real world, using AI is like a magical elixir that makes you 20% smarter instantly," and "not using these tools is like refusing to use the internet in 1995 because it wasn't perfect."

- **自研内部工具「Current」**：由一位前分析师在两年前（GPT-3 时代）主动搭起，如今每支基本面选股团队都在用，财报季用量飙升；从竞对跳槽来的人反馈说它「明显更好、已是工作中不可或缺的一环」。/ A homegrown internal tool, "Current," started two years ago (the GPT-3 era) by a former analyst; now every fundamental stock-picking team uses it, usage spikes during earnings, and hires from competitors call it "clearly better and essential."

- **工具不替代思考，而是把时间还给思考**：过去 50–60% 的时间花在机械敲字上，现在只剩 5%，省下的时间应该用来真正地想、读、校对——「这些工具不否定思考的必要，反而应该给你更多时间去思考」。/ Tools don't replace thinking—they hand time back to it: where 50–60% of the time once went to mechanical typing, now it's 5%, and the saved time should go to actually thinking, reading, and proofreading—"these tools don't negate the necessity to think; if anything they give you more time to think."

- **AI 作为「教练」**：以第一性原理的数学思维为例，机器能给出你自己未必想得到的直觉性答案，像教练指出你动作里被忽略的关联，与人的思考互补而非对立。/ AI as a "coach": using first-principles math thinking as an example, the machine can surface intuitive answers you wouldn't reach yourself—like a coach spotting a connection in your form—complementary to human thinking, not antagonistic.

- **落到极小的日常场景**：语音日记——因为机器认得他的声音，一段一分钟甚至三十秒的口述就能被捕捉、结构化并在数月后可检索，把「懒得动笔」的门槛降到几乎为零。/ Down to the tiniest daily use: voice journaling—because the machine knows his voice, a one-minute or even 30-second spoken entry gets captured, structured, and made retrievable months later, cutting the "too much effort to write" barrier to nearly zero.
