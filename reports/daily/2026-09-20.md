### 播客要点 / Podcast Notes

**No Priors —《Why Diffusion Will Win AI Inference with Inception Co-Founder and CEO Stefano Ermon》**

**No Priors — "Why Diffusion Will Win AI Inference with Inception Co-Founder and CEO Stefano Ermon"**

嘉宾是斯坦福教授、被誉为「扩散模型之父」之一的 Stefano Ermon，现为 Inception 联合创始人兼 CEO。核心观点：

The guest is Stanford professor and "one of the fathers of diffusion" Stefano Ermon, now co-founder and CEO of Inception. Key points:

- **赌注在推理，而非训练**：自回归模型逐 token 串行生成，属于内存受限型负载，GPU 利用率低；扩散模型一次并行生成大量 token，负载天然贴合 GPU 擅长的矩阵运算，因此推理更快、更省。"更并行的方案，最终会赢"（bitter lesson）。

- **The bet is on inference, not training**: Autoregressive models generate token-by-token in sequence — a memory-bound workload that underuses GPUs; diffusion models generate many tokens in parallel, mapping naturally to the matmuls GPUs excel at, so inference is faster and cheaper. "The more parallel solution is the one that is eventually going to win" (the bitter lesson).

- **已落地生产**：Inception 的 Mercury 模型在基准上与前沿实验室的 flash/mini/nano 速度优化模型相当，同时明显更快；团队还自建了服务引擎（现成的 vLLM/SGLang 跑不了扩散 LLM）。

- **In production already**: Inception's Mercury models match frontier labs' flash/mini/nano speed-optimized models on benchmarks while being significantly faster; the team even built its own serving engine (off-the-shelf vLLM/SGLang can't run diffusion LLMs).

- **真实客户与省钱效应**：语音 Agent 公司 Open Call 原本用 Cerebras 定制芯片求速度，改用 Inception 的扩散 LLM 后，在更易获得的 NVIDIA GPU 上拿到同等速度、更低成本。Ermon 认为速度体验一旦享受过就回不去，"就像宽带"。

- **A real customer and the cost story**: Voice-agent company Open Call previously ran on Cerebras custom chips for speed; switching to Inception's diffusion LLMs got the same speed on more-available NVIDIA GPUs at lower cost. Ermon argues that once you're used to fast, you can't go back — "it's kind of like broadband."
