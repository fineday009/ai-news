## 3. syv-ai/qwen38-27b-rtx3090

**What it is**: Qwen3.8-27B on a single RTX 3090 with vLLM: ~1,000 tok/s at 64 concurrent, ~114 tok/s single-user, 150k–262k context—patches, requant scripts, and benchmarks included.

**为什么值得关注**：把一块消费级显卡压榨到极限（int8 tensor-core GEMM、int4 lm_head 校准、split-KV 验证注意力等一整套技巧），并且把补丁和基准全部开源。对想在自有硬件上跑大模型的人来说，这是一份可复现的实战工程范本，而非空谈。

**Why it matters**: It squeezes a consumer GPU to its limit (int8 tensor-core GEMMs, calibrated int4 lm_head, split-KV verify attention, and a full bag of tricks) and open-sources every patch and benchmark. For anyone running large models on their own hardware, it's a reproducible engineering playbook rather than hand-waving.

### 🎧 播客 / Podcast

**AI & I by Every — A $10B Hedge Fund's AI Playbook（Best of the Pod）**

要点 / Key takeaways：

- 核心观点：近 100 亿美元规模对冲基金 Walleye 的 CEO/CIO Will 认为，AI 转型必须由一号位以身作则——「作为一家对冲基金，因为忽视让我们更快、更聪明、更高效的工具而把钱留在桌上，是我们应该感到羞耻的事。」

  The takeaway: Will, the CEO/CIO of ~\$10B hedge fund Walleye, argues an AI transformation has to be led from the very top by example—"As a hedge fund, we should be ashamed to leave money on the table by ignoring tools that make us faster, smarter, and more effective."

- 文化优先于工具：他刻意营造一种「容忍不完美」的文化——哪怕全公司三分之一的人在场的 demo 翻车也无所谓，重点是别把「它还不完美」当成拒绝使用的借口。

  Culture before tools: he deliberately builds a "tolerate imperfection" culture—it's fine if a demo with a third of the company watching fails; the point is not to use "it's not perfect yet" as an excuse to ignore the tech.

- 落地是真金白银：约 75% 的员工每天使用 ChatGPT 类工具，约三分之一使用 AI 编程工具；量化交易多年来已用 LLM 做大规模情绪分析；内部为基本面选股团队自建的工具 Current 在财报季使用量激增。

  Adoption is real money: ~75% of the firm uses ChatGPT-like tools daily and about a third use AI coding tools; quant trading has used LLMs for large-scale sentiment analysis for years; and an internal tool, Current, built for fundamental stock-pickers spikes in use during earnings.

- 竞争紧迫性：「作为长线选股者，如果你想保持竞争力，现在已经很难不靠这些工具熬过财报季」——因为对手能实时处理全部信息，让机器推断出人类来不及做的判断。

  Competitive urgency: "You kind of can't go through earnings period now as a long-term stock picker without some of these tools if you're going to be competitive"—because a rival can process everything in real time and let machines infer what humans can't fast enough.
