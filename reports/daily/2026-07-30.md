## 5. maderix/ANE

⭐ 7.2k（今日 +22）· Objective-C · [链接](https://github.com/maderix/ANE)

**是什么**：通过逆向 Apple 私有 API，在苹果神经引擎（Apple Neural Engine）上直接训练神经网络。

**What it is**: Training neural networks directly on the Apple Neural Engine (ANE) via reverse-engineered private APIs.

**为什么值得关注**：ANE 一直只对推理开放、且被官方 API 严密封装，这个项目把它撬开来做训练，属于硬核系统级探索。对想榨干 Mac 本地算力、研究端侧训练的开发者极具参考价值，也展示了逆向工程在 AI 基础设施里的独特位置。

**Why it matters**: The ANE has been inference-only and tightly sealed behind official APIs; prying it open for *training* is hardcore systems work. It's highly instructive for anyone trying to squeeze local compute out of a Mac or study on-device training, and showcases reverse engineering's distinct role in AI infrastructure.
