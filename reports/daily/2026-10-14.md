## 1. debpalash/VoiceStudio

⭐ 63.7k（今日 +1,748）· Python · [链接](https://github.com/debpalash/VoiceStudio)

**是什么**：一款完全本地运行的开源语音工作台，集语音克隆、音色设计、视频配音、听写、转写与有声书制作于一体，支持多达 646 种语言，自称"全本地的 ElevenLabs 替代品"。默认引擎基于 k2-fsa/OmniVoice，可切换其它引擎，支持 CUDA/Metal/CPU，云端服务仅为可选项。

**What it is**: A fully-local, open-source voice workbench for voice cloning, voice design, video dubbing, dictation, transcription and audiobook creation across 646 languages — billing itself as a "fully-local ElevenLabs alternative." Its default engine is powered by k2-fsa/OmniVoice (other engines selectable), and it runs on CUDA, Metal or CPU with remote services optional.

**为什么值得关注**：它单日新增近 1,800 星、本月累计 +3.6 万，是"语音 AI 本地化"浪潮最直接的信号。把克隆、配音、转写这些原本分散在多个付费服务里的能力打包进一个离线桌面应用，对注重隐私、想省订阅费、或在无网络环境工作的创作者极具吸引力。

**Why it matters**: Adding ~1,800 stars in a day (and +36k this month), it's the clearest signal yet of voice AI going local-first. Bundling cloning, dubbing and transcription — capabilities usually scattered across paid services — into one offline desktop app is compelling for creators who care about privacy, want to cut subscriptions, or work without a network.

## 2. onehumanbeing/esp32-iphone-agent

⭐ 1.6k · 2026-10-11 新建 · Python · [链接](https://github.com/onehumanbeing/esp32-iphone-agent)

**是什么**：用一块约 7 美元的 ESP32 让 AI 模型操作一部真实 iPhone——通过 AirPlay 当"眼睛"、USB HID 当"手"，搭配任意 OpenAI 兼容的视觉模型即可。无需 Mac、无需开发者账号、无需越狱。

**What it is**: A ~$7 ESP32 that lets an AI model operate a real iPhone — AirPlay as its "eyes," USB HID as its "hands," paired with any OpenAI-compatible vision model. No Mac, no developer account, no jailbreak.

**为什么值得关注**：它把"手机自动化/computer-use"的硬件门槛砍到了一杯咖啡的价格，走的是纯外设路线、不侵入系统，因此几乎不受 iOS 封闭生态限制。对想让 Agent 跑真机测试、做无障碍自动化或做硬件原型的人，这是一个极具想象力的廉价方案。

**Why it matters**: It cuts the hardware barrier for phone automation / computer-use down to the price of a coffee, going the pure-peripheral route that touches nothing inside the OS and so sidesteps iOS's walled garden. For anyone who wants agents to drive real devices for testing, accessibility automation or hardware prototyping, it's a wildly imaginative low-cost path.

### 播客要点 / Podcast

**《How Sam Altman Uses Dots to Take Back His Time》— The Every Podcast（原 AI & I，主持 Dan Shipper）**

在 DevDay 期间与 Sam Altman 的对谈里，几个值得记住的点：

- **工作不是变少而是变多**：编码模型能力飙升，但开发者"从未如此高产，门槛也从未如此高"；世界上潜在的软件需求远超预期，AI 反而催生了更多新活。
- **"Dots" 后台助理**：Altman 日常几乎都在用一个叫 Dot（他的叫 Dottie）的后台 Agent——它能跨 Slack、日历、截图等拼接上下文，主动帮你找回"以为是梦到的"信息、迭代产品点子、过滤海量信息。
- **以人为中心**："我们其实没那么在乎机器做了什么……我们痴迷的是人。"即便 1 万个 Agent 协作四天解决了难题，公众关注的仍是背后的人。
- **为"人机协作"重造软件**：现有办公软件不是为"多人+各自的 AI 一起协作"设计的；他看好"活文档（live documents）"——会随人和世界变化自动更新的文档。

**Key points from a DevDay conversation with Sam Altman on The Every Podcast (formerly AI & I, hosted by Dan Shipper):**

- **Work expands, it doesn't shrink**: coding models have leapt in capability, yet developers have "never been able to do more, but there's never been a higher bar either" — latent demand for software far exceeded expectations, and AI spawns more new work.
- **"Dots" background assistant**: Altman now does most of his work through a background agent called a Dot (his is "Dottie") that stitches context across Slack, calendar and screenshots, proactively surfaces things he'd given up on, iterates on product ideas and filters the firehose.
- **Human-centered**: "We just don't care that much about what the machines do... we're obsessed with other people." Even when 10,000 agents coordinated for four days to crack a hard problem, the public reaction was about the people.
- **Rebuilding software for human-AI collaboration**: today's apps weren't designed for "multiple people and multiple people's AIs all working together," and he's excited about "live documents" that update automatically as people and the world change.
