## 4. sybil-solutions/inference-optimisation-skill

**What it is**: Agent skill for hill-climbing local LLM serving speed (decode, speculative decoding, prefill, KV, startup, TTFT) on any hardware and quantization.

**为什么值得关注**：创建 4 天 1,270 星。它不是一份参数清单，而是把调优方法写成流程：先算理论上限，每次只改一个变量，与交错对照组比较，记录包括失败在内的每次结果，并用长上下文检索做稳定性把关。README 给出的例子之一是 Intel Arc B390 核显笔记本：单用户解码 43.6 → 53 tok/s，256K prefill 297 → 628 tok/s（2.1 倍），启动 113 → 43 秒。兼容 Claude Code 及任何加载 `SKILL.md` 的 Agent，适合自己部署本地模型的人。

**Why it matters**: 1,270 stars four days after creation. It isn't a list of flags but a method written down as a workflow: compute the theoretical ceiling, change one thing at a time, measure against interleaved controls, log every result including failures, and gate on long-context retrieval. One example from the README is a laptop with an Intel Arc B390 iGPU: single-user decode 43.6 → 53 tok/s, 256K prefill 297 → 628 tok/s (2.1×), startup 113 → 43 s. It works with Claude Code and any agent that loads `SKILL.md`-style skills—aimed at people serving their own local models.

## 8. imxieyi/Kernarium

⭐ 1,169 · C++ · 创建于 2026-11-07 · [链接](https://github.com/imxieyi/Kernarium)

**是什么**：借助 User-mode Linux，在 iOS 上原生运行真实的 Linux 内核。

**What it is**: Run a real Linux kernel natively on iOS, through User-mode Linux.

**为什么值得关注**：它的架构介于全系统模拟器（如 UTM）和纯用户态模拟器（如 iSH）之间：User-mode Linux 内核被编译成原生 arm64 代码并链接进 iOS 运行时，内核不经模拟直接运行；用户态程序用 iSH 式的线程化解释器模拟并支持 SMP，GPU 加速由 Vulkan 转 Metal 的 KosmicKrisp 提供。基于它的 Android 模拟器 Andarium 曾上架 App Store，后被苹果以违反审核指南为由下架，现在只能从 releases 侧载；通用 Linux 应用仍处于早期。对系统与模拟器爱好者是很有意思的一次工程尝试。

**Why it matters**: Its architecture sits between a full system emulator (like UTM) and a pure userspace emulator (like iSH): the User-mode Linux kernel is compiled to native arm64 and linked into an iOS runtime, so the kernel runs without emulation; userspace programs run in an iSH-style threaded interpreter with SMP support, and GPU acceleration comes from KosmicKrisp, a Vulkan-to-Metal layer. Andarium, an Android emulator built on it, was released on the App Store and later removed by Apple for violating the review guidelines, so it's sideload-only from releases; the generic Linux app is still early. A fascinating piece of engineering for systems and emulation enthusiasts.

- **Guest**: Ivan Zhao, founder and CEO of Notion. Notion is 13 years old, founded on bringing computing power to as many people as possible without forcing them to learn to code; he says the spirit of vibe coding is the spirit Notion started with.
- **"能做"不等于"该做"**：人应该有能力修改自己每天用的工具，这一点他"绝对赞成"；但像日历这样的东西，多数人更想要别人精心打磨好的方案，就像你会自己做饭、也常去好餐厅；而涉及安全、需要长期维护的企业软件，他个人不建议自己造。
- **Just because you can doesn't mean you should**: People should have the power to modify the tools they use every day—"hell yes." But for things like a calendar, most people want a well-crafted solution from someone else, the way you can cook and still like a good restaurant; and he personally wouldn't recommend building security-sensitive enterprise software you then have to maintain.
- **数据比流程和界面更持久**：过去软件的价值是定义流程并做出界面，Agent 把这两样都打开了。Notion 因此把自己定位为数据与上下文的保管者，向各种 Agent 开放，并为人类团队的协作提供护栏。
- **Data outlasts logic and UI**: Software's value used to be defining a workflow and building the buttons for it; agents opened up both. So Notion sees its job as the keeper of your data and context, open to different agents, with guardrails for a team of humans collaborating on it.
- **不要放弃理解**：他注意到自己更爱和 AI 聊天而不是阅读，有时感觉像在刷 TikTok。他的回答是做更好的抽象和可视化："我不认为我们应该交出自己的理解，至少不能不战而降。"他的团队不再各自对着黑盒 Agent 工作，而是用 Notion 看板来编排一组 Agent。
- **Don't surrender understanding**: He notices he likes chatting with AI more than reading, and that it sometimes feels like TikTok. His answer is better abstractions and visualization: "I don't think we should surrender our understanding. Definitely not without putting up a fight." Instead of each person talking to their own black-box agent, his team orchestrates agents on a Notion Kanban board.
- **乐高而不是黏土**：vibe coding 给你的是黏土，很难堆高；行业正在收敛到"vibe coding 的乐高"——权限、同步、协作由底座负责。他假设下一个十亿"知识工作者"绝大多数会是 Agent，并希望在未来一两个季度内，让 50 人的公司开箱即用地拥有全部上下文；他认为做得好的话有可能省下 20%–40% 的 token。
- **Lego, not clay**: Vibe coding hands you clay, which is hard to build tall; the industry is converging on "the Legos of vibe coding," where permissions, syncing and collaboration are taken care of. He assumes the vast majority of the next billion knowledge workers coming online will be agents, and hopes within the next quarter or two to give a 50-person company all its context out of the box—done right, he thinks saving 20–40% of tokens might be possible.
