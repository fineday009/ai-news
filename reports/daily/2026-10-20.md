## 1. shiyu-coder/Kronos

**是什么**：面向金融市场数据的基础模型。

**为什么值得关注**：大模型的"基础模型"范式过去集中在文本、图像、代码，Kronos 把它搬到 K 线/行情这类时间序列上——一个在海量市场数据上预训练、可迁移到多种下游预测任务的通用底座。相比每个策略各练一个小模型，这种"金融界的基础模型"思路更有想象力，也解释了它持续的高热度。

**Why it matters**: The "foundation model" paradigm has mostly lived in text, images and code; Kronos ports it to financial time series—a general backbone pretrained on large volumes of market data and transferable to many downstream forecasting tasks. **Why it matters**: Once you're running several coding agents at once—and want to check on them from your phone—"one agent per terminal" stops scaling. Orca pulls launching, orchestration and cross-device monitoring into one console, hitting the real management pain that shows up after agents graduate from toy to daily driver. Still adding 1.5k stars today.

## 3. google-research/harness2

⭐ 12.3k · Python · [链接](https://github.com/google-research/harness2)

**是什么**：Google Research 的框架，在**不动模型权重**的前提下，自动改进围绕模型的那层"外壳"——提示词、工具、记忆、技能；它通过提出、测试、组合各种修改来为每个任务打磨 Agent 配置，并把带证据的经验带到后续任务中。

**What it is**: A Google Research framework that improves the *system around* a model—prompts, tools, memory, skills—while the weights stay frozen, proposing, testing and combining edits per task and carrying evidence-backed lessons forward.

**为什么值得关注**：业界普遍认为"要让 Agent 变强就得换更大的模型"，Harness² 反过来证明：同一个模型，光是把外围脚手架自动调教好，就能在 JobBench、WorkBuddy-Bench 等基准上显著提升。这把"prompt/工具工程"从手艺活推向了可自动优化的系统工程，出自 Google Research 更添分量。

**Why it matters**: The default assumption is "to make an agent better, swap in a bigger model." Harness² shows the opposite can pay off: with the model fixed, automatically tuning just the surrounding scaffold yields clear gains on benchmarks like JobBench and WorkBuddy-Bench. It turns prompt/tool engineering from craft into an automatically optimizable systems problem—and it carries Google Research's weight.

## 5. headroomlabs-ai/headroom

⭐ 76.3k（今日 +310）· Python · [链接](https://github.com/headroomlabs-ai/headroom)

**是什么**：在工具输出、日志、文件和 RAG 片段进入大模型之前先压缩它们——编码 Agent 省约 20% token，JSON 可省 60–95%，答案不变。可作库、代理或 MCP 服务器使用。

**What it is**: Compresses tool outputs, logs, files and RAG chunks before they reach the LLM—~20% fewer tokens for coding agents and 60–95% fewer for JSON, same answers. Usable as a library, proxy or MCP server.

**为什么值得关注**：Agent 时代最隐蔽的成本是"上下文膨胀"——冗长的工具返回和日志把 token 账单和延迟一起推高。headroom 不换模型、不改业务逻辑，只在中间做无损压缩就能砍掉大段开销，且三种接入形态（库/代理/MCP）几乎零改造成本，是典型的"小切口、高杠杆"基础设施。

**Why it matters**: The sneakiest cost in the agent era is context bloat—verbose tool returns and logs inflate both the token bill and latency. headroom changes neither the model nor your logic; it just compresses in the middle, cutting large chunks of overhead with near-zero integration cost across three form factors (library/proxy/MCP). A classic small-incision, high-leverage piece of infrastructure.

**Why it matters**: Personalized education has long lacked a high-quality, structured "map of knowledge"—you can only do real adaptive learning once you know which concept is a prerequisite for which. By open-sourcing that graph together with AI-generated teaching rationale, Marble hands education apps and AI tutors a public foundation to build on directly—a rare, pragmatic data contribution at the AI-meets-education intersection.

### 播客要点 / Podcast

**《Beam：美国的伟大开放模型》（ReflectionAI 联合创始人兼 CEO Misha Laskin 做客 No Priors）**

**No Priors — "Beam: The Great American Open Model" (guest: ReflectionAI co-founder & CEO Misha Laskin)**

- 要点一：一年前"西方没有拿得出手的开放模型，好的开源模型全来自中国"，这成了 ReflectionAI 决定端到端自建开放模型的直接原因。他们刚发布首个开放模型 Beam——一个总参 5000 亿、激活 230 亿的 MoE 模型。

  A year ago "there were no good Western open models—all the good ones came from China," and that was the direct reason ReflectionAI decided to build open models end-to-end. They just released their first, Beam—a 500B-total / 23B-active MoE model.

- 要点二：追赶前沿的资本门槛每年约 4 倍跳升——18 个月前是"数亿美元量级"，如今是"单位数十亿美元"，明年将奔向"数百亿美元"；但追赶比领跑便宜得多，因为好人才让你少走探索弯路、专注执行已知有效的做法。

  The capital bar to reach the frontier jumps ~4× a year—"hundreds of millions" 18 months ago, "single-digit billions" now, heading toward "tens of billions" next year—yet catching up is far cheaper than leading, because great talent lets you skip exploration and focus on executing what's known to work.

- 要点三：强化学习"不会停止进步"。Laskin 用 AlphaGo 那条一直上扬、最后被人为叫停的曲线类比：如今把这套 RL 配方用到有经济价值的任务上，能力上限已经从科学问题变成了"你愿意投入多少算力"的经济决策。

  Reinforcement learning "never stops improving." Laskin invokes the AlphaGo curve that kept rising until it was cut off by choice: apply that RL recipe to economically valuable tasks and the ceiling becomes not a scientific question but an economic one—how much compute you choose to pour in.

- 要点四：开放模型是"把基础设施一起带进门的特洛伊木马"。一个国家乐于输出好用的开源模型，正因为别国会在其之上构建、进而被锁定到相应的软件与硬件生态里——这既是商业也是地缘优势，所以"竞争性张力是好事，你不会希望开放智能只来自单一国家"。

  Open models are "Trojan horses for the infrastructure they bring with them." A country is happy to ship great open models because others build on top and get locked into the surrounding software and hardware stack—a commercial and geopolitical edge—so "competitive tension is good; you don't want open intelligence coming from just one country."

- 金句 / Quote：开篇抛出的开放模型安全论——"当你移除网络攻击能力时，你也移除了网络防御能力……Linus 定律说'只要眼睛足够多，所有 bug 都浮浅'；我相信只要眼睛足够多，绝大多数安全与安全性漏洞也会变得浮浅。" ("When you remove cyber offensive capabilities, you also remove cyber defensive capabilities… with enough eyeballs, most security and safety vulnerabilities become shallow as well.")
