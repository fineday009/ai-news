**为什么值得关注**：TypeScript 分榜今日 +112。Agent 理解代码通常靠 grep、glob 和逐个读文件，手工重建调用链；CodeGraph 把每个符号、调用边和依赖预先建成图谱，Agent 一次调用就能拿到相关源码、符号之间的调用路径（包括 grep 跟不到的动态分发）以及改动的影响范围。难得的是 README 把代价也写了出来：2026 年 8 月的复测显示，七个基准仓库上平均成本低 44%、token 少 62%；但在多轮会话里，它留在上下文窗口中的检索内容比逐文件读取的 Agent 多约 80%。适合代码库较大、Agent 经常把预算花在"找代码"上的团队。

**Replit CEO Amjad Masad**：Replit 发布了关于 Agent 集群扩展规律的新研究：增加 Agent 数量可以带来显著的性能提升，但与传统的扩展规律不同，更多算力不一定换来更好的结果，Agent 如何自我组织很关键。固定两小时内，Opus 5.5 在 NumPy 上的加速从单 Agent 的 3.7 倍升到 32 个 Agent 的 18.9 倍；GPT-6.2 Astra 是最强的单体 Agent，但超过 8 个之后出现负向扩展；去中心化的集群比依赖中心协调的扩展得更好；单个模型的智能水平并不能很好地预测集体智能。Replit 同时开源了 Optimization-Bench。另一条里，他谈到与副总统 JD Vance 的会面，强调美国在开放权重 AI 上的领导力：盟友想要主权 AI，正越来越多地转向中国的开放模型，美国可能赢下前沿竞赛却输掉全球 AI 生态。[集群研究](https://x.com/amasad/status/2124969305674748175) · [开放权重](https://x.com/amasad/status/2125008810041610359)

**Amjad Masad, CEO of Replit**: New research from Replit on scaling laws for agent swarms: scaling the number of agents can yield dramatic gains, but unlike traditional scaling laws, more compute doesn't necessarily mean better results, and how agents organize themselves matters. In a fixed two-hour window, Opus 5.5 went from a 3.7x speedup on NumPy with one agent to 18.9x with 32; GPT-6.2 Astra was the strongest solo agent but scaled negatively beyond 8 agents; decentralized swarms scaled better than centrally coordinated ones; and individual model intelligence is a poor predictor of collective intelligence. Replit is also open-sourcing Optimization-Bench. Separately, he describes a meeting with Vice President JD Vance and stresses American leadership in open-weight AI: allies want sovereign AI and are increasingly looking to Chinese open models, so America could win the frontier race and still lose the global AI ecosystem. [Swarm research](https://x.com/amasad/status/2124969305674748175) · [Open weights](https://x.com/amasad/status/2125008810041610359)

**Box CEO Aaron Levie**：服务型业务一直受限于交付能力，规模基本只能靠增加人手。Agent 改变了这笔账：产出可以与投入的时间脱钩，一个工程师可以有过去几十个工程师的产出。但这不等于全交给 Agent——关键仍是前端要有专家，确保 Agent 交付的是正确的结果。他预计网络安全、广告营销、法律、会计、IT 转型等过去纯靠人的服务行业都会出现这种模式。[原推](https://x.com/levie/status/2125089009018040779)

**Aaron Levie, CEO of Box**: Services businesses have always been held back by their ability to deliver, with scale essentially coming only from headcount. Agents change the calculus: output can be decoupled from the time that goes in, and a single engineer can have the output of dozens. That doesn't make it all agents, though—the key is still having experts on the front end who ensure agents deliver the right outcomes. He expects this pattern across cybersecurity, advertising and marketing, legal, accounting, IT transformation and other historically people-only services. [Tweet](https://x.com/levie/status/2125089009018040779)

**Meta AI 高级总监 Madhu Guru（曾在 Google 负责 Gemini、Veo、Nano Banana）**：他认为"数据"这个名字严重低估了它在模型开发中的分量。圈外人把数据看作买来往模型里铲的惰性大宗商品，像煤一样；实际上，优质数据编码的是极高价值的知识、品味和专业能力，往往以前所未有的方式被表达和组织，因此是构建更强智能的基础构件之一。[原推](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2125102190196859156)

**Madhu Guru, Sr Director of AI at Meta (previously led Gemini, Veo and Nano Banana at Google)**: He argues the name "data" massively undersells what it represents in model development. People who aren't close to it think of data as an inert commodity you buy and shovel into a model, like coal; in reality great data encodes very high-value knowledge, taste and expertise, often articulated and structured in ways that did not exist before, which makes it one of the fundamental building blocks for more capable intelligence.
