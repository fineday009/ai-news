## 2. Diolinux/PhotoGIMP

**是什么**：为 GIMP 3+ 打造的补丁，让界面、快捷键和布局向 Photoshop 用户靠拢。

**What it is**: A patch for GIMP 3+ that makes the interface, shortcuts, and layout feel familiar to Photoshop users.

**为什么值得关注**：非 AI，却精准命中「想逃离订阅制、又不愿重学操作」的庞大用户群。它不改内核，只做一层贴心的界面/快捷键映射，几乎零成本降低了迁移门槛——正是这种「小而准」的适配层最容易口口相传涨星。

**Why it matters**: Non-AI, yet it nails a huge audience that wants off subscriptions without relearning muscle memory. It doesn't touch the core — just a thoughtful UI/shortcut remap — cutting the switching cost to near zero. Exactly the kind of small-and-precise adaptation layer that spreads by word of mouth.

## 3. Graphify-Labs/graphify

⭐ 80.5k（今日 +856）· Python · [链接](https://github.com/Graphify-Labs/graphify)

**是什么**：把任意一个装着代码、SQL 表结构、脚本的文件夹，变成可查询的知识图谱。

**What it is**: Turns any folder of code, SQL schemas, and scripts into a queryable knowledge graph.

**为什么值得关注**：Agent 理解大型代码库的瓶颈是「上下文放不下」，graphify 用图谱把代码间的关系结构化，让 Agent（和人）能精准检索「谁调用了谁、这张表被哪些地方用」。相比一股脑塞进 RAG，结构化图谱在跨文件推理上更靠谱，是给编码 Agent 的实用底座。

**Why it matters**: The bottleneck for agents on large codebases is "context won't fit"; graphify structures inter-code relationships into a graph so agents (and humans) can precisely query who-calls-what and where-a-table-is-used. Versus dumping everything into RAG, a structured graph reasons across files more reliably — a practical substrate for coding agents.

**Why it matters**: Most agent-memory solutions lean on an external vector service or cloud API, carrying privacy and cost baggage. This keeps memory fully local and manages hot/cold information through tiers — a strong fit for privacy-sensitive and offline settings, and a signal of a major cloud vendor entering the agent-memory race.

## 6. microsoft/SkillOpt

⭐ 11.7k（今日 +275）· Python · [链接](https://github.com/microsoft/SkillOpt)

**是什么**：一个「文本空间」优化器，为冻结（不微调）的 LLM Agent 训练出可复用的自然语言技能。

**What it is**: A text-space optimizer that trains reusable natural-language skills for frozen LLM agents.

**为什么值得关注**：微调昂贵且难以迁移，SkillOpt 反其道而行——不动模型权重，而是在文本层面「优化」出一套可复用的技能提示，让通用模型在特定任务上稳定变强。这条「不训练权重也能进化 Agent」的路线，对没有算力做微调的团队极具吸引力。

**Why it matters**: Fine-tuning is expensive and hard to transfer; SkillOpt goes the other way — leave the weights alone and "optimize" reusable skill prompts in text space so a frozen model gets reliably better at a task. This "evolve the agent without training weights" path is compelling for teams without the compute to fine-tune.

### 播客要点 / Podcast

**《Inside Zipline's Autonomous System: 140M Miles, Zero Incidents》— Training Data（嘉宾：Zipline 联合创始人/CEO Keller Rinaudo Cliffton，以及系统工程与安全负责人 Eric）**

**"Inside Zipline's Autonomous System: 140M Miles, Zero Incidents" — Training Data (guests: Keller Rinaudo Cliffton, Zipline co-founder/CEO, and Eric, head of systems engineering & safety)**

- 核心成绩：Zipline 已完成 1.4 亿英里商业自主飞行、零安全事故；他们坚持「我们不是无人机公司」，而是在「为地球造一套自动化物流系统、逼近瞬间传送」——客户根本不关心背后是不是无人机。

- The headline: Zipline has flown 140 million commercial autonomous miles with zero safety incidents; they insist "we're not a drone company" but are "building an automated logistics system for Earth to approximate teleportation" — customers don't care that drones are behind the curtain.

- 找到 PMF 的信号很朴素：早期在卢旺达送救命血液时，用户最强烈的反馈不是夸产品，而是「你们为什么一天只开 12 小时，人可是 24 小时都会生病」——客户要的是「更多」，这就是 PMF。

- The PMF signal was humble: delivering life-saving blood in Rwanda early on, the loudest user feedback wasn't praise but "why are you only open 12 hours a day — people get sick 24/7?" When customers demand more of your service, that's product-market fit.

- 安全标准被上调：从最初「比开车安全 10 倍」被董事会追问后重设为「比 Waymo 还安全 2 倍」；最意外的工程挑战之一是太阳耀斑——太阳风暴会干扰 GPS 信号，逼他们把导航做到即使 GNSS 失灵也能靠冗余厘米级定位。

- The safety bar got raised: from "10x safer than cars" to, after a board challenge, "2x safer than Waymo." One of the most unexpected engineering problems was solar weather — solar flares distort GPS signals, forcing navigation robust enough to hold centimeter precision even when GNSS degrades, with redundancy beyond it.

- 垂直整合的教训：外购零件反复坠机后，他们逐个「拔掉重造」——自研电机控制器、GPS 模块。如今由「舰队指挥官（fleet commander，灵感来自《安德的游戏》）」一人调度约 100 架飞机；规模上，从 2014 到 2024 才做到第一个百万次配送，如今正冲向「每天百万次」，此时「百万分之一」的意外会每天发生。

- The vertical-integration lesson: after off-the-shelf parts kept crashing, they ripped each out and rebuilt it — designing the motor controller and GPS module from scratch. Today a single "fleet commander" (inspired by Ender's Game) oversees ~100 aircraft; it took 2014→2024 to reach the first million deliveries, and now heading toward a million a day, where one-in-a-million events happen daily.

> 「我们现在有 1.4 亿英里的商业自主飞行里程，零安全事故。零。若换成人开 1.4 亿英里，会有约 600 起事故、100 人受伤、2 到 6 人死亡。」

> "We now have 140,000,000 commercial autonomous miles and zero safety incidents. Zero. If you were to drive 140,000,000 miles, you would have 600 accidents, 100 injuries, and between two and six fatalities."
