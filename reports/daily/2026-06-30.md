### 🎧 播客要点 / Podcast

**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck —《The GPU Myth: State of AI Compute 2026》，嘉宾 Stephen Balaban（Lambda 联合创始人兼 CTO）**

**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — "The GPU Myth: State of AI Compute 2026," with Stephen Balaban (co-founder & CTO of Lambda)**

- **核心观点**：GPU 算力从来不是大宗商品。它是一门从土地审批、数据中心建设、HPC 设计到虚拟化、云服务的高度垂直整合生意——正因如此才像传统云那样容得下多个巨头，而非赢家通吃。

  The takeaway: GPU compute was never a commodity. It's a deeply vertically integrated business spanning land entitlement, data-center construction, HPC design, virtualization, and cloud services — which is why, like traditional cloud, it has room for several giants rather than a single winner.

- 算力仍在「持续欠建」：对大模型从助手到代码生成的需求看不到尽头，"我们有了一套能把钱投进去、把软件吐出来的惊人系统。"

  Compute is still chronically *underbuilt*: demand for LLMs — from assistants to code generation — shows no end, and "we have an amazing system that can take in money and output software."

- H100 租金的「下跌」多是统计假象：长租与按需是两种价格，指数若偏向长租合约就会显得在跌，实际长期租金稳中有升。

  The apparent "drop" in H100 rental prices is largely a measurement artifact: long-term and on-demand are two different rates, and if an index over-weights long-term contracts it looks like a decline when long-term rates are actually flat-to-rising.

- 效率提升 10 倍并不会压低需求，反而意味着大家能处理 10 倍的 token——典型的杰文斯悖论；世界上任一时刻的总算力仍是固定的。

  A 10× efficiency gain doesn't shrink demand — it means everyone processes 10× more tokens (the Jevons paradox); the fixed pool of compute at any moment stays fully used.

- 当前真正的瓶颈不是 GPU，而是「带电的厂房」（land + power + shell）——土地、电力和建好的数据中心外壳。

  The real bottleneck today isn't GPUs but "powered shell" — land, electricity, and a built-out data-center shell.
