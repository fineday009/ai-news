### 播客要点 / Podcast

**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck —《OpenAI's Dan Roberts: Why AI Can Now Make Discoveries》**

OpenAI「强化学习基础」团队负责人 Dan Roberts（理论物理出身，曾研究黑洞与量子引力）谈 AI 从「替我们干活」走向「自主做科学发现」。要点：

- **核心洞见**：近期 OpenAI、DeepMind、Anthropic 相继破解了数学界长期未解的 Erdős 问题。Roberts 认为关键不只是算力，而是模型敢于「逆势假设」——比如假设某个猜想为假，并有足够定力沿着极长的计算路径坚持下去。他的原话：当你「逆着主流去做一件反直觉的事，你必须对自己在做的事有强烈信念才能坚持走完一条很长的计算路径」。

- **RL 为何强大、又在哪失效**：RL 的力量来自与环境的反馈循环、按「适合自己的难度」学习；但当反馈极其稀疏（走了无数步才知道整体对错）时，credit assignment（功劳分配）变得极难，这是它最易崩的地方。

- **来自物理的「反直觉」方法论**：理解 scaling 不应「从小到大」，而要「从大到小」——当大模型出现小模型没有的「涌现」现象时，不要说「scaling law 断了」，而是承认你没理解所 scale 的东西，回头把它简化到仍包含你关心现象的最小玩具模型。

- **对科学的展望**：未来六个月会看到更多数学与科学突破，AI 也会越来越多地用于改进 AI 自身；但「提出正确的问题」「研究品味」这类难以验证的能力，仍是人类暂时不可替代的部分。

**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — "OpenAI's Dan Roberts: Why AI Can Now Make Discoveries"**

Dan Roberts, who leads OpenAI's Foundations of Reinforcement Learning team (a theoretical physicist by training, formerly on black holes and quantum gravity), on AI shifting from "doing the work we ask" to "autonomously making discoveries." Highlights:

- **The takeaway**: OpenAI, DeepMind, and Anthropic recently cracked long-unsolved Erdős problems. Roberts argues the key isn't just compute but a model willing to take a contrarian stance—e.g., assuming a conjecture is false—and persevering down a very long calculation path. In his words, going "against the grain and [doing] something contrarian... you really have to have strong conviction in what you're doing in order to persevere down a really long calculation path."

- **Why RL works and where it breaks**: RL's power comes from environmental feedback and learning at "the right level for you"; it breaks when rewards are extremely sparse—many actions before a single yes/no—making credit assignment brutally hard.

- **A contrarian, physics-flavored method**: Think about scaling not small-to-big but big-to-small. When a large model "groks" something a small one didn't, don't say the scaling law broke—admit you didn't understand what you were scaling, and retreat to the simplest toy model that still contains the phenomenon you care about.

- **On science**: Expect more math and science breakthroughs in the next six months and AI increasingly turned on improving AI itself—yet asking the right question and "research taste," hard-to-verify skills, remain where humans are still essential.

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oWOz2htozfI>
