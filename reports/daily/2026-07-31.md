## 1. mvanhorn/last30days-skill

⭐ 55.6k（今日 +378）· Python · [链接](https://github.com/mvanhorn/last30days-skill)

**是什么**：一个可安装的 Agent 技能，`/last30days <话题>` 就能跨 Reddit、X、YouTube、Hacker News、Polymarket 和全网检索某个话题，再用点赞、投票、预测市场赔率等真实参与度打分，合成一份有出处的简报。

**What it is**: An installable agent skill — `/last30days <topic>` researches any subject across Reddit, X, YouTube, Hacker News, Polymarket and the open web, then scores results by real engagement (upvotes, likes, prediction-market odds) to synthesize a grounded brief.

**为什么值得关注**：它把"研究"这件事标准化成一个可复用技能，兼容 Claude Code、Cursor、Copilot 等 50+ 宿主。相比一次性 prompt，它用真实社群信号而非编辑口味排序，是"信息聚合 Agent"里少见的既好用又克制的实现。

**Why it matters**: It turns "do research" into a reusable, portable skill that plugs into 50+ agent hosts. By ranking on community signal rather than editorial taste, it's one of the rare aggregation agents that is both useful and disciplined.

### 播客要点 / Podcast

**《AI & I》(Every) — 精选重播：Wired 联合创始人 Kevin Kelly 谈"AI 是一场五十年的一夜成功"**

>

*AI & I* (Every) — Best of the Pod: Wired's Kevin Kelly on why AI is a "50-year overnight success."

- 变革性技术往往是"五十年的一夜成功"：VR 从 1987 年至今体验没质变，只是从几百万美元降到 100 美元——很多东西"还在等它的 LLM 时刻"。
- 我们其实根本不知道"智能"是什么，就像牛顿当年完全不懂电——连他都对电有各种荒谬猜想。
- Kelly 的猜想：智能不是一种"元素"，而是一种"化合物"，由许多尚未识别的认知成分构成——"我们现在做的 AI 就像在造某种盐，却还不知道它由什么组成。"
- 待在前沿的方式是"去访问、别定居"：把最新的 AI 与历史阅读、动手工作平衡起来；最会看未来的人往往也是出色的历史学家。

>

- Transformative tech is often a "50-year overnight success": VR hasn't fundamentally changed since 1987, only dropped from millions of dollars to $100 — many things are "still waiting for their LLM moment."
- We genuinely don't know what intelligence *is*, as ignorant of it as Newton was of electricity — and even he held absurd ideas about it.
- Kelly's hunch: intelligence isn't an element but a *compound* of many yet-unidentified cognitive parts — "the current AI is we're making some kind of salt, and we don't even know what it's made from."
- The way to live at the frontier is to *visit, not settle*: balance the latest AI with reading history and working with your hands; the best futurists are also great historians.
