## 1. zai-org/GLM-5

⭐ 4.2k（今日新增） · Python · [链接](https://github.com/zai-org/GLM-5)

**是什么**：智谱 AI（Zhipu）开源的 GLM-5 系列大模型，含 GLM-5.2 / 5.1 / 5 三个版本，总参数 744B、激活 40B，原生 1M token 上下文，主打长程推理与 Agentic 工程，Apache 2.0 协议放出权重。

**What it is**: Zhipu's open-weight GLM-5 family (GLM-5.2 / 5.1 / 5), a 744B-total / 40B-active model with a native 1M-token context, built for long-horizon reasoning and agentic engineering, released under Apache 2.0 with downloadable weights.

**为什么值得关注**：编码基准 Terminal-Bench 2.1 拿到 81.0，支持 SGLang / vLLM / Transformers / KTransformers 部署。更有看点的是 Vercel CEO 当天就晒出：GLM-5.2 在他们的 Next.js Evals 上超过了 Opus 4.8——一个开放权重模型在真实工程评测里压过闭源旗舰，分量不轻。

**Why it matters**: It scores 81.0 on Terminal-Bench 2.1 and ships with SGLang / vLLM / Transformers / KTransformers deployment paths. The headline: Vercel's CEO showed the same day that GLM-5.2 surpassed Opus 4.8 on their Next.js Evals — an open-weight model beating a closed flagship on a real engineering benchmark is no small thing.

## 2. Kilo-Org/kilocode

**为什么值得关注**：在「编码 Agent 太多、平台太散」的当下，它试图用单一平台收敛工具链，而不是再造一个孤立的 CLI。22k 星的体量说明它已经越过了玩具阶段，适合想把 Agent 真正落到工程流程里的团队。

**Why it matters**: With coding agents fragmenting across countless CLIs, Kilo bets on consolidating the toolchain into one platform rather than shipping yet another isolated CLI. At 22k stars it's past the toy stage — a fit for teams who want agents wired into a real engineering flow.

**Why it matters**: Putting audio and video generation in one model and opening up LoRA training lets creators fine-tune stylized A/V pipelines locally instead of renting a closed API. Open-source video generation is closing in on the proprietary frontier fast, and this is one to watch.

**Why it matters**: Its pitch is "not another SDK": progress survives failures and restarts, it runs locally via CLI or deploys to Node.js / Cloudflare / GitHub Actions / GitLab CI, and it ships native OpenTelemetry, Braintrust, and Sentry hooks. Coming from the Astro team, the engineering pedigree is credible.

## 5. yifanfeng97/Hyper-Extract

⭐ 1.8k（今日新增） · Python · [链接](https://github.com/yifanfeng97/Hyper-Extract)

**是什么**：用 LLM 把非结构化文本转成结构化知识，输出图、超图等多种结构。

**What it is**: Hyper-Extract turns unstructured text into structured knowledge with LLMs, producing graphs, hypergraphs, and more.

**为什么值得关注**：多数「文本转知识图谱」工具止步于普通二元关系图，它把超图（一条边能连多个节点）也纳入，更适合表达多方参与的复杂关系，给 RAG 和知识工程提供了更丰富的结构选项。

**Why it matters**: Most "text-to-knowledge-graph" tools stop at plain binary-relation graphs; Hyper-Extract adds hypergraphs (one edge linking many nodes), better suited to multi-party relationships and giving RAG and knowledge-engineering pipelines a richer structural option.

### 🎧 播客 / Podcast

**AI & I by Every：《GitHub's COO Explains Why AI Hasn't Replaced Developers》（嘉宾：GitHub COO Kyle Daigle）**

一句话要点：AI 没有取代开发者，而是把每个开发者变成了「一个人 + 一群 Agent」的指挥官，GitHub 正在为这波指数级增长重铺底座。

The takeaway: AI hasn't replaced developers — it's turning each one into a commander of "one human plus many agents," and GitHub is re-laying its foundations for that exponential surge.

- 量级惊人：3 月单月就有 1700 万个由 Agent 创建的 PR，今年提交量有望从去年的约 10 亿冲到 140 亿。Kyle 强调这不是「全是 slop」，而是行业正越过早期采用阶段、向上爬坡。/ The scale is staggering: 17M agent-authored PRs in March alone, with commits on track to jump from ~1B last year toward 14B. Kyle insists it isn't "all slop" — the industry is climbing past early adoption.

- GitHub 的定位是「为开发者而非买家造工具」；面对开源维护者被 PR 洪水淹没的困境，它选择给维护者更多控制权（如 vouch 信任机制）而非强推统一标准。/ GitHub builds "for developers, not buyers"; facing maintainers drowning in PRs, it gives them more control (e.g. vouch systems) rather than imposing a single standard.

- 关于「200 美元订阅怎么不变成 2000 美元」：答案是 frontier-tuning 让模型更懂你 + 自动「模型路由器」按任务难度选模型——琐碎的改名、查找替换没必要动用最贵的前沿模型，工具应该替你降到 Haiku 这种小模型。/ On keeping a $200 subscription from ballooning to $2,000: frontier-tuning models to know you, plus auto "model routers" that pick by task — trivial renames shouldn't burn the priciest frontier model; the tooling should drop to a small model like Haiku for you.

- 内部文化是「爬山（hill climbing）」：每周看 evals，用工具使用数据（点赞/点踩、采纳率）迭代模型，并警惕「硬指标上升但用户体感崩盘」的过拟合。/ The internal culture is "hill climbing": weekly evals, iterating models on tool-usage data (thumbs up/down, acceptance), while watching for overfitting where hard metrics rise but user sentiment crashes.

- 最有意思的细节：Kyle 自己养了个名叫 Baxter 的 OpenClaw Agent，每天读完他的邮件和 Slack，反过来给他一份「沟通改进报告」。他的金句——「人类远比从其他人那里、更愿意接受来自机器人的批评意见。」/ The most striking detail: Kyle runs a personal OpenClaw agent named Baxter that reads his emails and Slack daily and hands him a comms-improvement report. His line: "humans are way more willing to take critical feedback from robots than other humans."
