**Why it matters**: It upgrades "agents using a browser" from brittle automation scripts to a first-class native experience, claiming up to 2.5× faster task completion. As more work happens in the browser, owning the "human-and-agent shared browser context" owns a key on-ramp for real-world agents.

**Why it matters**: While tools like Grammarly ship your text to the cloud, harper offers a local-inference, privacy-preserving alternative; backed by Automattic (the company behind WordPress), its engineering quality and sustainability are solid — today's standout non-AI utility.

## 10. apache/ossie

**是什么**：Apache 旗下的一项行业规范努力，旨在标准化分析、AI 与 BI 平台之间的"语义元数据"交换。

**What it is**: An Apache-hosted industry specification effort to standardize the exchange of semantic metadata across analytics, AI and BI platforms.

**为什么值得关注**：数据孤岛的根源之一是各平台对"字段含义"各说各话。ossie 想在 AI/BI/分析之间立一套共同语义标准，属于不性感但极关键的底层基础设施；有 Apache 背书，值得数据工程与平台团队长期跟踪。

**Why it matters**: A root cause of data silos is that every platform describes field meaning differently. ossie aims to set a shared semantic standard across AI/BI/analytics — unglamorous but critical plumbing; with Apache's backing, it's worth long-term tracking by data-engineering and platform teams.
