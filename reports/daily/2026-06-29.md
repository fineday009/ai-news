### 🎧 播客要点 / Podcast

**Training Data — Memory and Continual Learning: Engram's Dan Biderman and Jessy Lin**

要点 / The Takeaway：让模型更有用的瓶颈不再是"原始智能"，而是把不断变化的新情境真正学进权重里。

- Engram（一家专注记忆与持续学习的"NeoLab"）联合创始人 Dan Biderman 与 Jessy Lin 认为，外置记忆数据库 + 上下文工程只是权宜之计；真正的解法是把情境像预训练那样"烤进"模型权重。
- 他们为每个团队训练专属小模型（合作方包括 Notion、Microsoft、Harvey），用 LoRA、前缀、稀疏架构等 adapter 微调，配合 SFT、RL、on-policy 蒸馏，让模型像"在公司干了多年的老员工"一样理解上下文。
- 与前沿大厂"一个越来越大的通用模型"路线相反，他们押注"人人都有自己的模型"：很多知识是私有的、甚至彼此冲突的，永远不会进入公开的后训练数据集。

- Dan Biderman and Jessy Lin, co-founders of Engram (a "NeoLab" focused on memory and continual learning), argue that an external memory database plus context engineering is only a stopgap; the real fix is baking context into model weights the way pretraining does.
- They train per-team small models (partners include Notion, Microsoft, Harvey) using adapter fine-tuning — LoRA, prefixes, sparse architectures — alongside SFT, RL, and on-policy distillation, so a model understands context like "an employee who's worked at the company for years."
- Against the frontier-lab bet on one ever-bigger general model, they wager on "everybody has their own model": much knowledge is private or even conflicting and will never appear in a public post-training dataset.

> "我们设想这样一个世界：每个人都拥有自己的模型。" — Jessy Lin
>
> "We imagine this world where everybody has their own model." — Jessy Lin
