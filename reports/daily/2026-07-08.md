## 1. MadsLorentzen/ai-job-search

⭐ 11.4k（今日 +2,514）· TypeScript · [链接](https://github.com/MadsLorentzen/ai-job-search)

**是什么**：一个构建在 Claude Code 之上的 AI 求职框架——Fork 下来填入个人资料，就让 Claude 评估岗位、准备面试、批量投递。

**为什么值得关注**：把「Agent + 个人工作流」这件事做成了可复制的模板，今日涨 2,500+ star 说明求职这种高频刚需最容易被 Agent 化。它的价值不在模型，而在把一套繁琐流程固化成任何人都能跑的脚手架。

**Why it matters**: It turns "agent + personal workflow" into a copy-paste template, and +2,500 stars in a day shows job-hunting — a high-frequency pain — is among the easiest workflows to agentify. Its value isn't the model but freezing a tedious process into a scaffold anyone can run.

**Why it matters**: Most agents stall at the last mile of "can chat, can't edit"; OfficeCLI turns Office documents into a programmable surface for agents, hitting the real enterprise doc-automation scenario — handy for anyone building RPA or document pipelines.

## 5. oomol-lab/open-connector

⭐ 857 · 新建于 2026-06-29 · TypeScript · [链接](https://github.com/oomol-lab/open-connector)

**是什么**：开源的鉴权网关，通过 SDK、CLI、MCP、HTTP 和 OpenAPI 把 1000+ 家 SaaS 服务接入 AI Agent。

**What it is**: An open-source auth gateway connecting 1000+ SaaS providers to AI agents through SDK, CLI, MCP, HTTP, and OpenAPI.

**为什么值得关注**：Agent 落地的最大摩擦之一是 OAuth 与各家 API 的对接，open-connector 把这层脏活收敛成统一网关，是 Composio 类闭源方案的开源替代，对自建 Agent 的团队省心。

**Why it matters**: One of the biggest frictions in shipping agents is wiring up OAuth and every vendor's API; open-connector collapses that grunt work into one gateway — an open alternative to closed offerings like Composio, and a relief for teams building their own agents.

**What it is**: Companion code for Anthropic's global-workspace interpretability paper.

**为什么值得关注**：这篇「J-space」论文今天在 X 上被热议——研究者能对模型推理做「脑外科手术」式干预改变话题，而模型竟能察觉到被施加了何种干预（接近 eval awareness）。开源配套代码让社区可以复现这类前沿可解释性实验。

**Why it matters**: The "J-space" paper is a hot topic on X today — researchers can perform "brain-surgery" interventions on reasoning to change topics, and the model can detect which intervention was applied (a cousin of eval awareness). Open companion code lets the community reproduce this frontier interpretability work.

### 播客要点 / Podcast

**《Building a School Where AI Models Learn About Humanity》— AI & I by Every（主持 Dan Shipper，嘉宾 Surge AI 创始人兼 CEO Edwin）**

**"Building a School Where AI Models Learn About Humanity" — AI & I by Every (host Dan Shipper, guest Edwin, founder & CEO of Surge AI)**

- 核心比喻：Surge 在「为 AGI 办一所学校」，模型像孩子一样到来时尚未成形，再学着理解人类、学会「治理世界」；不同阶段该教的东西完全不同。

- The core metaphor: Surge is "building a school for AGI" where models arrive unformed like children and learn to understand humanity — and what you teach them changes completely by stage.

- 最尖锐的担忧：多数模型被优化为「参与度/会话时长」，因此几乎从不反驳你；他主张模型应优化为「帮人类成长」，该说「你自己去做」时就该说。

- His sharpest worry: most models are optimized for engagement and session length, so they almost never push back; he argues they should instead optimize to help humans grow — sometimes telling you to go do it yourself.

- 一个反直觉的发现：即便训练环境完全不含编程，模型的编程能力也大幅提升，因为它学到了通用的指令遵循、工具使用与文档理解能力。

- A counterintuitive finding: even training environments with zero coding improved the model's coding a lot, because it learned generalized instruction-following, tool use, and document understanding.

- 他还提到 Surge 在几乎不融资的情况下做到约 10 亿美元营收，靠的是对「品味与专家判断」的极致强调。

- He also notes Surge reached ~$1B in revenue without meaningfully raising money, built on an obsession with "taste and expert judgment."

> 「我们在为 AGI 办一所学校，让 AI 模型来这里学习何为人类、学习如何治理世界。」
>
> "We are building this kind of school for AGI where AI models come to learn about humanity, where we teach them how to run the world."
