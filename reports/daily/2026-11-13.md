## 1. CopilotKit/OpenBot

⭐ 11.7k（今日 +298）· TypeScript · [链接](https://github.com/CopilotKit/OpenBot)

**是什么**：开源的 AI "同事"——每个 Agent 都拥有自己的一台电脑：浏览器、文件和工具，且每一步操作在执行前都先定好。

**What it is**: Open-source AI coworkers that each get a computer of their own—a browser, files and tools—with every action decided before it happens.

**为什么值得关注**：10 月 30 日上日报时 8.2k 星，如今 1.17 万，定位也更明确了：一个模块化、可自托管的"AI 同事"工作区——自选模型、接入你已有的 Agent、基础设施自己掌握。Bot 用自己的浏览器和工具干活，用可交互的结果回复，发出前等你批准，需要时你可以接管。笔记本上有一体化的 Mac/Windows 安装包（前端加基于 Docker 的后端），团队版加上企业登录、工具权限、审批和审计，并支持 Kubernetes 部署。出自 AG-UI 协议的作者 CopilotKit，适合想把 Agent 放进真实工作流、又要守住审批与审计的团队。

**Why it matters**: It had 8.2k stars when it made the daily on October 30 and is at 11.7k now, with a sharper pitch: a modular, self-hostable workspace for AI coworkers—choose your models, bring your agents, own your infrastructure. A bot works in its own browser with its own tools, answers with interactive results and waits for your approval before it sends, and you can take over when it needs you. There's a self-contained Mac/Windows app for laptops (frontend plus a Docker-based backend), and the business edition adds company sign-in, tool permissions, approvals and audit, with Kubernetes deployment. From CopilotKit, creators of the AG-UI protocol—for teams that want agents in real workflows without giving up approvals and audit.

## 2. QwenLM/qwen-code

⭐ 29.9k（今日 +151）· TypeScript · [链接](https://github.com/QwenLM/qwen-code)

**是什么**：开源 AI 编码 Agent，可用于终端、编辑器、桌面、浏览器和聊天工具。

**What it is**: The open-source AI coding agent for your terminal, editor, desktop, browser, and chat.

**为什么值得关注**：README 置顶的 Qwen Code 1.0 Preview 把 Qoder 的 Agent harness 能力合并进开源版本。它开箱即带自动记忆、自动 Skills、子 Agent、Agent Teams 和 MCP；同时支持 OpenAI、Anthropic、Gemini、Qwen 等协议，可接任意第三方服务商或 Ollama/vLLM 本地模型并在运行时切换；除终端外还有 IDE 插件、桌面应用、Web UI、SDK，以及 Telegram、钉钉、微信、飞书等聊天集成。框架与 Qwen 模型都开源，是不想被单一厂商锁定的团队的一个完整选项。

**Why it matters**: The Qwen Code 1.0 Preview pinned in the README brings the agent harness capabilities of Qoder into the open-source release. It ships Auto-Memory, Auto-Skills, SubAgents, Agent Teams and MCP out of the box; speaks the OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini and Qwen APIs, works with any third-party provider or local models via Ollama/vLLM and switches at runtime; and goes beyond the terminal with IDE plugins, a desktop app, a web UI, SDKs and chat integrations (Telegram, DingTalk, WeChat, Feishu). Both the framework and the Qwen models are open source—a complete option for teams that don't want vendor lock-in.

## 3. ashhart/TensorFold

⭐ 2.0k（今日 +54）· Zig · [链接](https://github.com/ashhart/TensorFold)

**是什么**：面向 Metal、CUDA 和 Vulkan 的 LLM 推理引擎。

**What it is**: LLM Inference Engine for Metal, CUDA and Vulkan.

**为什么值得关注**：一个 Zig 写的单二进制推理服务：自己读取权重、做分词、直接调用 Metal 或 CUDA kernel，部署时不需要 Python 或 MLX。Mac 上用 Homebrew 安装即可服务 MLX 格式的量化模型，Linux 上需要 R580 以上驱动（CUDA 13）。另一个有意思的点是协作方式：项目要求贡献者（包括编码 Agent）先通过作者的 Agent Traffic Control 工具认领 issue、定时上报进度，并在公开"雷达"上显示，以避免多个 Agent 重复或冲突地改代码。

**Why it matters**: A single Zig binary that serves models on its own: it reads checkpoints, tokenizes requests and runs Metal or CUDA kernels directly, with no Python or MLX needed for serving. On a Mac it installs via Homebrew and serves quantized MLX-format checkpoints; on Linux it needs driver R580 or newer (CUDA 13). Its contribution model is just as interesting: contributors—coding agents included—claim issues through the author's Agent Traffic Control tool, pulse progress on a timer and show up on a public "radar," so multiple agents don't duplicate or collide on the same code.

## 4. Alishahryar1/free-claude-code

⭐ 69.4k（今日 +415）· Python · [链接](https://github.com/Alishahryar1/free-claude-code)

**是什么**：把 Claude Code、Codex、Antigravity、VS Code、Pi、OpenCode 等编码 Agent 接到免费（及其他）模型服务商上使用。

**What it is**: Use Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity, VSCode, Pi, and OpenCode (and other harnesses) with models from free and other providers.

**为什么值得关注**：8 月 24 日上日报时 4.8 万星，如今 6.94 万。它本质是一个路由层：把 12 种 harness 的请求转发到 61 家服务商（免费、付费、订阅和本地都有），保留原生流式输出、工具调用和交错思考，并内置重试等容错，让服务商出故障时也能继续写代码；还集成了 RTK、Headroom 来压缩 token 用量。项目声明与 Anthropic 无关，并称会遵守服务商条款、在不再被允许时移除集成——使用前仍建议自行核实各上游的服务条款。

**Why it matters**: It had 48.0k stars when it made the daily on August 24 and has 69.4k now. At heart it's a routing layer: it sends requests from 12 harnesses to 61 providers (free, paid, subscription and local), preserving native streaming, tool use and interleaved thinking, with automatic retries and other reliability features so you can keep coding through provider outages; it also integrates RTK and Headroom to cut token usage. The project states it isn't affiliated with Anthropic and says it follows provider terms and removes integrations that stop being allowed—still, check each upstream's terms of service before using it.

## 5. amosblomqvist/learnharness

⭐ 9.1k · TypeScript · 创建于 2026-10-16 · [链接](https://github.com/amosblomqvist/learnharness)

**是什么**：用来学习难懂知识的 Agent 外壳：Agent 先测出你的理解边界，再规划并讲授从现有理解到目标的课程。

**What it is**: Agent harness for learning difficult things. AI agents plan and teach lessons from your current understanding to your goal, building a detailed picture of what you understand and how you reason.

**为什么值得关注**：10 月 28 日上日报时 3.2k 星，两周后已达 9.1k（取自本周新建高星仓库候选）。流程是"测量 → 规划 → 讲授 → 练习"：先找出你在每个前置知识点上懂到哪，再画出到目标的依赖图，一步一推理地讲，并用不点名知识点的变式题做练习；"把思路说出来"的输入框让 Agent 看到你的推理而不只是对错。学到的内容沉淀为一张持久的命题与推理链图谱，跨会话复用。

**Why it matters**: It had 3.2k stars when it made the daily on October 28 and is at 9.1k two weeks later (picked from this week's high-star new-repo candidates). The loop is measure → plan → teach → practice: it finds where your understanding ends on each prerequisite, maps the dependencies to your goal, teaches one reasoning step at a time, and practices with varied problems that don't name the concept up front; "think out loud" fields let the agent see your reasoning, not just right or wrong answers. What you learn accumulates in a persistent graph of propositions and reasoning chains that later sessions reuse.

⭐ 134.6k（今日 +176）· C++ · [链接](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp)

**是什么**：用 C/C++ 实现的 LLM 推理。

**What it is**: LLM inference in C/C++.

**为什么值得关注**：本地推理的事实标准，13.5 万星仍保持每天上百星的增长。今天榜上同时出现了 TensorFold 这样的新挑战者，正好对照：llama.cpp 胜在硬件与模型覆盖面最广、生态最成熟（GGUF 格式、大量上层应用都基于它），新引擎则在特定后端和部署体验上做文章。想在自己机器上跑开源模型，它依然是第一站。

With a newcomer like TensorFold on today's chart, the contrast is useful: llama.cpp wins on the broadest hardware and model coverage and the most mature ecosystem (the GGUF format, plus a long tail of apps built on it), while newer engines compete on specific backends and deployment experience. For running open models on your own machine, it's still the first stop.

## 7. libracoder/kiri

⭐ 1.7k · Rust · 创建于 2026-11-02 · [链接](https://github.com/libracoder/kiri)

**是什么**：一个小巧、快速的容器运行时，支持 macOS、Windows 和 Linux，直接运行你的 Docker 镜像，兼容现有的 docker CLI、Compose 和 buildx。

**What it is**: A tiny, fast container runtime for macOS, Windows and Linux. Runs your Docker images and works with the docker CLI, Compose and buildx you already use.

**为什么值得关注**：定位是 Docker Desktop 和 OrbStack 的轻量替代：用 Rust 写成，在一台小 Linux 虚拟机里（Linux 上直接用内核）跑容器，并实现 Docker Engine API，所以现有工具照常可用。README 的数据是通过 API 启动并退出一个容器约 2.5 ms，约为 Docker Desktop 的 80 倍快；虚拟机空闲 60 秒后休眠，守护进程约 15 MB。还带 `*.kiri.local` 本地域名与 HTTPS、一条命令起单节点 Kubernetes，以及从 OrbStack/Docker Desktop 一键迁移镜像和卷。

**Why it matters**: It's pitched as a lightweight replacement for Docker Desktop and OrbStack: written in Rust, it runs containers in one small Linux VM (or straight on the kernel on Linux) and speaks the Docker Engine API, so your existing tools just work. The README reports a container starting and exiting in about 2.5 ms through the API—around 80x faster than Docker Desktop—with the VM sleeping after 60 s idle and the daemon at about 15 MB. It also ships `*.kiri.local` local domains with HTTPS, a one-command single-node Kubernetes, and `kiri import` to bring images and volumes over from OrbStack or Docker Desktop.

## 8. Verlintas/NOIR

⭐ 3.3k · Kotlin · 创建于 2026-10-31 · [链接](https://github.com/Verlintas/NOIR)

**是什么**：手机隐私审计：用 adb 扫出 Android 应用的权限、上报端点与数据流向，并给出可回滚的处置。

**What it is**: Desktop Android leak audit: scans an Android device over adb for app permissions, reporting endpoints and data flows, and proposes reversible fixes.

**为什么值得关注**：11 月 4 日上日报时 1.8k 星，如今 3.3k。它把"这台手机到底能泄露什么"变成可复现的报告：在设备端从 APK 中抽取 URL、按厂商与类别（遥测、广告、定位等）打分，把权限翻译成"能拿到什么数据"再与上报域名配对，并为每条处置生成回滚命令；不联网、不需要 root、默认不卸载任何东西。和近期一波"安卓去广告/电视去臃肿"项目相比，它更偏审计与取证，适合想先看清再动手的人。

**Why it matters**: It had 1.8k stars when it made the daily on November 4 and has 3.3k now. It turns "what can this phone actually leak?" into a reproducible report: it extracts URLs from APKs on-device, scores apps by vendor and category (telemetry, ads, location and more), translates permissions into the data they expose and pairs them with reporting domains, then generates a rollback command for every action. No network, no root, and nothing is uninstalled by default. Compared with the recent wave of Android/TV debloaters, it leans toward audit and forensics—for people who want to see clearly before touching anything.

## 9. s1dashu/quiet-md

⭐ 1.7k · Rust · 创建于 2026-11-01 · [链接](https://github.com/s1dashu/quiet-md)

**是什么**：快速、安静的本地笔记应用和富文本编辑器，用 Rust + GPUI 原生绘制，支持 macOS、Windows 和 Linux；本地优先。

**What it is**: Blazing-fast native notes app and rich-text editor for macOS, Windows, and Linux. Built with Rust + GPUI. Local-first.

**为什么值得关注**：整个界面由 Rust 和 GPUI（Zed 的 UI 框架）在 GPU 上绘制，没有 WebView 和 Electron；README 给出的数据是在 100 万字、549 个章节的文档里，打字、删除和滚动的 P95 帧耗时都在 6 ms 以内。笔记以飞书文档的 DocxXML 格式存为普通文件夹里的一个个 XML 文件，无账号、默认不联网，可以用 Git 或 iCloud 自行同步，复制粘贴和导入导出都能保留结构。目前仍是 beta，适合重度写作又讨厌臃肿笔记软件的人。

**Why it matters**: The whole interface is drawn on the GPU with Rust and GPUI (Zed's UI framework)—no WebView, no Electron; per the README, typing, deleting and scrolling in a one-million-character, 549-chapter document all stay under 6 ms per frame at P95. Notes are saved in Feishu (Lark) Docs' DocxXML format as one XML file each in an ordinary folder, with no account and nothing sent over the network by default, so you can sync with Git or iCloud and copy-paste or import/export keeps its structure. Still in beta—for heavy writers tired of bloated note apps.

It keeps IDEA's muscle memory and swaps out the foundation—like Quiet.md and Kiri on today's list, part of the wave of rewriting bloated desktop software natively in Rust.
