### 播客要点 / Podcast

**No Priors —《Coinbase 的「万物交易所」：Agentic Finance、稳定币与代币化》，嘉宾 Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong**

**No Priors — "Coinbase's Everything Exchange: Agentic Finance, Stablecoins, and Tokenization," with CEO Brian Armstrong**

- **给 AI 开银行账户**：Armstrong 认为 AI Agent 需要自己的金融账户，一条 prompt 就能让 Agent 拥有自托管钱包、走加密轨道无需 KYC；他指出约 76% 的 Agent 电商交易低于 30¢，而信用卡约 30¢ 的固定费根本撑不起微支付。金句：「我们不想让 AI 成为无银行账户者——如果要给 AI 开户，它们也理应享有金融服务。」

  **Banking the AIs**: Armstrong argues AI agents need their own financial accounts — a single prompt gives an agent a self-custodial wallet on crypto rails with no KYC; he notes ~76% of agent e-commerce transactions are under 30¢, while a card's ~30¢ flat fee can't support micropayments. His line: "We don't want the AIs to be unbanked. If we want to bank the AIs, they deserve financial services as well."

- **万物交易所**：股票、大宗商品、加密、衍生品、永续合约、预测市场都在上链、集中到一处交易；Coinbase 已有 88% 的收入来自非比特币交易。

  **The Everything Exchange**: stocks, commodities, crypto, derivatives, perpetuals and prediction markets are all coming on-chain to trade in one place; 88% of Coinbase revenue now comes from non-Bitcoin trading.

- **稳定币与代币化**：稳定币支付即便在比特币下跌时仍持续高增长，可在一秒内、不到 1¢ 完成全球转账；Coinbase 还推出了首个真正 1:1 托管的代币化股票（美国以外），Armstrong 预期私人信贷、国债、银行存款等「一切都会被代币化」。

  **Stablecoins and tokenization**: stablecoin payments keep growing even when Bitcoin falls, moving money globally in under a second for under 1¢; Coinbase launched the first truly 1:1-custodied tokenized stock (outside the US), and Armstrong expects "everything" — private credit, treasuries, bank deposits — to get tokenized.

- **AI 与公司规模**：他认为 AI 消灭的是任务而非人——现有团队会做得更多、节奏更快、利润率随之上升，同时也会出现能量极大的 2–5 人小公司。

  **AI and company size**: he sees AI eliminating tasks, not people — existing teams get far more done at higher velocity and margins, even as tiny 2–5 person companies punch far above their weight.
