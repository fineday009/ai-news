### 播客 / Podcast

**《The AI Workflows Behind Every's Consulting Team》— AI & I by Every**（嘉宾 Natalia，Every 咨询主管；主持 Dan Shipper）

要点：

- **知识工作正在变成「园艺」**：你负责营造生长的条件，而不是亲手造出那株植物——这是对「与智能体协作」最贴切的比喻之一。

- **AI 员工「Claudie」已从新奇变日常**：它每天跑 CRM、发销售提案、管理看板，还有自我评估的「trust battery」循环；但它擅长执行标准流程，仍需要持续的监督、管理与「品味」上的方向。

- **能做 ≠ 该做**：即使可以 vibe code 出一套 CRM/CMS，团队最终还是选择购买 Attio、上 Asana——因为维护数据质量、把上千条确定性规则做对，本身就是别人专门在做的生意。「问题不是你能不能建，而是你该不该长期建和维护它。」

- **软件是骨骼，语言模型是大脑与韧带**：软件提供确定性的结构与规则，模型提供灵活性，二者配合才成体；AI 无法一次性写出真实软件所需的全部规则。

- **Codex 对非技术运营者「改变人生」**：把终端和浏览器直接放进聊天界面、配上强大的模型，让不懂代码的人也能「感受到算力」，真正动手构建。

Key points:

- **Knowledge work is becoming gardening**: you create the conditions for growth rather than making the plant by hand — one of the aptest metaphors for collaborating with agents.

- **The AI employee "Claudie" has gone from novel to routine**: it runs the CRM daily, sends sales proposals, manages dashboards, and now has a self-evaluating "trust battery" loop; but it excels at executing standard procedures and still needs constant oversight, management, and direction on taste.

- **Can-build ≠ should-build**: even able to vibe-code a CRM/CMS, the team chose to buy Attio and adopt Asana — because maintaining data quality and getting thousands of deterministic rules right is itself a business others do full-time. "The question is not whether you can build it, but whether you should build and maintain it."

- **Software is bones, the language model is brain and ligaments**: software provides deterministic structure and rules, the model provides flexibility, and they only work as a body together; AI won't one-shot all the rules real software needs.

- **Codex was "life changing" for a non-technical operator**: putting the terminal and browser directly in the chat interface with a powerful model lets someone who can't code "feel the compute" and actually build.
