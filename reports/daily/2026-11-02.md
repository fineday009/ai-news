**Why it matters**: Today's top gainer, with a third of its total stars added today. It reduces voice input to hold, speak, release, transcribing on-device and pasting into whatever app you're in; after the first-run model download, nothing leaves your machine. It comes from anomalyco, the team behind opencode, covers all three desktop platforms plus ARM, and has an unusual maintenance model: no pull requests, only issues, which "our agents usually turn around fast." For anyone dictating requirements to coding agents all day, it's a convenient input layer that doesn't leak.

**What it is**: FreeToken brings datacenter-scale model serving to your desktop. Run massive models locally, fast and efficiently.

**为什么值得关注**：一个面向边缘设备的 MoE 推理引擎，目标是在游戏 PC 上以交互速度运行 290B+ 的前沿 MoE 模型。核心做法是把 GPU、CPU、内存和互联当成一个弹性推理平台：按带宽自适应的 CPU–GPU 协同执行、全局 LRU 专家缓存、运行时在专家缓存与 KV 之间动态分配显存；还有针对 Agent 场景的"语义锚点"缓存，工具调用或思考块被改动时不必重算整段上下文。提供 Anthropic/OpenAI 兼容 API，配有论文，作者名单包括 Ion Stoica、Matei Zaharia、Song Han 等。

**Why it matters**: An edge-native MoE serving engine aiming to run 290B+ frontier MoE models at interactive speeds on a gaming PC. There's also agent-aware "semantic anchor" caching, so edits to tool calls or thinking blocks don't force recomputing the whole context. It exposes Anthropic/OpenAI-compatible APIs and comes with a paper whose authors include Ion Stoica, Matei Zaharia and Song Han.

**是什么**：BLA（Blind LLM Agents）——保护二进制、Electron 应用、JS 包、Python 和 WebAssembly 免遭 REA 等 AI 逆向工程 Agent 分析的对抗性防护盾：投毒 → 耗尽 → 致幻。

**What it is**: BLA (Blind LLM Agents)—an adversarial defense shield that protects binaries, Electron apps, JS bundles, Python, and WebAssembly against AI reverse-engineering agents like REA. Poison -> Exhaust -> Hallucinate.

**为什么值得关注**：今年星数第一的新仓库 rea 让"一条命令逆向任意软件"成为现实，BLA 就是它的反制面：在你发布的产物里植入格式合法的诱饵和假控制流、把产物推过分析器的资源预算、让 Agent 拿到截断的清单——而运行时行为保持不变。一句 `bla harden .` 即可完成审计→加固→验证，也能注册为 MCP 让编码 Agent 代劳。作者明确定位为保护自有软件的防御工具。AI 逆向与反 AI 逆向的军备竞赛，正式开始了。

**Why it matters**: rea, the year's most-starred new repo, made "reverse-engineer any software in one command" real; BLA is the counter-move. It plants format-valid decoys and fake control flow in the artifacts you ship, pushes them past the analyzer's resource budgets and leaves the agent with truncated inventories—while runtime behavior stays the same. One `bla harden .` runs audit → protect → verify, and it can register as an MCP server so your coding agent does it for you. The author positions it explicitly as a defensive tool for your own software. The arms race between AI reverse engineering and anti-AI reverse engineering has officially begun.

### 推文精选 / Tweets

**Box CEO Aaron Levie** 给出一个检验"AI 用得够不够狠"的简单标准：如果你现在做的事在没有 AI 时也做得出来，那多半还没把它用到位。他认为大约半年前，AI 最多只能让你更快做出原本就能做的东西；而现在，与 AI 协作的产出应当在很短时间内显著超越以往——原型不仅能交互、功能还更好，架构能拿下以前不敢追求的性能，研究能穷尽一个主题的所有事实，业务流程能以人做不到的方式从过往失败中学习。

**Box CEO Aaron Levie** offered a simple test for whether you're pushing AI enough: if you could have done what you're doing before AI, you probably aren't. Until maybe six months ago, he argues, the best AI could do was help you produce what you already would, a little faster; now the output of working with AI can and should dramatically exceed what was possible before, in a fraction of the time—prototypes that are fully interactive with better features, architectures that chase performance gains you couldn't before, research that covers every fact on a subject, and business processes that learn from past failures in ways no human could. ### 播客要点 / Podcast

**《Latent Space》：用 Agent 处理长周期金融工作——Arca 的 Aman Singh**（AI Engineer New York 2026 现场访谈）。Arca 是刚融资 6400 万美元的 AI 原生财富管理公司，Singh 负责其 AI 平台团队。一句话结论：在高风险行业，"拥有自己的 harness"才是护城河。

- **AI 不取代顾问，而是取代顾问周围的所有工作**：客户并不直接和 AI 打交道，体验到的是更主动、更及时的人工服务；用 Singh 的话说，在 Arca"Agent 提示人，多于人提示 Agent"。一项任务可以横跨 10 天，Agent 靠每位客户的持久记忆加每日"反思"（dreaming）流程，在合适的时间把合适的人拉进来。
- **模型中立、自建 harness**：Claude 是主力，但他们换过 Gemini、OpenAI，一切以内部基准为准——某个外部跑分亮眼的模型在他们那里"太慢也太贵"。按任务复杂度分档调度模型，顾问完全无感；也不打算依赖实验室的托管 Agent 产品，因为"把记忆和运营知识交出去，就失去了让 AI 真正改变业务的控制权"。
- **护栏放在能力边缘，且尽量确定性**：Agent 能做全部分析，但任何资金划转、资产变现、直接对客沟通都必须人工批准，目前不做自主交易；每次写库都有快速 LLM 校验，外部输入做提示注入检测，数据外发既有 PII 的确定性检查，也有小模型共识防泄露。沙箱里生成的代码会被转成严格格式后丢弃，产出的财务计划 PDF 还要经过合规检查（如不得承诺收益）。
- **审计即默认，反馈即评测**：每个动作（无论 Agent 还是人）都留下"改了什么、谁改的、何时"的变更日志，既满足合规又反哺 Agent 循环。员工的每次修改或取消都要附原因并成为 eval；每晚由一个元 Agent 分析真实轨迹、建议改 harness，但提示词改动仍需工程师把关——因为 Agent 自己改提示词时"每个小修补都会变成整段提示词"。
- **用"决策模型"纠正过度自信**：模型会理直气壮地用 2025 年的税率表做分析，所以在做出需要金融严谨性的建议前，决策模型会提醒它查经过验证的知识库或联网搜索。

**Latent Space: Using Agents for Long-Horizon Financial Work — Aman Singh, Arca** (recorded at AI Engineer New York 2026). Arca, an AI-native wealth-management firm that recently raised $64M, has Singh leading its AI platform team. The takeaway: in a high-stakes industry, owning your harness is the moat.

- **AI doesn't replace the advisor—it replaces all the work around the advisor**: clients don't interact with AI directly; they experience more proactive, responsive human service. In Singh's words, at Arca "agents prompt people more than people prompt agents." A single task can span 10 days, and agents use per-client persistent memory plus a daily reflection ("dreaming") process to pull the right person in at the right time.
- **Model-agnostic, in-house harness**: Claude is the primary model, but they've moved through Gemini and OpenAI before, guided entirely by internal benchmarks—one model with strong external scores proved too slow and too costly for them. Models are tiered by task complexity, invisibly to advisors, and they avoid depending on labs' managed-agent products, since handing over memory and operational knowledge means losing the control you need for AI to really shape the business.
- **Guardrails at the capability edges, deterministic where possible**: agents do all the analysis, but any money movement, liquidation or direct client communication needs human approval, and there's no autonomous trading today. Every database write gets a fast LLM verification, inbound content is screened for prompt injection, and outbound data gets both deterministic PII checks and a consensus of small models against exfiltration. Code written in the sandbox is translated into a strict format and thrown away, and generated financial-plan PDFs go through compliance checks (no promising returns, for example).
- **Auditability by default, feedback as evals**: every action, by agent or human, produces a change log of what changed, who changed it and when—serving compliance and feeding the agent loop. Every staff edit or cancellation comes with a reason and becomes an eval; a nightly meta-agent analyzes real traces and suggests harness changes, but an engineer still reviews prompt changes, because when agents edit prompts themselves "every little fix becomes the whole prompt."
- **Decision models to check overconfidence**: models will happily use 2025 tax brackets in an analysis, so before any recommendation needing financial rigor, a decision model nudges the agent to consult a knowledge base of verified current facts or search the web.
