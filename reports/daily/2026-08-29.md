## 1. abhigyanpatwari/GitNexus

⭐ 46.2k（今日 +202）· TypeScript · [链接](https://github.com/abhigyanpatwari/GitNexus)

**是什么**：把代码库索引成客户端知识图谱，并通过 MCP 工具暴露给 AI agent（Claude Code、Cursor 等），可本地 CLI 或纯浏览器运行。

**为什么值得关注**：它给"让 agent 真正读懂大型代码库"提供了一条不依赖向量检索的路——用可解释的图谱表达调用链与依赖关系，且完全在本地/浏览器运行，隐私与可解释性都更好。适合被大代码库幻觉困扰、想给 agent 补上"架构级理解"的团队。

**Why it matters**: It offers a non-vector path to "let agents truly read a large codebase"—expressing call chains and dependencies as an explainable graph that runs entirely local/in-browser, improving both privacy and interpretability. A fit for teams tired of hallucinations on big repos who want to give their agent architecture-level understanding.

## 2. tailscale/tailcat

⭐ 2.8k（今日 +965）· Go · [链接](https://github.com/tailscale/tailcat)

**是什么**：一个类 netcat 的工具，直接跑在 Tailscale 的数据平面之上。

**What it is**: A netcat-like utility that operates directly over Tailscale's data plane.

**为什么值得关注**：官方出品，把最经典的网络瑞士军刀 netcat 搬到 WireGuard/tailnet 之上——无需公网端口、无需打洞，即可在私有网络里做端口转发与管道传输。对每天和内网、跳板机打交道的运维与开发是即取即用的顺手工具。

**Why it matters**: An official tool that ports the classic network Swiss-army knife onto WireGuard/tailnet—port forwarding and piping across a private network with no public ports and no NAT hole-punching. An instantly useful daily driver for ops and devs who live in internal networks.

## 4. tashfeenahmed/freellmapi

⭐ 21.7k（今日 +433）· TypeScript · [链接](https://github.com/tashfeenahmed/freellmapi)

**是什么**：把 34 家 LLM 提供商的免费额度聚合成一个 OpenAI 兼容端点，带智能路由与自动故障转移；MIT 开源，核心免费。

**What it is**: Aggregates the free tiers of 34 LLM providers into one OpenAI-compatible endpoint with smart routing and automatic failover; MIT-licensed, core is free.

**为什么值得关注**：它把散落各家的免费模型额度收敛成一个 `/v1` 入口（号称每月约 74 亿 token、635 个免费端点），按速度/能力/可靠性打分自动选模并容错。对预算有限的独立开发者和做原型的人是极具吸引力的"零成本试验场"——但聚合免费额度类项目需留意各家条款与稳定性。

**Why it matters**: It funnels scattered free model quotas into a single `/v1` endpoint (claiming ~7.4B tokens/month across 635 free endpoints), auto-selecting models by speed/capability/reliability scores with failover. A compelling zero-cost sandbox for budget-constrained indie devs and prototypers—though free-tier aggregators come with provider-terms and stability caveats.
