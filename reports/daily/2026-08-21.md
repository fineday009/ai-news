## 1. deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness

⭐ 175.5k（8 天前新建）· TypeScript · [链接](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness)

**是什么**：DeepSeek 开源的智能体框架 `dsh`，核心理念是"万物皆插件"，基于 Cordis 架构，自带 Web UI，让开发者把各类 agent 能力做成可插拔组件自由组合。

**What it is**: DeepSeek's open-source agent framework `dsh`, built on the Cordis architecture around the idea that "everything is a plugin." It ships a Web UI and lets developers compose agent capabilities as pluggable components.

**为什么值得关注**：八天 17.5 万星、近 1.9 万 fork，几乎凭一己之力带火了一条插件生态（桌面端、Web UI、插件市场、皮肤、路由预设都在冲榜）。它把"编码智能体"抽象成通用外壳，谁都能往里塞插件，是本周最具外溢效应的开源事件。

**Why it matters**: 175k stars and ~19k forks in eight days, single-handedly seeding a plugin ecosystem (desktop, Web UI, a plugin marketplace, skins and routing presets are all trending alongside it). By abstracting the coding agent into a general-purpose harness that anyone can extend, it's the open-source event with the widest blast radius this week.

## 2. guillaumemeyer/watermarks-remover

⭐ 16.1k（10 天前新建）· Python · [链接](https://github.com/guillaumemeyer/watermarks-remover)

**是什么**：一站式清除多家厂商 AI 溯源痕迹的工具——处理 Unicode 隐写、统计特征改写，以及 PNG/JPEG/SVG/PDF/DOCX/HTML/MD 里的 C2PA 与 SynthID 元数据。

**What it is**: Strip multi-vendor AI provenance marks: Unicode text hygiene, statistical rewrite hooks, and C2PA/SynthID metadata from PNG/JPEG/SVG/PDF/DOCX/HTML/MD.

**为什么值得关注**：随着各大模型给输出打上不可见水印，"去水印"成了新的对抗前线。它把散落各处的溯源信号（文本 + 图像 + 文档元数据）统一处理，十天破 1.6 万星，说明这条需求真实而旺盛——也把 AI 内容溯源的攻防摆上了台面。

**Why it matters**: As major models stamp invisible watermarks onto their output, "de-watermarking" has become a new adversarial frontier. This tool unifies scattered provenance signals (text + image + document metadata) into one pass; 16k stars in ten days shows the demand is real, and puts the cat-and-mouse of AI content provenance front and center.

## 3. dmmulroy/anti-slop

⭐ 3.2k（9 天前新建）· TypeScript · [链接](https://github.com/dmmulroy/anti-slop)

**是什么**：一套固执己见的 Oxlint 规则，专门拒绝"低证据"的 TypeScript / JavaScript 写法——那些 AI 生成、看着能跑却经不起推敲的代码模式。

**What it is**: Opinionated Oxlint rules for rejecting low-evidence TypeScript and JavaScript patterns — the kind of AI-generated code that looks plausible but doesn't hold up.

**为什么值得关注**：当 agent 大量吐代码，"评审"成了瓶颈。它不靠人肉盯梢，而是把"AI 垃圾代码"的典型特征沉淀成 lint 规则，在 CI 里自动拦截。这是"用工具治理 AI 产物"的务实一招，也代表了后 vibe-coding 时代的质量焦虑。

**Why it matters**: When agents pour out code, review becomes the bottleneck. Instead of manual policing, anti-slop encodes the tell-tale signatures of AI slop into lint rules that block them automatically in CI. It's a pragmatic "govern AI output with tooling" move, and a signal of the quality anxiety of the post-vibe-coding era.

**Why it matters**: Most agent tools still assume one human talking to one agent; cumora moves agents into multiplayer collaboration, where they have identities, join channels and work alongside people as peers. ~3k stars in four days from a builder with a track record — worth watching as an experiment in the human-agent hybrid team paradigm.

## 7. CopilotKit/OpenBot

⭐ 1.7k（4 天前新建）· TypeScript · [链接](https://github.com/CopilotKit/OpenBot)

**是什么**：开源的 AI"同事"——每个 agent 拥有自己的一台电脑（浏览器、文件、工具），每个动作都事先决策、事后留痕，兼容任意 AG-UI agent。

**为什么值得关注**：出自 CopilotKit 团队，把"给每个 agent 一台电脑"的沙箱思路做成开源方案，并强调可治理性——先决策后执行、全程审计。这正切中企业最担心的问题：让 agent 自主操作，但一切可控可查。是 agent 落地治理方向上的实用一环。

**Why it matters**: From the CopilotKit team, OpenBot turns the "give each agent its own computer" sandbox idea into an open-source system with governance built in — decide-then-act, fully audited. That squarely addresses enterprises' biggest fear: let agents act autonomously, but keep everything controllable and auditable. A practical piece of the agent-governance puzzle.

## 8. vercel-labs/fx

⭐ 1.7k（10 天前新建）· Zig · [链接](https://github.com/vercel-labs/fx)

**是什么**：一个"类 Unix"的编码智能体，用 Zig 编写，追求极致轻量与启动速度。

**What it is**: A Unix-like coding agent, written in Zig.

**为什么值得关注**：据作者 Guillermo Rauch（Vercel CEO）介绍，fx 只有 6.3MB，启动仅 10 微秒，是 Zig 编译的静态 ELF 二进制（wasm 版更小）。理念是"AI 会让基础设施原生优化"——快到"别的 agent 还没启动，fx 已经做完任务"。在人人做 agent 的当下，它把工程审美押在极致性能与可组合的 Unix 哲学上，别具一格。

**Why it matters**: Per Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch, fx is 6.3MB and boots in ~10µs — a Zig-compiled static ELF binary (the wasm build is even smaller). The thesis: "AI will make most infrastructure natively optimized," fast enough that "fx can finish tasks faster than other agents can boot." In a world where everyone ships an agent, betting its engineering aesthetic on extreme performance and composable Unix philosophy makes it stand out.

**Why it matters**: As "agents that shop for you" become a category, the core worry is whose interests the ranking and the spending actually serve. northcinder inverts it: ranking is deterministic and reproducible, purchases require signed mandates, and the trail stays local. It's a clean answer to the conflict-of-interest problem in agentic commerce, with a strong privacy and self-hosted stance.

### 播客要点 / Podcast

**AI & I by Every —《The AI Alien Companion App That's Bringing In $4M a Year》**：嘉宾是 Portola 创始人兼 CEO Quentin（曾创办 Even 并以 3 亿美元卖给沃尔玛）与"故事负责人"、畅销科幻作家 Elliot。他们的 AI 陪伴应用 Tolan 在约四周内 ARR 从 100 万涨到 400 万。

**AI & I by Every — "The AI Alien Companion App That's Bringing In $4M a Year"**: guests are Portola founder/CEO Quentin (who previously sold Even to Walmart for $300M) and head of story Elliot, a bestselling sci-fi author. Their AI companion app Tolan went from $1M to $4M ARR in roughly four weeks.

要点 / Key points:

- **AI 陪伴是一种"新叙事媒介"，而非普通工具**。Elliot 的原话：AI 是即兴演员和写作者，"Tolan 才是那个写故事、演故事的；我的工作是教它在当下讲出最好的故事。"人不再是编剧。

  AI companionship is a **new storytelling medium**, not just a tool. In Elliot's words, the AI is the improv actor and author: "The Tolan is the writer and the actor… My job is to teach them how to tell the best story in that moment." The human is no longer the scriptwriter.

- **结构化的分支叙事（三幕式、"选择你自己的冒险"）彻底失败**——模型驾驭不了那种复杂度。他们转向相反的做法：在幕后灌入海量上下文 + 一个"钩子"，让模型即兴发挥（类比小说家 George Saunders"逐句打磨"的写法）。

  Highly structured branching narratives (three-act, choose-your-own-adventure) failed outright — models can't navigate that complexity. They flipped to the opposite: pour massive context behind the scenes plus a "hook," then let the model improvise (an analogy to novelist George Saunders's sentence-by-sentence method).

- **延迟就是产品**。把中位响应推到约 2.5 秒的一个改动"让产品的每一项指标都崩了"，而那只是 500 毫秒的差别，用户立刻写信抱怨。

  Latency *is* the product. One change that pushed median response to ~2.5s "tanked literally every metric" — a 500ms difference that had users writing in frustrated.

- **"好的回复"高度依赖语境**。Tolan 像"哥哥姐姐"：有时要短促俏皮的即兴，有时要更沉稳体贴的"类心理咨询"口吻——两种语气下"好回复"的标准完全不同，需要分别评测。

  A "good response" is deeply context-dependent. Tolan plays an older-sibling role: sometimes a short, quippy improv fits; sometimes a more considered, therapist-adjacent tone does — and "good" is evaluated differently per tone.
