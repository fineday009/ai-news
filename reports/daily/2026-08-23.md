**是什么**：全栈式 AI 红队（Red Teaming）平台，通过扫描与评测来保护 AI 生态的安全。

**What it is**: A full-stack AI red-teaming platform that secures AI ecosystems through scanning and evaluation.

**为什么值得关注**：当 Agent、MCP、模型服务大规模上线，攻击面也随之扩大，"给 AI 系统本身做安全扫描"正成为刚需。它把提示注入、越权、供应链等风险的探测与评估打包成可复用的平台，正好接住企业把 AI 投产后对安全评估的需求缺口。

**Why it matters**: As agents, MCP, and model services ship at scale, the attack surface grows with them, and "security-scanning the AI system itself" is becoming a hard requirement. It packages the probing and evaluation of risks—prompt injection, privilege escalation, supply-chain—into a reusable platform, filling exactly the security-assessment gap enterprises hit after putting AI into production.

## 3. Wei-Shaw/sub2api

⭐ 约 38.8k（今日 +278）· Go · [链接](https://github.com/Wei-Shaw/sub2api)

**是什么**：统一的 API 中转服务，把 Claude、OpenAI、Gemini、Grok 等订阅聚合成一个接口对外提供。

**What it is**: A unified API relay service that pools Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, and Grok subscriptions behind a single endpoint.

**为什么值得关注**：很多人手里握着多家模型的月度订阅，却被各自的 SDK、鉴权和限流割裂。它用一层中转把"订阅额度"转成 OpenAI 兼容 API，方便在自建工具里自由切换后端，是当下"订阅入口聚合"这波热潮里工程完成度较高的一个。

**Why it matters**: Many people hold monthly subscriptions to several model vendors but are fragmented across each one's SDK, auth, and rate limits. It relays those subscriptions into an OpenAI-compatible API so you can freely swap backends inside your own tooling—one of the more finished entries in the current wave of "subscription-gateway aggregation."

### 播客要点 / Podcast

**No Priors —《What Chess.com Teaches Us About Superhuman Capabilities》，嘉宾 Erik Allebest（chess.com 联合创始人兼 CEO）**

**No Priors — "What Chess.com Teaches Us About Superhuman Capabilities," with Erik Allebest (co-founder & CEO of Chess.com)**

- 反直觉的创业样本：2005 年他从破产拍卖里花 5.6 万美元买下 chess.com 域名，被几乎所有投资人劝"去找份正经工作"；此后几乎全程 bootstrap，做到 2.5 亿+注册用户、约 2 亿美元年收入、650 人全远程团队，"基本不需要融资来增长"。

- A counterintuitive founder story: in 2005 he bought the chess.com domain for $56K out of a bankruptcy auction while nearly every investor told him to "go get a real job"; he then bootstrapped almost the whole way to 250M+ registered members, ~$200M annual revenue, and a 650-person fully-remote team—"no capital needed to grow this business."

- 对 AI 从业者的启示一：机器在国际象棋上碾压人类已三十年，这项游戏却比以往任何时候都火——超人类能力的存在，并不消灭人类参与该活动的意义。作弊（用引擎冒充人类高手）是象棋几十年的老问题，靠统计与机器学习模型来检测，正是如今各行各业都要面对的课题的预演。

- Lesson one for AI builders: machines have outclassed humans at chess for thirty years, yet the game is more popular than ever—the existence of superhuman capability doesn't erase the meaning of humans doing the activity. Cheating (engines passing as human masters) is a decades-old chess problem fought with statistical and ML detection—a preview of what every field now faces with AI.

- 对 AI 从业者的启示二：AI 是"口袋里的教授"，能陪你深挖任何主题、快速接触与打磨想法；但要警惕它的偏见、迎合与吹捧你自我的倾向。而且无论工具多强，学习本身没有捷径——"你没法真正给练习抄近道，必须投入时间做重复。"

- Lesson two: AI is a "professor in your pocket" that lets you deep-dive any subject and refine ideas fast—but beware its biases and its tendency to tell you what you want to hear and inflate your ego. And however strong the tools, learning has no shortcut: "you can't really shortcut practice—you have to put in the time."

- 金句："你可以给 AI 一个提示，明天就有一万种新棋盘游戏……但当音乐无限时，人们有时反而回到 Beatles 和 Led Zeppelin。"——在万物爆炸式增殖的世界里，规则简单、无运气成分、可共享的"经典"反而成了人们愿意抓住的锚。

- Quote: "You could give an AI a prompt and you could have 10,000 new board games tomorrow… but when there's infinite music, sometimes people go back to the Beatles and Led Zeppelin." In a world of exploding proliferation, the simple-ruled, luck-free, shareable "classics" become the anchor people want to hold onto.
