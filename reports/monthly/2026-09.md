**Why it matters**: Vendor peripheral software is notoriously bloated, login-gated, and always-on; OpenLogi replaces it with a lightweight native tool that respects privacy. It's a textbook case of local-first thinking reaching hardware companion software, and it's a hit with hardware enthusiasts.

## 9. google-research/timesfm

⭐ 32.9k（本月 +5,249）· Python · [链接](https://github.com/google-research/timesfm)

**是什么**：由 Google Research 开发的时间序列基础模型（TimesFM），面向时间序列预测的预训练模型。

**What it is**: TimesFM (Time Series Foundation Model) is a pretrained time-series foundation model developed by Google Research for time-series forecasting.

**为什么值得关注**：大模型多聚焦文本与图像，而时间序列（销量、负载、金融等）长期缺乏「基础模型」。TimesFM 提供开箱即用的零样本预测能力，把基础模型范式带到预测领域，对做运维、供应链、量化的团队价值极高。

**Why it matters**: Foundation models have focused on text and images, while time series — sales, load, finance — long lacked one. TimesFM offers out-of-the-box zero-shot forecasting, bringing the foundation-model paradigm to prediction, with high value for ops, supply-chain, and quant teams.
