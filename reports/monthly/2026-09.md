## 10. baidu/Unlimited-OCR

⭐ 25.3k · 创建于 06-18 · Python · [链接](https://github.com/baidu/Unlimited-OCR)

**是什么**：百度放出的 OCR/文档解析项目，主打"一次性长文档解析（One-shot Long-horizon Parsing）的时代"。

**What it is**: "Welcome the Era of One-shot Long-horizon Parsing."

**为什么值得关注**：传统 OCR 擅长逐页识字，却在"整份长文档的版面、结构与跨页关系"上力不从心。Unlimited-OCR 把长文档解析当成一等目标，试图一次性吃下版面复杂的完整文档。对要把海量 PDF、扫描件转成结构化数据喂给大模型的团队，这是一块关键拼图。

**Why it matters**: Traditional OCR is good at reading characters page by page but weak on the layout, structure, and cross-page relationships of a whole long document. Unlimited-OCR treats long-document parsing as a first-class goal, aiming to ingest layout-complex full documents in one shot. For teams turning mountains of PDFs and scans into structured data for LLMs, it's a key piece.
