## 4. JustVugg/colibri

⭐ 27k · C · 7/01 创建 · [链接](https://github.com/JustVugg/colibri)

**是什么**：用纯 C、零依赖，在你已有的硬件上跑前沿 MoE 模型——把专家（experts）从磁盘按需流式加载，引擎极小、能力却很大。

**What it is**: Run frontier MoE models on hardware you already own—pure C, zero dependencies, with experts streamed from disk. A tiny engine with outsized capability.

**为什么值得关注**：MoE 模型显存需求高，是本地部署的最大拦路虎。它用「专家磁盘流式」绕开显存墙，思路和 llama.cpp 一脉相承却更进一步，让普通硬件也能跑大 MoE，27k star 说明社区反响热烈。

**Why it matters**: MoE models' memory appetite is the biggest blocker to local deployment. Streaming experts from disk sidesteps the VRAM wall—in the lineage of llama.cpp but a step further—letting ordinary hardware run large MoE. 27k stars shows strong community resonance.
