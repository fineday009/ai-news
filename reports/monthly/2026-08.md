**Why it matters**: When running one agent at a time isn't enough, orchestrating, observing, and reusing many parallel agents becomes the new problem. orca offers a unified workbench and lets you bring your own subscription (no vendor lock-in). Gaining 25k stars this month, it shows multi-agent orchestration moving from concept to everyday tool.

## 8. antirez/ds4

**是什么**：DeepSeek 4（Flash 与 PRO）的本地推理引擎，支持 Metal、CUDA 和 ROCm。

**What it is**: A local inference engine for DeepSeek 4 (Flash and PRO), supporting Metal, CUDA, and ROCm.

**为什么值得关注**：Redis 之父 antirez 亲手用 C 写的推理引擎，跨三大 GPU 后端，主打精炼、可读、可移植。相比庞大的推理框架，ds4 以「小而精、单人可维护」的工程美学切入 DeepSeek 4 本地部署，对想读懂推理内核、或在多平台上轻量跑 DeepSeek 的人极具价值。

**Why it matters**: A C inference engine hand-written by antirez (creator of Redis), spanning all three major GPU backends, prizing concision, readability, and portability. Against sprawling inference frameworks, ds4 brings a small-but-sharp, one-person-maintainable engineering aesthetic to local DeepSeek 4 deployment — valuable for anyone wanting to understand an inference kernel or run DeepSeek lightly across platforms.
