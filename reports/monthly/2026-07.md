**Why it matters**: The season's undisputed phenomenon at 81k stars. It takes "AI design" — territory otherwise held by closed products — and rebuilds it as a local-first, BYOK, all-major-agents open tool. Almost a culmination of the "vibe coding + vibe design" wave.

## 4. JustVugg/colibri

⭐ 19.1k · 2026-07-01 创建 · C · [链接](https://github.com/JustVugg/colibri)

**是什么**：用纯 C、零依赖，在 25GB 内存的消费级机器上跑 GLM-5.2（744B MoE），专家权重从磁盘按需流式加载。

**What it is**: Runs GLM-5.2 (744B MoE) on a 25GB-RAM consumer machine — pure C, zero dependencies, experts streamed from disk on demand.

**为什么值得关注**：本月"本地推理"方向最硬核的项目。用磁盘流式加载让普通机器跑起 744B 级 MoE，直接挑战"大模型必须上数据中心"的定论，也和 antirez 的 ds4 一起把民间高性能推理引擎推向新高度。

**Why it matters**: The month's most hardcore local-inference project. Streaming experts from disk lets a normal machine run a 744B-class MoE, directly challenging the "big models require a data center" orthodoxy — and together with antirez's ds4, pushing community inference engines to a new level.
