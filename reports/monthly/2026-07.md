**Why it matters**: Peer-to-peer connectivity has long been plagued by NAT traversal and IP drift; iroh's "dial keys, not IPs" approach wraps QUIC and NAT traversal into a ready-to-use library so apps can hold stable direct connections across messy networks. As low-level networking infrastructure it chases no hype but solves a real problem; +2,981 stars this month and a Rust implementation make it a practical library worth bookmarking in this period's non-AI camp.
