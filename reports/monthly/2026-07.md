## 9. n0-computer/iroh

**是什么**：一个 Rust 网络库——「IP 会变，改拨密钥吧」，为应用加上 QUIC + NAT 穿透，让服务用公钥而非 IP 相互直连。

**为什么值得关注**：P2P/直连一直难在 NAT 穿透与连接稳定性。iroh 把这套底层能力封装成开箱即用的库，用密钥寻址替代脆弱的 IP，是构建去中心化、本地优先应用时少数真正好用的网络基座之一。

**Why it matters**: P2P and direct connectivity have always struggled with NAT traversal and connection stability. Iroh packages this plumbing into an out-of-the-box library, replacing brittle IP addressing with key-based dialing — one of the few genuinely usable networking foundations for decentralized, local-first apps.
