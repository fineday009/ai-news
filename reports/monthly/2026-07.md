**Why it matters**: Unlike Signal or Telegram, which still rely on phone numbers or user IDs, SimpleX needs no identifier at all, eliminating metadata linkage at the protocol level. In an era of scarce privacy, this radical "doesn't even know who you are" design is one of messaging's most hardcore privacy efforts — and the month's most solid non-AI project.

## 9. n0-computer/iroh

⭐ 11.3k（本月 +2,617）· Rust · [链接](https://github.com/n0-computer/iroh)

**是什么**：IP 地址会变，改用「拨号密钥」——一个为你的应用加上 QUIC + NAT 穿透的库。

**What it is**: IP addresses break, dial keys instead — a library that adds QUIC + NAT traversal to your apps.

**为什么值得关注**：点对点连接的老大难是 NAT 穿透与地址漂移。iroh 用 Rust 把「基于公钥拨号、自动打洞、走 QUIC」这套能力封装成可直接嵌入的库，让开发者无需自建复杂网络层就能做出真正的 P2P 应用。它是本地优先、去中心化软件浪潮下最实用的底层基建之一。

**Why it matters**: The perennial pain of peer-to-peer is NAT traversal and shifting addresses. In Rust, iroh packages "dial by public key, auto hole-punch, ride on QUIC" into an embeddable library, letting developers build true P2P apps without hand-rolling a complex network layer. It's one of the most practical foundations under the local-first, decentralized-software wave.

## 10. alibaba/zvec

⭐ 14.7k（本月 +4,912）· C++ · [链接](https://github.com/alibaba/zvec)

**是什么**：一个轻量、极快的进程内向量数据库。

**What it is**: A lightweight, lightning-fast, in-process vector database.

**为什么值得关注**：RAG 与语义检索离不开向量库，但许多方案要么重、要么得单独部署服务。zvec 主打「进程内、轻量、极快」，可以像 SQLite 一样嵌进应用，省去运维一整套向量服务的成本。阿里出品、用 C++ 写就，为「把语义检索直接嵌入本地应用」提供了一个务实高效的选择。

**Why it matters**: RAG and semantic search depend on vector stores, but many options are heavy or require running a separate service. zvec is "in-process, lightweight, and blazing fast" — embeddable like SQLite, sparing you the cost of operating a whole vector service. From Alibaba and written in C++, it's a pragmatic, efficient choice for baking semantic search straight into local apps.
