**Why it matters**: Pure-LLM review is noisy and pure-rules is rigid; the "deterministic pipelines + LLM agent" hybrid is the most pragmatic route right now. ## 7. microsoft/SkillOpt

⭐ 13.0k（自创建约 2.5 个月 +13.0k）· Python · [链接](https://github.com/microsoft/SkillOpt)

**是什么**：SkillOpt 是一个「文本空间优化器」，通过轨迹驱动的编辑、验证门控的更新，为冻结参数的 LLM Agent 训练出可复用的自然语言「技能」，并产出可部署的 best_skill.md。

**What it is**: SkillOpt is a text-space optimizer that trains reusable natural-language skills for frozen LLM agents through trajectory-driven edits, validation-gated updates, and deployable best_skill.md artifacts.

**为什么值得关注**：不动模型权重、只在「文本空间」优化提示/技能，是让 Agent 自我进化的低成本路线。微软把这套「自进化技能」方法开源，兼具研究深度与工程可部署性，比市面上大量手写 skill 合集更有方法论价值。

**Why it matters**: Optimizing prompts/skills in "text space" without touching model weights is a low-cost path to self-evolving agents. Microsoft open-sourcing this "self-evolving skill" method combines research depth with deployable engineering — more methodologically valuable than the many hand-written skill collections out there.
