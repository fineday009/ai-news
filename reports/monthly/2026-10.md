## 2. NandhaKishorM/laya

⭐ 33.3k（本月 +巨量）· Python · [链接](https://github.com/NandhaKishorM/laya)

**是什么**：非自回归的"System 1"决策引擎，用单次前向推理对任意文本做分类、打分、是/否判断，支持 100+ 语言，并带一个按请求挑选 checkpoint 的路由器。

**What it is**: A non-autoregressive "System 1" decision engine—typed choice, score and yes/no decisions over any text in a single forward pass, across 100+ languages, with a router that picks the right checkpoint per request.

**为什么值得关注**：本月最有新意的研究型项目之一。它不走"什么都让大模型生成"的老路，而是把高频的轻量决策任务从昂贵的自回归生成里剥离出来，用一次前向搞定——对延迟和成本敏感的生产系统意义重大，并已衍生出 laya-mlx 等本地运行时。

**Why it matters**: One of the month's most original research projects. Instead of funneling everything through generative LLMs, it peels high-frequency lightweight decisions off expensive autoregressive generation and resolves them in a single forward pass—big for latency- and cost-sensitive production systems, and already spawning runtimes like laya-mlx.
