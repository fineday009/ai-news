## 5. NandhaKishorM/laya

⭐ 33.4k · Python · [链接](https://github.com/NandhaKishorM/laya)

**是什么**：非自回归的"系统 1"决策引擎——在一次前向传播里对任意文本做分类、打分、是非判断，支持 100+ 语言，并有路由器按请求挑选合适的 checkpoint。

**What it is**: A non-autoregressive "System 1" decision engine—typed choice, scoring and yes/no decisions over any text in a single forward pass, across 100+ languages, with a router that picks the right checkpoint per request.

**为什么值得关注**：当所有人都在堆自回归大模型时，laya 走了另一条路：对"分类/判断"这类任务，用一次前向传播给出结果，速度和成本远优于让 LLM 逐 token 生成。基于 ModernBERT，这是对"不是所有任务都需要生成式大模型"的有力提醒，方向新颖。

**Why it matters**: While everyone stacks autoregressive LLMs, laya takes a different route: for classification/decision tasks it returns an answer in one forward pass, far faster and cheaper than token-by-token generation. Built on ModernBERT, it's a pointed reminder that not every task needs a generative LLM—a genuinely novel direction.
