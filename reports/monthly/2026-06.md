## 3. ruvnet/RuView

**为什么值得关注**：用普通 WiFi 信号「看见」房间里的人，是传感领域极具想象力的方向。它不是 PPT 概念——有固件、边缘计算、发布在 Hugging Face 的预训练模型、可复现的基准数据集（MM-Fi），还能接入 Home Assistant/Matter。对智能家居、健康监护、安防都意义重大，是本月最具新意的硬核项目。

**Why it matters**: "Seeing" people in a room through ordinary WiFi signals is one of the most imaginative directions in sensing. And it's not vaporware — there's firmware, edge computing, pretrained models published on Hugging Face, reproducible benchmark datasets (MM-Fi), and Home Assistant/Matter integration. Meaningful for smart homes, health monitoring, and security — the month's most inventive hardcore project.
