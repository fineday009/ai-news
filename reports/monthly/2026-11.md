As more code is written by agents, having agents audit it is becoming standard—on substance, it's the month's most solid pick.

## 5. pingdotgg/t3code

**是什么**：T3 Code——一个"Agent harness 控制面"：通过手机 App（iOS/Android）、网页端和 Electron 桌面端，控制你电脑上的各个编码 Agent。

**What it is**: T3 Code is an "agent harness control surface": control the agents on your machine from a mobile app (iOS, Android), a web app and an Electron desktop app.

**为什么值得关注**：近 30 天 +2.9 万星，其中约 1.20 万来自最近一周——加速明显。它不卖模型也不卖订阅，直接复用你电脑上已登录的 Claude Code、Codex、Cursor、OpenCode 等，统一在一个界面里调度，并可从手机远程接管，且完全开源。当一个人同时用好几个 harness 成为常态，多 Agent 控制台正在成为一层独立的工具，t3code 是其中增长最快的一个。

**Why it matters**: +28.6k stars in 30 days, about 12.0k of them in the last week alone—clearly accelerating. It sells neither models nor subscriptions: it reuses the Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode and other installs already signed in on your machine, drives them from one interface, lets you take over from your phone, and is fully open source. With one person running several harnesses now the norm, multi-agent consoles are becoming a tool layer of their own, and t3code is its fastest grower.

## 6. heygen-com/hyperframes

⭐ 82.4k（本月 +22,380）· TypeScript · [链接](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes)

**是什么**：写 HTML，然后渲染成视频——专为 Agent 打造。

**What it is**: Write HTML. Render video. Built for agents.

**为什么值得关注**：让 Agent 直接驱动像素或视频模型来做视频，既不稳定也难控制。hyperframes 换了个聪明的抽象：用 Agent 最擅长的 HTML/CSS 描述画面，再渲染成视频，于是视频也变得可编程、可审查、可版本管理。近 30 天 +2.2 万星、总星数突破 8 万，对自动化内容、讲解动画和营销素材都很实用。

**Why it matters**: Having an agent drive pixels or a video model directly is unstable and hard to control. hyperframes picks a smarter abstraction: describe each frame in the HTML/CSS agents are best at, then render it to video, which makes video programmable, reviewable and version-controlled. +22.4k stars in 30 days, now past 80k; handy for automated content, explainer animations and marketing assets.
