**为什么值得关注**：它给出了「让 agent 真正理解代码库」的另一条路——不靠向量检索的模糊匹配，而是本地确定性 AST 解析出可解释的知识图谱。「每条边都有解释、无需向量库」这一取向，直击 RAG 幻觉与不可解释的痛点。

**Why it matters**: It offers another path to "let agents truly understand a codebase"—not fuzzy vector retrieval, but local deterministic AST parsing into an explainable knowledge graph. Its "every edge explained, no vector store" stance targets RAG's hallucination and opacity problems head-on.
