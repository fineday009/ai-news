## 1. openclaw/openclaw

⭐ 379,537 · TypeScript · 创建于 2025-11-24 · [链接](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw)

**是什么**：属于你自己的个人 AI 助手，支持任意操作系统、任意平台——「龙虾之道」🦞。

**What it is**: Your own personal AI assistant. Any OS. Any platform. The lobster way. 🦞

**为什么值得关注**：以近 38 万星稳居年度第一，openclaw 把「人人都该有一个自己掌控的 AI 助手」从口号变成可落地的开源项目。它强调自有数据、跨平台、不绑定单一厂商，是 2026 年开放 AI 助手运动的精神图腾。

**Why it matters**: At nearly 380k stars it sits atop the year, turning "everyone should own a self-controlled AI assistant" from a slogan into a deployable open-source project. Its emphasis on own-your-data, cross-platform, and vendor independence makes it the spiritual totem of 2026's open AI-assistant movement.

⭐ 197,700 · Python · 创建于 2025-07-22 · [链接](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent)

**是什么**：「会与你一同成长」的 AI Agent。

**Why it matters**: From Nous Research, Hermes Agent centers on "evolving alongside the user," making memory, personalization, and continual learning core rather than a one-off tool. Nearly 200k stars reflect huge community appetite for agents with continuity that "get to know you the more you use them" — one of the most magnetic projects in the open-source agent camp.

**Why it matters**: As coding agents proliferate, juggling multiple agents, configs, and models became a new pain point. cc-switch offers a lightweight, unified control panel in Rust that nails power users' daily needs — and its 100k+ stars show that "too many agents, need one switch" is a real and widespread frustration.

## 5. github/spec-kit

⭐ 114,178 · Python · 创建于 2025-08-21 · [链接](https://github.com/github/spec-kit)

**是什么**：帮助你上手「规格驱动开发」（Spec-Driven Development）的工具包。

**为什么值得关注**：GitHub 官方出品，回答了一个关键问题：在 Agent 写代码的时代，人该怎么把意图讲清楚？spec-kit 把「先写规格、再让 Agent 实现」固化成可操作的流程，代表了「规格即新源码」的工程思潮，是大厂对 AI 协作开发方法论的正式背书。

**Why it matters**: An official GitHub project answering a key question: in an age where agents write the code, how do humans express intent clearly? spec-kit codifies "spec first, then let the agent implement" into an actionable workflow, embodying the "spec as the new source code" school — a big-tech endorsement of AI-collaborative development methodology.

## 7. thedotmack/claude-mem

⭐ 83,272 · JavaScript · 创建于 2025-08-31 · [链接](https://github.com/thedotmack/claude-mem)

**是什么**：为每一个 Agent 提供跨会话的持久上下文——捕捉会话中的一切、用 AI 压缩，再把相关上下文注入未来的会话。兼容 Claude Code、OpenClaw、Codex、Gemini、Hermes、Copilot、OpenCode 等。

**What it is**: Persistent context across sessions for every agent — captures everything during a session, compresses it with AI, and injects relevant context back into future sessions. Works with Claude Code, OpenClaw, Codex, Gemini, Hermes, Copilot, OpenCode, and more.

**为什么值得关注**：「Agent 没有记忆、每次从零开始」是过去一年最被诟病的体验缺陷。claude-mem 用「捕捉—压缩—注入」的闭环跨工具解决这个问题，且兼容几乎所有主流 Agent，是 2026 年「给 Agent 装记忆」运动中落地最广的方案之一。

**Why it matters**: "Agents have no memory and start from scratch every time" was the most-criticized experience gap of the past year. claude-mem solves it across tools with a capture–compress–inject loop, compatible with nearly every major agent — one of the most widely adopted solutions in 2026's "give agents memory" movement.

**是什么**：一个 CLI 代理，在常见开发命令上把 LLM 的 token 消耗降低 60–90%；单一 Rust 二进制、零依赖。

**Why it matters**: It shares the token-thrift lane with the later-breakout headroom, but moved earlier and sits at the CLI-command layer. A single zero-dependency binary means instant adoption, and its 64k stars prove that "cut agent invocation costs" was a year-long, unflagging throughline.
