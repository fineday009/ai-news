## 1. TauricResearch/TradingAgents

**为什么值得关注**：它把交易决策拆成分析师、研究员、交易员、风控等协作 Agent，用「团队辩论」逼近专业投研流程，是本年度多智能体在高价值垂直领域落地最具代表性的项目，深刻影响了大家对 Agent 协作范式的认知。

It's the year's flagship example of multi-agent systems landing in a high-value vertical, shaping how the field thinks about agent-collaboration patterns.

## 2. github/spec-kit

⭐ 累计约 137.4k · Python · 2026 年度爆发 · [链接](https://github.com/github/spec-kit)

**是什么**：一个帮助你上手「规范驱动开发」（Spec-Driven Development）的工具包。

**What it is**: 💫 Toolkit to help you get started with Spec-Driven Development.

**为什么值得关注**：当 AI 能大批量写代码，瓶颈从「写」转移到「把需求讲清楚」。GitHub 官方的 spec-kit 把规范提升为一等公民，让 Agent 依据明确规范生成代码，是本年度对「Vibe Coding 失控」最重要的方法论纠偏。

**Why it matters**: When AI writes code at scale, the bottleneck shifts from writing to specifying intent. GitHub's spec-kit elevates the spec to a first-class citizen so agents build against explicit requirements — the year's most important methodological corrective to runaway "vibe coding."

**是什么**：🧠 仅需 2 小时，从零训练一个 6400 万参数的大语言模型。

## 8. google-research/timesfm

⭐ 累计约 32.9k · Python · 2026 年度爆发 · [链接](https://github.com/google-research/timesfm)

**是什么**：由 Google Research 开发的时间序列基础模型（TimesFM），面向时间序列预测的预训练模型。

**What it is**: TimesFM (Time Series Foundation Model) is a pretrained time-series foundation model developed by Google Research for time-series forecasting.

**为什么值得关注**：这一年里，基础模型范式从文本、图像扩展到时间序列，TimesFM 提供开箱即用的零样本预测能力，填补了运维、供应链、金融等领域长期缺乏「预测基础模型」的空白，是本年度基础模型「破圈」到新模态的标志之一。

**Why it matters**: This year the foundation-model paradigm extended from text and images to time series; TimesFM offers out-of-the-box zero-shot forecasting, filling a long-standing gap in ops, supply-chain, and finance. It's one of the year's markers of foundation models breaking into new modalities.
