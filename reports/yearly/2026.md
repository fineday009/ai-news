**Why it matters**: In a 2026 where nearly everyone feeds agents via vector retrieval, graphify insists on a deterministic, explainable, vector-store-free path that attacks RAG's hallucination and traceability pains. 125k+ stars signal strong demand for *precise* code understanding — one of the year's most technically opinionated tools.
