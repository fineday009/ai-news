## 1. openclaw/openclaw

⭐ 392.0k · 2025-11-24 新建 · TypeScript · [链接](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw)

**是什么**：一个开源（MIT）、运行在你自己设备上的 AI 助理——通过 WhatsApp、Telegram、Slack、Discord、iMessage 等聊天应用工作，记忆、状态与凭据都留在本地硬件，模型可在 Claude、Codex、本地模型之间自由切换；既能当个人助理，也能通过中心 Gateway 做团队共享部署。

**What it is**: An open-source (MIT) AI assistant that runs on your own devices — working through chat apps like WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Discord and iMessage, keeping memory, state and credentials on your hardware, with model providers swappable between Claude, Codex and local models; it runs as a personal assistant or, via a central Gateway, as a shared team deployment.

**为什么值得关注**：接近 39 万星、全站居首，openclaw 几乎定义了 2026 年的关键词——"真正会做事、你自己掌控、无托管/无付费层"的 AI 助理。它把"助理"从云端黑箱拉回用户设备，并催生了 Paperclip 等一整套"管理 Agent"的上层生态，是本年度最具象征意义的项目。

**Why it matters**: At ~392k stars and the top of the entire board, openclaw practically defines 2026's keyword — an AI assistant that "really does things," fully under your control, with no hosted or paid tier. It pulls the "assistant" out of the cloud black box and back onto user devices, spawning a whole upper layer of "manage your agents" ecosystem (Paperclip among them); the year's most emblematic project.

## 2. deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness

⭐ 249.8k · 2026-08-13 新建 · TypeScript · [链接](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness)

**是什么**：DeepSeek 官方开源的 Agent harness（`dsh`），核心理念"万物皆插件"，基于 Cordis 框架，`npx @deepseek-ai/dsh web` 即可启动，带 `dsh-plugin` 标签的仓库可被自动发现。

**What it is**: DeepSeek's official open-source agent harness (`dsh`), built on an "Everything is a Plugin" philosophy and the Cordis framework; it launches with `npx @deepseek-ai/dsh web`, and repos tagged `dsh-plugin` are auto-discoverable.

**为什么值得关注**：短短两个月近 25 万星，是下半年最具统治力的开源项目，围绕它迅速长出 dsh-desktop、awesome-dsh-plugin、免费模型插件等庞大卫星生态。它标志着头部模型厂商亲自下场做"开放 Agent 运行时平台"，是年度"Agent 平台之战"的分水岭事件。

**Why it matters**: ~250k stars in just two months make it the second half's most dominant open-source project, rapidly growing a vast satellite ecosystem — dsh-desktop, awesome-dsh-plugin, free-model plugins and more. It marks a top model vendor directly entering the "open agent-runtime platform" arena, a watershed in the year's agent-platform war.

## 3. morluto/rea

⭐ 246.0k · 2026-04-14 新建 · TypeScript · [链接](https://github.com/morluto/rea)

**是什么**：用 Agent"逆向工程一切"的工具——从应用的外部行为一路扒到原生二进制，接入 Ghidra、Hopper 等工具链做静态分析与反编译。

**What it is**: A tool that reverse-engineers anything with agents — from an app's observable behavior down to native binaries, wiring in toolchains like Ghidra and Hopper for static analysis and decompilation.

**为什么值得关注**：全年累计 24.6 万星，且在 10 月单周狂揽 21 万星爆红。它把只有少数安全专家才掌握的逆向手艺"Agent 化"，让任何人都能让 Agent 帮忙看懂、拆解任意程序，是"Agent + 专业领域"解锁新场景的年度代表作，对安全研究与互操作意义深远。

**Why it matters**: 246k stars for the year, with a stunning +215k in a single week in October. It "agent-ifies" a reverse-engineering craft once held by a few security experts, letting anyone have an agent make sense of and take apart arbitrary programs — the year's banner example of "agents + a specialist domain" unlocking new use-cases, with deep implications for security research and interoperability.

**Why it matters**: From a top-tier researcher, at nearly 100k stars. It grounds the frontier question of "agents doing ML research automatically" in something reproducible and low-cost (single GPU) — both a highly instructive open-source template and an echo of a deeper theme this year: AI starting to accelerate AI research itself.

## 5. paperclipai/paperclip

⭐ 102.2k · 2026-03-02 新建 · TypeScript · [链接](https://github.com/paperclipai/paperclip)

**是什么**：一个自托管的"Agent 团队控制台"——给 Agent 派目标、从任意供应商招聘 Agent 放进组织架构图，带任务线程、持久会话、预算告警与审批关卡。"如果 OpenClaw 是员工，Paperclip 就是公司。"

**What it is**: A self-hosted control plane for teams of AI agents — assign goals, hire agents from any provider onto an org chart, with task threads, persistent sessions, budget alerts and approval gates. "If OpenClaw is an employee, Paperclip is the company."

**为什么值得关注**：10 万+ 星、合并逾 2,700 个 PR。它代表了 2026 下半年最清晰的演进：当人人都养着一群 Agent，真正稀缺的是"编排、治理、成本控制"的上层平台。Paperclip 把自主 Agent 当员工来运营的理念做成了可用产品，是年度"Agent 组织化"趋势的标杆。

**Why it matters**: 100k+ stars and 2,700+ merged PRs. It represents the clearest evolution of late 2026: once everyone runs a swarm of agents, what's truly scarce is the upper-layer platform for orchestration, governance and cost control. Paperclip turns "run autonomous agents like employees" into a usable product — the benchmark for the year's "agent organization" trend.

**Why it matters**: 100k+ stars, the year's standout for human-AI collaboration in design and creative work. It extends agents from "help you write code" to "co-produce design work with a whole team," while staying local-first and open source, squaring off against Figma-style collaboration tools and pointing at how creative workflows get reshaped by agents.

## 8. rtk-ai/rtk

⭐ 83.4k · 2026-01-22 新建 · Rust · [链接](https://github.com/rtk-ai/rtk)

**是什么**：一个 CLI 代理（proxy），在常见开发命令上把 LLM 的 token 消耗削减 60–90%，单一 Rust 二进制、零依赖。

**What it is**: A CLI proxy that cuts LLM token consumption by 60–90% on common dev commands — a single Rust binary with zero dependencies.

**为什么值得关注**：8 万+ 星。随着编码 Agent 普及，"token 成本"成了真金白银的痛点，由此催生出 rtk、caveman、headroom、OmniRoute 等一整条"省 token/降成本"赛道。rtk 以极简的单二进制形态成为其中的代表，是本年度 AI 基础设施层最务实的方向之一。

**Why it matters**: 80k+ stars. As coding agents went mainstream, token cost became a hard-dollar pain, spawning a whole "save tokens / cut cost" lane — rtk, caveman, headroom, OmniRoute and more. rtk became the lane's poster child with its minimalist single-binary form, one of the most pragmatic directions in the year's AI infrastructure layer.

**Why it matters**: 60k+ stars within two weeks, igniting the year-end wave of "rewrite classic desktop software in Rust." It represents 2026's other clear thread: beyond AI, strong user hunger for local, high-performance, auditable, cloud-free open-source creative and office software — a force that shaped the year's open-source landscape just as much.
