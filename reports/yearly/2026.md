**Why it matters**: Statelessness — forgetting everything once a session closes — is a core weakness of today's agents, making memory one of the year's most essential directions. At nearly 87k stars, claude-mem proves the broad demand for cross-session memory, turning "agents remembering the past" into a reusable component — a key piece for anyone wanting agents that accompany and accumulate over time.
