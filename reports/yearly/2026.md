## 2. github/spec-kit

⭐ 121.3k · Python · 创建于 2025-08 · [链接](https://github.com/github/spec-kit)

**是什么**：GitHub 官方推出的「规格驱动开发」（Spec-Driven Development）工具包。

**What it is**: GitHub's official toolkit to help you get started with Spec-Driven Development.

**为什么值得关注**：过去一年最具影响力的方法论类项目之一。当 AI 会写代码后，"先把规格写清楚"成为控制质量的关键，spec-kit 把这套流程标准化并官方背书，重塑了大量团队的协作方式。

**Why it matters**: One of the most influential methodology projects of the year. Once AI can write code, "get the spec right first" becomes the key to quality; spec-kit standardizes that flow with official backing and reshaped how many teams collaborate.
