**Why it matters**: It represents an alternative path to "letting agents understand code"—not fuzzy vector matching, but deterministic AST building a precise, explainable graph. 110k stars show that on large codebases, explainable, verifiable structured understanding earns more engineering trust than black-box embeddings.
