## 1. openclaw/openclaw

**是什么**：真正会"动手做事"的 AI。任意操作系统、任意平台。龙虾之道 🦞

**What it is**: The AI that really *does* things. Any OS, any platform. The lobster way. 🦞

**为什么值得关注**：2026 年最现象级的开源项目，星数登顶全年新建仓库榜首。openclaw 把"会执行真实动作的通用 Agent"做成跨平台、以用户数据自主为旗帜的开放系统，催生了围绕它的庞大社区与文化（连不少知名 builder 的简介都挂着 🦞）。它定义了这一年"Agent 从对话走向行动"的集体想象。

**Why it matters**: The most phenomenal open-source project of 2026, topping the year's new-repo chart by stars. openclaw builds a cross-platform, data-ownership-first open system around a general agent that performs real actions, spawning a huge community and culture around it (plenty of well-known builders even wear the 🦞 in their bios). It defined this year's collective imagination of agents moving from conversation to action.

## 3. deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness

## 4. Graphify-Labs/graphify

⭐ 129.2k · Python · 创建于 2026-04-03 · [链接](https://github.com/Graphify-Labs/graphify)

**是什么**：把任意代码库——连同文档、SQL 结构、配置与 PDF——变成可查询的知识图谱。一个面向 Claude Code、Cursor、Codex、Gemini CLI 的 `/graphify` 技能：本地确定性 AST 解析，每条边都有解释，不用向量库。

**What it is**: Turns any codebase—with its docs, SQL schemas, configs and PDFs—into a queryable knowledge graph. A `/graphify` skill for Claude Code, Cursor, Codex and Gemini CLI: local deterministic AST parsing, every edge explained, no vector store.

**为什么值得关注**：在"RAG + 向量检索"几乎成为默认答案的一年里，graphify 走了条更硬核的路：用确定性 AST 解析构建可解释、可追溯的代码知识图谱，不依赖向量相似度。这让 Agent 对大型代码库的理解从"模糊检索"升级为"结构化推理"，是全年最有分量的代码智能基础设施之一。

**Why it matters**: In a year where "RAG plus vector search" became the default answer, graphify takes the harder road: deterministic AST parsing to build an explainable, traceable code knowledge graph without vector similarity. That upgrades an agent's grasp of large codebases from fuzzy retrieval to structured reasoning—one of the year's weightiest pieces of code-intelligence infrastructure.

**Why it matters**: Design collaboration has long been dominated by cloud-subscription tools like Figma; open-design fuses AI agents, local-first and team collaboration to reshape the workflow. It isn't content with "AI makes an image"—it's an open-source workspace spanning the whole creative-production pipeline, an open answer for design tooling in the AI era.

**Why it matters**: From Andrej Karpathy, it explores a minimal viable form of "AI doing AI research"—letting agents autonomously propose, run and evaluate experiments on single-GPU, small-scale nanochat training. It brings "automated science" down from grand narrative to an experiment anyone can run, at once educational and forward-looking—one of the year's most thought-provoking open-source explorations.

## 9. diegosouzapw/OmniRoute

⭐ 78.6k · TypeScript · 创建于 2026-02-13 · [链接](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute)

**是什么**：永不停工。免费的 MIT 协议 AI 网关：一个端点、375 家供应商（150+ 免费）、1400+ 个模型（Kimi、Claude、GPT、Gemini、GLM、DeepSeek、MiniMax）；配额感知自动回退，兼容 Claude Code、Codex、Cursor、OpenCode、Cline 与 Copilot。

**What it is**: Never stop coding. A free, MIT-licensed AI gateway: one endpoint, 375 providers (150+ free), 1,400+ models (Kimi, Claude, GPT, Gemini, GLM, DeepSeek, MiniMax); quota-aware auto-fallback; works with Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode, Cline and Copilot.

**为什么值得关注**：模型越来越多、配额与价格越来越碎，"统一接入 + 自动回退"的网关因此成了开发者刚需。OmniRoute 把上千个模型收进一个端点、在配额耗尽时自动切换，并免费开源，是全年"模型中立、永不断供"理念最彻底的基础设施实现之一。

**Why it matters**: With ever more models and increasingly fragmented quotas and prices, a "unified access plus auto-fallback" gateway became a developer necessity. OmniRoute funnels thousands of models into one endpoint, switches automatically when a quota runs dry, and is free and open source—one of the year's most thorough infrastructure embodiments of the "model-neutral, never-out-of-service" idea.
