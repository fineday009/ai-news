## 1. openclaw/openclaw

⭐ 392.4k · TypeScript · 创建于 2025-11-24 · [链接](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw)

**是什么**：开源 AI 助手，跑在你自己的设备上，通过 Telegram/WhatsApp/Slack/Discord 等你已有的消息应用与你对话，并有 macOS/iOS/Android/Windows/Linux 原生应用。

**What it is**: An open-source AI assistant that runs on your own devices and talks to you through messaging apps you already use (Telegram, WhatsApp, Slack, Discord), with native apps across macOS/iOS/Android/Windows/Linux.

**为什么值得关注**：近 40 万星的年度现象级项目。它最大的差异化是"用户掌控"：状态、记忆、凭据都留在你自己的硬件上，可自由切换模型供应商，没有付费层也没有托管服务，由独立非营利基金会托管。在"AI 要不要把你的数据交出去"成为核心议题的 2026，它给出了最旗帜鲜明的答案。

**Why it matters**: The year's phenomenon at nearly 392k stars. Its key differentiator is user control: state, memory and credentials stay on your own hardware, you can swap model providers freely, there's no paid tier or hosted service, and it's stewarded by an independent nonprofit foundation. In a 2026 where "must AI take your data?" became the central question, it offers the most emphatic answer.

## 5. Graphify-Labs/graphify

⭐ 128.5k · Python · 创建于 2026-04-03 · [链接](https://github.com/Graphify-Labs/graphify)

**是什么**：把任意代码库（连同文档、SQL schema、配置、PDF）变成可查询的知识图谱——本地确定性 AST 解析，每条边都有解释，无需向量库；可配合 Claude Code、Cursor、Codex、Gemini CLI。

**What it is**: Turn any codebase (with its docs, SQL schemas, configs and PDFs) into a queryable knowledge graph—local deterministic AST parsing, every edge explained, no vector store—usable with Claude Code, Cursor, Codex and Gemini CLI.

**为什么值得关注**：当 Agent 要理解大型代码库时，靠向量检索常常"似是而非"。graphify 用确定性 AST 构建可解释的知识图谱，给 Agent 一个精确、可追溯的代码地图。12 万星印证了"让 Agent 真正读懂代码"是全年最硬的需求之一。

**Why it matters**: When agents need to understand a large codebase, vector retrieval is often fuzzy and unreliable. graphify builds an explainable knowledge graph via deterministic AST parsing, giving agents a precise, traceable map of the code. 128k stars confirm that "let agents actually comprehend code" was one of the year's hardest needs.
