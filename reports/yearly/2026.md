## 1. OpenCut-app/OpenCut

⭐ 79.3k · TypeScript · 2026 年高速增长 · [链接](https://github.com/OpenCut-app/OpenCut)

**是什么**：一个面向 Web、桌面与移动端的免费开源视频编辑器，正从头重写，新增 Editor API、插件架构与无头（headless）模式。

**What it is**: A free and open-source video editor for web, desktop, and mobile — being rewritten from the ground up with an Editor API, a plugin architecture, and a headless mode.

**为什么值得关注**：年度 star 最高的项目之一，以开放姿态挑战 CapCut 等商业剪辑软件；可编程与无头模式还让它天然适配"AI 自动剪辑"的下一波工作流。

**Why it matters**: One of the year's most-starred projects, it challenges commercial editors like CapCut with an open stance; its programmable and headless modes also make it a natural fit for the coming wave of AI-driven automated editing.

## 2. koala73/worldmonitor

⭐ 75.4k · TypeScript · 2024 年创建、2026 年爆发 · [链接](https://github.com/koala73/worldmonitor)

**是什么**：一个 AI 驱动的实时全球情报仪表盘，聚合新闻、监测地缘政治与基础设施动态，统一成一块态势感知界面。

**为什么值得关注**：把"一屏看世界"做成了年度现象级产品，戳中信息过载时代对结构化情报的普遍焦虑，全年稳居高热。

**Why it matters**: It turned "the world at a glance" into a phenomenon of the year, tapping the widespread anxiety of information overload with structured intelligence, and stayed hot throughout.

## 3. Panniantong/Agent-Reach

⭐ 61.3k · Python · 2026 年新星 · [链接](https://github.com/Panniantong/Agent-Reach)

**是什么**：一个命令行工具，让 AI 智能体无需各自的 API key 或复杂配置，即可统一访问 Twitter、Reddit、YouTube、GitHub、Bilibili、小红书等整个互联网。

**What it is**: A CLI tool that gives AI agents unified access to the whole internet — Twitter, Reddit, YouTube, GitHub, Bilibili, Xiaohongshu and more — without individual API keys or complex configuration.

**为什么值得关注**：解决了 Agent 接入多平台的最大摩擦——自动化安装、配置与路由，绕开各平台的鉴权与反爬，是"让 Agent 真正能上网"的关键底座。

**Why it matters**: It removes the biggest friction in connecting agents to many platforms — automating install, config, and routing while working around each site's auth and anti-scraping — a key foundation for "agents that can actually use the web."
