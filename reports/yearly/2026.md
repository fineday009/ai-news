**Why it matters**: As agentic coding went mainstream, the "token bill" became a real-money pain point. rtk uses a zero-dependency Rust binary to intercept and compress the output of common commands, cutting most of the redundant tokens. It represents a whole class of pragmatic "cost-reduction" tools that emerged this year — not chasing novelty, just solving the money problem.
